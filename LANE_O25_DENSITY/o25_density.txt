====================================================================================================
O-25   CAN DENSITY FORCE CURVATURE AMONG PAULI WRITERS?
====================================================================================================
  code                    n  records  writer overlap   max ||[X_i,X_j]||   verdict
  [[4,2,2]]               4        2               1           0.000e+00      FLAT
  [[8,3,2]]               8        3               1           0.000e+00      FLAT
  [[6,4,2]]               6        4               3           0.000e+00      FLAT
  toric 2x2 [[8,2,2]]     8        5               2           0.000e+00      FLAT

  every code flat: True

  READ
    Writers overlap on up to several sites and STILL commute exactly. In a stabiliser code the
    logical algebra is symplectic: conjugate pairs anticommute, every other pair commutes, and
    support has nothing to do with it. So DENSITY CANNOT FORCE CURVATURE among Pauli writers,
    and O-25's premise is wrong.

    O-24's curvature came from X_0 * S_1 with S_1 a PHASE gate -- not a Pauli, and not a
    minimal writer. Curvature requires leaving the stabiliser formalism, which is exactly what
    minimality forbids.
