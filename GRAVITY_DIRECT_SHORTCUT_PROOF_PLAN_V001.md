## 1. Closure decision

The program will not first derive a generic gravitational theory and then try
to reconnect it to record formation.  It will carry the exact Phase A
record-derived tensor source through every subsequent limit and prove that
this same physical response becomes gravity.

The load-bearing chain is

\[

 \longrightarrow

 \longrightarrow
 \text{universal soft spin-two coupling}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{consistent self-coupling}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{Einstein gravity}.
\]

The final static calculation fixes the sign and normalization of the
long-range response.  A separate constraint-algebra reconstruction,
thermodynamic derivation, singularity theorem, and black-hole calculation are
not prerequisites for this closure and are not load-bearing lanes in this
plan.

## 2. Target theorem

The intended theorem is:

> The same tensor operator derived from the record-forming F3 parent has a
> nonvanishing, healthy, massless helicity-two infrared continuation.  Its
> owned total momentum and on-shell/1PI soft Ward identity make the
> longitudinal soft sector decouple.
> Locality, factorization, positivity, and the common Lorentz cone then force
> universal coupling to total stress.  Consistent coupling to its own stress
> yields the Einstein--Hilbert interaction at leading derivative order.  Its
> static response is attractive and Newtonian, with coefficient
> `G_eff`.  Therefore the record-derived response is gravity, and record
> formation is its demonstrated microscopic ancestry in the declared domain.

This theorem reaches gravity.  It does not require a parameter-free numerical
prediction of the observed value of `G_eff`; that value is the normalization
of the derived interaction and can be calculated or calibrated separately.

The shortcut is valid only if every gate preserves the identity of the Phase
A object.  Let `P_IR,L(k)` project onto the candidate low-energy pole or
controlled pole cluster.  The required matching has the form

\[

 =Z_Q(L,a_*,k)h_L^{TT}(k)+\mathcal R_L(k),
 \tag{AI1}

where:

under the same coarse-graining map.  A field with the right representation

This invariant is checked at every gate, not appended as a separate final
mechanism.  If all gates pass, the former microscopic-to-RGRL composition is
the transitive conclusion of the proof rather than an additional discovery

**Shortcut consequence:** the former B2--B4 construction is no longer on the
