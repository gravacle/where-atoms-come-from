## 0. WHEN IT RUNS

**On ROUTE DECISIONS only.** A route decision is one whose answer determines **what gets built or
investigated next**. A decision that records a fact is not one. Four in this program to date:
**R1** the founding question (*record inside the carrier, or adjoined outside?*) ·
**R2** the disposition (*STOP, or STOP-FALLS-REBUILD?*) ·
**R3** W-11 (*finding, or restatement of the convention?*) ·
**R4** W-12/W-14 (*can a constraint act on the connection, or on the state?*).

Running it on every question would make it ceremony. **Ceremony is how the isolation ledger died.**

## 1. THE FIVE TESTS

Each must produce **a number or a citation** into the frame lane's sealed output. A route may not be
taken on a frame missing any of the five. This is the pointer rule (custody §1) applied to framing.

### F1 — **EMBED.** *What space are the named options points in?*
Find the parameter space containing both arms. **If one exists, the binary is a SAMPLE of a space
and not a partition of it**, and the route decision must be re-posed over the space.
*Output: the space, its dimension, and the coordinates of each named arm.*
**This is the most productive of the five** — the two best artifacts in this corpus (W-10's
four-valued scope table, W-14's `pi`/`u`/`v` relevance test) are both F1 results.

### F2 — **DEGENERACY.** *Are the arms the same object under a map?*
Compute `||A - f(B)||` over the natural candidate maps `f` — power, restriction, change of
coordinates, monomial substitution. **Report the number AS A FRAME RESULT.**
*Failure mode this exists to catch, observed:* W-11 leg A computed `||T^L - M_gamma|| = 4.64e-15`
and reported it under the heading *"COR-F's transport is a legitimate rival convention"*. **The
degeneracy number was present and read backwards.** F2 forbids that by requiring the number be
reported against the frame.

### F3 — **CARVING.** *Does the predicate partition anything?*
Measure both cells. **An empty cell, a measure-zero cell, or an overlap means the predicate does not
partition** and the dichotomy is a distinction without a difference.
*Output: the measure of each cell under a stated measure.*

### F4 — **PRESUPPOSITION.** *Write the sentence the question assumes, and test it as a claim.*
Every choice presupposes something. State it in one sentence and test it independently of the
choice.
*Failure mode this exists to catch, observed:* R1 presupposes a system/environment **factorization**,
and W-04's located residue is precisely that **in a gauge theory the physical algebra does not
factorize.** The founding presupposition was false for the deepest reason available.

### F5 — **THE NULL OPTION.** *Does either branch obtain at all?*
Test whether the object the question is about exists. **"Neither" must be an admissible answer with
its own evidence**, not a residue of failing to establish the others.

## 2. HOW IT COMPOSES WITH W-16's GATES

W-16 gate 1 runs a **frame lane first, alone, with no candidate answers in its brief**. This page is
that lane's checklist. Gate 2 gives every lane a `frame_objection` field **separate from its
verdict**, so objecting no longer costs a lane its answer. Gate 3 makes an objection **block**
synthesis. Gate 4 is a `grep` at registration: **no row may close a route over a frame objection it
does not name and address.**

## 3. WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT DOES NOT FIX

**The registrar writes the frame lane's brief.** Keeping candidate answers out of it is as far out
of the registrar's hands as this goes without a different model, and the lineage-independent lane
W-03 specified still does not exist for anything after W-06.
**F1..F5 is itself a coined enumeration of five.** Per W-15's own discriminator that makes it a
place to look for a sixth. **It is not claimed exhaustive.**
