## 1. THE TARGET

**What relationship, if any, holds between gravitational structure, electromagnetic structure,
and alpha, in the process by which quantum cells form and records come into being?**

Stated so it can fail. Its predecessor's target — *derive alpha* — could only be unfinished,
and stayed unfinished for months.

## 2. THE FOUNDING REFRAME

**Alpha is an INPUT.** A known feature the process must accommodate, alongside electromagnetism.
We do not compute it; we require any candidate process to have a place where a dimensionless
coupling of its kind lives.

Its first consequence is the reason to proceed: the predecessor's partition theorem established
the magnitude as **allow-side** — permitted, forced by nothing — and read that as its wall. On
this footing it is the **prediction**. A process that leaves the coupling's slot open is exactly
what a process should look like when the coupling comes from outside.

Its second consequence is that the predecessor's three fences and its frozen release condition
have no subject here. They guarded a computation this project does not perform. **Custody §5
replaces them with a distinction, not a gate: alpha may constrain structure; alpha may not
select structure.**

## 3. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS ACTUALLY BUILDING — THE AMENDMENT THAT MATTERS

The predecessor established, and its audit confirmed, that **the process gap is a missing
CROSSING, not a missing generator.**

The formation gate takes a **cell-level** input — *"one physical Lorentz-covariant interval,
support region, and closure configuration over which its action is accumulated"* — and yields a
**record-level** output — *"the compatible outgoing sector of a future-directed, causally
sequential exhaustion."* **And it does not deliver it:** *"global orthogonality is not yet a
durable public record."*

The connecting object has a name — **the durability map** — appears in an obligation list, and
has **no form** in any of 5,512 archive files.

**So the object to be built is the durability map, and this project exists to build it.**

## 4. THE PROVED OBSTRUCTION, PROMOTED

The predecessor proved that **finiteness alone does not produce durability**: *"the one-cell
operator therefore provides a reversible write, not a durable record"*; *"a finite discrete
spectrum is recurrent"*; *"merely increasing the finite cell count or calling orthogonality
durability does not pass."*

This is **not a check to run at the end. It is the obstruction the construction must answer at
the start.** A finite spectrum recurs; a record must not.

**And the corpus already contains one escape, built, pointed elsewhere.** Its single built
cell-to-record crossing is an **inductive limit** of cell-indexed finite record algebras into a
quasi-local algebra. An inductive limit of finite objects is not finite — which is precisely
how it escapes recurrence. **It has never been pointed at formation.** That is this project's
first serious lead and it is inherited as a template, not as a result.

## 5. THE REQUIREMENTS, ALREADY WRITTEN

Nine properties any crossing must produce are enumerated and quoted in the predecessor's
register at Q-1179: durability and irreversibility jointly · persistence under later cells ·
thresholded non-return · recoverability · redundancy · asymptotic centrality · sector-hood ·
inductive compatibility · orthogonal reduced supports.

**This is the specification. It does not have to be rediscovered.** The ninth is recorded
`false` of record and is the sharpest single target.

## 6. HONEST SCOPE — WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES NOT PROMISE

The predecessor's three standing demands lie at **neither** the cell nor the record level —
zero of six endpoints at either. **Solving formation would not discharge them.** There are at
least two independent level problems, and this project addresses one.

Anyone expecting the crossing to deliver the predecessor's magnitude demands will be
disappointed, and the design says so here rather than discovering it later.

## 7. THE STAGES

**S1 — BUILD ONE CARRIER, CONCRETELY.** A specific finite oriented complex: named cells,
incidence, orientation, one root; a one-dimensional Hermitian fiber at each vertex; a discrete
unitary connection on edges. Holonomies computed around every face.
*Falsifier:* none — if it cannot be written down the project stops at once, and that is a result.

**S2 — THE FORMATION CONDITION ON THAT CARRIER'S OWN DATA.** The predecessor's gate is written
on a source / designated-record / environment split introduced by an imperative "Let", which
**the complex itself does not contain.** State the condition without it, or declare the split
under custody §5 and proceed with it declared.
*Falsifier:* if it cannot be stated without a stipulated split, say so plainly.

**S3 — BUILD THE CROSSING.** Against the nine requirements of §5, answering the recurrence
obstruction of §4, with the inductive-limit template as the starting point.
*Falsifier:* no construction satisfies the nine, or none escapes recurrence. **This is the
project's central question and its central way to fail.**

**S4 — RUN IT, AND VARY THE ELECTROMAGNETIC STRUCTURE.** Change holonomies, winding, charge.
Does formation change, and how?
*Falsifier:* formation is wholly insensitive to the connection — a real and publishable negative.

**S5 — LOOK FOR THE SLOT.** Does the running construction carry a **free dimensionless
coupling**? Where does it enter, what does it govern?
*Falsifier:* every dimensionless quantity is fixed. Then it cannot accommodate alpha as an input
and it is the wrong process.

**S6 — ONLY THEN, FORCING.**
