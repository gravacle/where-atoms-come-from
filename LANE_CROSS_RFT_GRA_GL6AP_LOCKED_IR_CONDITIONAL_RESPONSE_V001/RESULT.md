# Result

Sealed GL6AN does not force an infrared `E` pole.

The exact link constraint and the exact local pair filter live in inequivalent
`S4` representations.  At the trivial character,

\[
 \ker B(1)=T_2,\qquad \dim T_2=3,
\]

whereas strict locked pair fluctuations occupy

\[
 E\subset A_1\oplus E\oplus T_2,\qquad \dim E=2.
\]

The two-dimensional kernel at a generic character is the transverse plane
inside the moving `T2`; it is not a local `E` irrep.  Exact character algebra
gives `Hom_S4(T2,E)=0`.

The native loop motion also supplies no `E` conservation law.  On an explicit
locked `Q4` background, one alternating hexagon changes its six-vertex
opposite-pair type counts from `(1,2,3)` to `(3,2,1)`.  Combined with GL6AN's
nonzero `-63/8 h^6/U_d^5` matrix element, this gives a nonzero commutator of
the uniform pair-`E` count with the effective dynamics.  The kernel of the
commutator map on `E` is `S4` invariant, so irreducibility upgrades this one
witness to the absence of every nonzero conserved uniform `E` combination
at sixth perturbative order.  This is a statement about the bare count in the
formal series, not an uncomputed all-orders effective Hamiltonian.

For a chosen translation/`S4`-invariant state, the most general reciprocal
analytic quadratic inverse `E` response is

\[
 \Gamma_E^R=
 [a_0^R(\omega)+c_0^R(\omega)I_2(\theta)]I_2
 +c_2^R(\omega){\cal T}(Q_E(\theta))+O(|\theta|^4).
\]

An `E` mass is allowed, and an `E` cubic invariant is also allowed.  Therefore:

- positive mass permits a gapped response;
- zero mass plus positive analytic stiffness and finite inertial weight gives
  a conditional linearly soft character dispersion;
- damping or a continuum can remove an isolated pole even at zero mass;
- discrete `E` ordering has no symmetry-mandated Goldstone mode.

A pole sequence requires positive atomic spectral weight approaching zero
energy, not merely a vanishing threshold.  A robust infrared pole of
nonvanishing visibility further requires a residue bounded away from zero.
The single-mode upper bound uses only strictly positive-frequency weight, so
an elastic zero-frequency atom cannot spuriously lower its quotient.
The character becomes physical momentum only after a calibrated physical
embedding, refinement limit, state selection, and stable response scaling
are supplied.

**Ceiling:** no gauge phase, photon, graviton, physical momentum, common cone,
gravity, or `G` is established.
