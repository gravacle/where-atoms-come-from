====================================================================================================
O-24   DOES MINIMALITY PICK THE CONNECTION?  WHAT DOES CURVATURE COST?
====================================================================================================
  carrier: 3 qubits, H = 0, records Z_1 Z_2 Z_3, dim 8

  ENUMERATING ADMISSIBLE WRITERS BY WEIGHT
  writer for R_0                      weight  admissible
  X_0  (the minimal writer)                1        True
  X_0 * S_0   (phase on site 0)            1        True
  X_0 * S_1   (phase on site 1)            2        True
  X_0 * S_2   (phase on site 2)            2        True
  X_0 * S_1 * S_2                          3        True

  HOLONOMY OF EACH CHOICE, against the minimal writers for R_1 and R_2
  writer for R_0                      weight  ||[U0,W1]||  loop ||H-I||   verdict
  X_0  (the minimal writer)                1    0.000e+00     0.000e+00      FLAT
  X_0 * S_0   (phase on site 0)            1    0.000e+00     0.000e+00      FLAT
  X_0 * S_1   (phase on site 1)            2    4.000e+00     4.000e+00    CURVED
  X_0 * S_2   (phase on site 2)            2    0.000e+00     0.000e+00      FLAT
  X_0 * S_1 * S_2                          3    4.000e+00     4.000e+00    CURVED

  minimum weight of a FLAT   writer for R_0 : 1
  minimum weight of a CURVED writer for R_0 : 2
  -> CURVATURE COSTS 1 EXTRA SITE(S) OF WEIGHT

  READ: the cheapest operation that flips a record is FLAT. Curvature requires reaching
  a site the minimal writer does not touch. If the physics uses the cheapest writer, the
  flat connection is SELECTED rather than assumed -- and curvature has a price.
