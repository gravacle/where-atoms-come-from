--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE REASON, IN ONE LINE, AND THE TEST THAT WOULD BREAK IT
  The admissible writer F found by the exhaustive Pauli search in step 1 is the global flip.
  F commutes with H, so every eigenspace is F-invariant and the diagonal algebra splits into
  F-EVEN and F-ODD parts -- a Z_2 grading with nothing in between.
     F-ODD  observables are carried to minus themselves by the writer, so Tr(P_E O) = 0 on
            every eigenspace: they satisfy clause (iv), they are records -- and they are
            non-constant on eigenspaces, so they cannot label an energy.
     F-EVEN observables are constant on each eigenspace, so they CAN label an energy -- and
            being constant there they FAIL clause (iii) and are not records.
  CLAUSE (iv) IS ITSELF THE SEPARATOR.  Being writable by an admissible operation is exactly
  what forces an observable's energy content to zero.  Not a feature of the chain: a feature
  of demanding a writer that commutes with H.

  READ: the maximum over the ENTIRE record sector is 0.000000 bits in every row -- reading ANY

  THAT IS A STATEMENT ABOUT ONE RECORD AT A TIME AND MUST NOT BE STRETCHED.  Two records read
  TOGETHER determine their product, and the product is a carrier.  The next table measures
  exactly how much the joint reading buys, so that the single-record zero above cannot be
  mistaken for a claim about the collection.

  READ: the k=1 column is 0.00000 in EVERY row -- one record never buys anything, on any family.
  On the generic family (superinc, no accidental degeneracy) the joint reading buys EXACTLY k-1
  bits, one per extra record after the first.  The degenerate families buy LESS than k-1, never
  more, because there the energy does not determine the bond pattern -- so k-1 is an upper
  bound, and the direction of the conclusion is the same in every row.
  The first record buys nothing; every record after it buys at most one bit, and that bit is
  the CORRELATION with what is already read.  So the
  energy content of k records is carried entirely by the k-1 correlations among them and not at
  all by the records.  Since no admissible operation changes any of those correlations (step 1,
  exhaustive over 4^n Paulis, zero flippers found), the energetic content of this construction
  is exactly the part that CANNOT be written.
