--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Q1.  DOES THE RECORD-PAIR CORRELATION FALL OFF WITH SEPARATION?
  <Z_i Z_j> is evaluated exactly on three ensembles: the GROUND eigenspace, a single generic
  eigenspace, and the uniform ensemble over all 2^n configurations.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  family        n ensemble                     d=1       d=2       d=4       d=8      d=16
  uniform      33 GROUND eigenspace             -1         1         1         1         1
  uniform      33 a generic eigenspace          -1         1        -1         1         1
  uniform      33 uniform over all 2^n           0         0         0         0         0

  randpos      33 GROUND eigenspace             -1         1         1         1         1
  randpos      33 a generic eigenspace          -1         1        -1         1         1
  randpos      33 uniform over all 2^n           0         0         0         0         0

  randsign     33 GROUND eigenspace              1         1         1         1         1
  randsign     33 a generic eigenspace          -1         1        -1         1         1
  randsign     33 uniform over all 2^n           0         0         0         0         0

  READ: on ANY single eigenspace |<Z_iZ_j>| = 1 at EVERY separation -- perfectly FLAT, no decay
  of any kind, neither power law nor exponential.  Averaged over the whole configuration space
  it is exactly 0 at every separation -- also flat.  Neither is a falloff.  A 1D chain at fixed
  energy is long-range ordered, so there is no length scale in the correlation to measure.
  Clause (e) asks for separation DEPENDENCE; this quantity has none at all.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Q2.  DOES THE ENERGY COST OF CHANGING THE CORRELATION DEPEND ON THE SEPARATION?
  cost(d) = min over odd subsets of the d-bond window of the exact integer energy change.
  Evaluated from the GROUND configuration (every bond satisfied, so every single flip costs).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  family        n    cost d=1    cost d=2    cost d=4    cost d=8   cost d=16   cost d=32    d-dependent?
  uniform      33           2           2           2           2           2           2      NO -- FLAT
  uniform      65           2           2           2           2           2           2      NO -- FLAT

  randpos      33         110          50          50           6           6           6             YES
  randpos      65         110          50          50           6           6           6             YES

  randsign     33          98           6           6           6           6           6             YES
  randsign     65          98           6           6           6           6           6             YES

  READ THE 'uniform' ROWS FIRST.  With every J_k equal the cost is the SAME NUMBER at every
  separation -- exactly flat, no d-dependence whatsoever.  That is the control that decides
  the meaning of the disordered rows: any d-dependence seen there comes from the SPREAD OF THE
  COUPLINGS, which was INSERTED into the venue, and not from separation as such.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  From the ground configuration cost(d) = 2 * min_{k<d} |J_k|, a MINIMUM OVER d SAMPLES of the

  that minimum is a closed form; it is computed below and compared to the measured mean.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      2                         42.0600                   41.005556   1.0257    82.0111                    2

  READ: E[cost(d)] -> 2 as d grows (the floor of the inserted distribution), and cost*d is NOT

  distribution that was PUT IN BY HAND, and it saturates at the smallest coupling present.
  It carries no information about the records: min_{k<d} |J_k| is a function of H alone --
  the same record-blindness found in step 3, now in the separation channel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Q3.  THE HONESTY CONTROL.  Insert a long-range coupling J_ij = round(A / |i-j|^p) and measure
  the two-body energy at separation d.  If the measured exponent comes back equal to p, the
  measurement was of the INPUT.  This is run so that no later reading of clause (e) on this
  construction can mistake an inserted exponent for an induced one.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   inserted p   measured exponent (log-log, exact ints)   |recovered - inserted|
          1.0                                  1.000000                 3.33e-16
          1.5                                  1.500000                 3.80e-08
          2.0                                  2.000000                 1.48e-07
          3.0                                  3.000005                 5.47e-06

  READ: the recovered exponent reproduces the inserted exponent to within 5.5e-06 in every
  row -- the residual is integer rounding of A/d^p, nothing else.  This control is DELIBERATELY
  trivial: that is the point.  A power law in this construction is exactly as strong as the
  power law written into H, and no stronger.
  NOTHING in the nearest-neighbour chain INDUCES a power law: its energy is contact-only, its
  correlations are flat, and the only separation dependence anywhere in the lane came from the
  extreme-value statistics of couplings that were inserted by hand and saturates.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  family        n        d=1        d=2        d=3        d=4        d=8       d=16    CONTROL: sum over all d
  uniform      33          1          0          0          0          0          0                         32
  randpos      33         55          0          0          0          0          0                       1106

  READ: the two-body energy is exactly zero at every separation beyond 1, while the same table's
  control column shows the total is large -- the instrument is reading a real H.  The energy of

  spread(Phi).  Clause (e) is not merely failed by an exponential here; there is no falloff to
  measure, because there is nothing at range at all.
