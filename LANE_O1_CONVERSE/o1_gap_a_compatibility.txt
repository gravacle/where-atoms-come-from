EXPERIMENT 4 -- THE TRAP.  Jump operators that VANISH on every eigenspace of H
                and still leave no record anywhere in the system.
================================================================================================
  H  = diag(0,0,1,1)
  L1 = |e2><e0| + |e1><e2|     L2 = |e3><e1| + |e0><e3|

  TRAP     max_k max_lam ||P_lam L_k P_lam|| = 0.000e+00   (the noise is INVISIBLE inside every eigenspace)
           dim(P A P) on E_0 = 4 of m^2 = 4   -> IRREDUCIBLE
           dim of the Hermitian commutant of the whole *-algebra = 1
           record on E_0: NO    record on E_1: NO

  CONTROL  max_k max_lam ||P_lam L_k P_lam|| = 0.000e+00   (the noise is INVISIBLE inside every eigenspace)
           dim(P A P) on E_0 = 2 of m^2 = 4   -> reducible
           dim of the Hermitian commutant of the whole *-algebra = 2
           record on E_0: YES    record on E_1: YES
  control record                         (i) 0.00e+00/0.00e+00  (ii) 0.00e+00  (iii) 1.4142  Tr R = +0.0000

  CONTROL differs from TRAP only by deleting the RETURN leg of each jump.
  READING: 'the jump operators act trivially inside the degenerate subspace' is
  NOT sufficient for clause (ii).  Second-order excursions out of the eigenspace
  and back enter A and make the compression irreducible.  Any attempt to prove
  the converse by inspecting P L_k P alone is answered by this counterexample.

EXPERIMENT 5 -- HOW LARGE IS THE COMPATIBLE SET?  An exact codimension.
================================================================================================
  For a FIXED record R with eigenvalue multiplicities (p,q), p+q = d, the set of
  jump operators satisfying [L,R] = 0 is a LINEAR SUBSPACE of C^{dxd} of complex
  dimension p^2 + q^2, i.e. of complex codimension 2pq.  Measured against theory:

    d   p   q    dim{L:[L,R]=0}   p^2+q^2   codim  ok
    4   2   2                 8         8       8  PASS
    4   1   3                10        10       6  PASS
    6   3   3                18        18      18  PASS
    6   2   4                20        20      16  PASS
    8   4   4                32        32      32  PASS
    8   3   5                34        34      30  PASS

  codimension law verified: PASS
  The compatible set has measure ZERO in the space of jump operators, for every
  d >= 2 and every splitting.  Degeneracy of H places no constraint on L at all.

EXPERIMENT 6 -- THE FAILURE OF CLAUSE (ii) IS FIRST ORDER IN THE PERTURBATION.
================================================================================================
  L(eps) = diag(0.3,0.3,-1.2,2.0) + eps * (generic complex matrix);
  R = diag(1,-1,1,-1) is an exact record at eps = 0.

         eps    ||[L(eps),R]||    defect/eps
       1e-08      4.945604e-08        4.9456
       1e-06      4.945604e-06        4.9456
       1e-04      4.945604e-04        4.9456
       1e-02      4.945604e-02        4.9456
       1e-01      4.945604e-01        4.9456

  Slope 1 across six decades: clause (ii) is violated at FIRST order.
  Now the exact question -- does ANY record survive a finite perturbation?

       eps  trials   records found
     1e-04     200               0
     1e-03     200               0
     1e-02     200               0
     1e-01     200               0

  STATED LIMITATION: below eps ~ 1e-5 the surviving commutant directions are
  distinguished from float noise only by second-order singular values (eps^2 vs
  1e-16), so this sweep is reported only where it is unambiguous.  The exact
  statement is the codimension law of Experiment 5, which needs no tolerance.

  READING: the converse cannot be rescued by a 'small enough noise' hypothesis.
  Clause (ii) as written is exact and its violation is linear.  That is O-5
  (exactness) seen from inside O-1.
