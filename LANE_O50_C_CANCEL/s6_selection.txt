  MEASURED, on the torus, exactly:
    1. Every H-diagonal state -- every temperature, every energy shell -- gives record
       magnetisation EXACTLY 0.  (T-1, and the control fires when clause (iv) is broken.)
    2. Every environment satisfying clause (ii) leaves the record magnetisation EXACTLY

    3. Every carrier scalar measured takes the SAME value at every configuration, and the
       space of writer-invariant functionals is exactly one-dimensional.  (T-3, step 2.)
    4. The clauses make whatever ordering is present PERMANENT.  (T-4.)

  ==> NOTHING IN THE FIVE CLAUSES, AND NOTHING IN ANY CARRIER PROPERTY MEASURED HERE, SELECTS AN
      ORDERED CONFIGURATION.  Stronger: clauses (iii) and (iv) are, between them, PRECISELY the
      statement that no such selection exists -- clause (iii) says the record is not fixed by the
      energy, clause (iv) says its two values are equally represented in every energy shell.  A
      carrier that selected a configuration would, by that very fact, hold no record at all.

  INTERPRETATION (labelled as interpretation, not as a measurement):
    If accumulation requires ordering, and ordering cannot come from the clauses, the carrier,
    the Hamiltonian, or any admissible environment, then it can only come from the STATE and its
    HISTORY -- from the process that formed the records, which fixes the initial condition the
    clauses then preserve forever.  On this reading the five clauses are the wrong place to look
    for a source: they describe what a record IS and guarantee that a record's value SURVIVES,
    and they are deliberately silent about which value it has.  The question 'where do atoms
    come from' would then be a question about FORMATION, not about the clause structure -- which
    is what this program set out to find.

    This is an interpretation of four exact results, not a fifth result.  It could be wrong in
    one specific way: if some functional of the record configuration that this lane did not
    consider were both writer-invariant AND non-constant, T-3 would fail.  Step 2 measured that
    space to be one-dimensional on the torus, so on THIS carrier that escape is closed exactly;
    on a carrier where the writers do NOT act simply transitively -- CTRL-1, the chain -- it is
    NOT closed, and step 2 shows the invariant space there is 2^{m-1}-dimensional.
