================================================================================================================
READ OF V3  (filled in from the numbers above)
================================================================================================================
 * At g = 0 every cross-region J_2 is exactly 0 -- the lane's proof and its number are
   reproduced here independently.  The proof is correct WITHIN ITS HYPOTHESIS.
 * At g != 0 the cross-region J_2 is non-zero, on records in disjoint regions with their
   own bath sites, and it FALLS OFF WITH SEPARATION with a fitted decay length.
 * Therefore 'J_2 between records in different regions is IDENTICALLY ZERO at every N' is a
   property of a bath whose Hamiltonian has no site-site term.  It is not a property of
   records, and it does not survive the smallest physical generalisation of the mediator.
 * The lane's stated ground for refusing to call the shared-site coupling a field -- 'no
   range, no falloff to extrapolate' -- is removed the moment the mediator is allowed to
   propagate.  A range appears.
