# LANE X6 — IS THE RECORD-LEVEL CONSTRAINT THE SAME CONSTRAINT AS GRAVITY'S?

**Opened 2026-08-18.** Replaces the withdrawn allow-to-require test (X2 erratum, `5c01e47`).

## THE QUESTION
Gravity is **ALLOW** at every level — a constraint on admissible data, never a production rule.
So is *our* record-level constraint **the same constraint as gravity's, seen at another scale**, or
**two unrelated statements that merely happen both to be permissive?**

## THE FALSIFIER — WRITTEN BEFORE THE RESULT, AND IT MAY FIRE
> **Different generators, or physical content that is NOT a boundary term on one side, kills the
> identification.**

## CHOICE LEDGER
| choice | why | alternative rejected |
|---|---|---|
| compare **constraint ALGEBRAS**, not solutions | two theories can share solutions by coincidence; the algebra is what makes a constraint *that* constraint | comparing the constraint equations termwise — dimensionally meaningless |
| Z2 on a torus, exact diagonalisation | the carrier every prior lane used; results must be commensurable with G1/G2/W-62 | a new carrier — would break comparability |
| test BOTH 3+1 (ADM) and 2+1 gravity | 2+1 gravity is a different animal; a verdict that ignores it is incomplete | 3+1 only |

## TOY_SEPARATION
The lattice is a **finite exact model of the algebra**, not a stand-in for spacetime. Every claim
must be about **algebraic structure** (generators, closure, structure constants vs functions,
boundary behaviour), which is scale-free and survives the finiteness. Any claim that depends on the
lattice being small, or on a particular numeric value of a coupling, is OUT OF SCOPE for this lane.

## THE RESCUE THAT IS FORBIDDEN HERE
"The generators differ, but we never expected record-level gravity to look classical." That is
**level migration** — rescuing an import by relocating it. If the falsifier fires, X-6 fails.
The standing expectation that record-level gravity looks unlike classical gravity is a licence to
find a **different shape**, NOT a licence to keep an identification whose own stated test failed.
