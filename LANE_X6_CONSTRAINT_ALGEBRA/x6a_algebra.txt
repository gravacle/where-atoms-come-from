5.  STRUCTURE CONSTANTS vs STRUCTURE FUNCTIONS  —  THE DECISIVE TEST
============================================================================================
   THE QUESTION: G_v G_w expanded in the generator basis,
       G_v G_w  =  prod_u G_u^{c_u(v,w)}   with c_u in F2,
   are the c_u the SAME for every state, or do they depend on the field configuration?
   (A structure-FUNCTION algebra -- e.g. the ADM hypersurface-deformation algebra, whose
    bracket of two Hamiltonian constraints returns the momentum constraint with a
    coefficient built from the inverse spatial metric -- has coefficients that change
    from field configuration to field configuration. That is what we are looking for.)

   METHOD (state-resolved, not assertion): for each ordered pair (v,w) and each sampled
   state |psi>, compute y = G_v G_w |psi> by applying the two operators in sequence, then
   test EVERY one of the 2^NV generator words g against y. The set of words matching on
   THAT state is read off from the state itself; the coefficients c_u are then whatever
   the state says they are. We compare those read-off coefficients across states.
