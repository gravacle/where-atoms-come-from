== F2  DOES 'M_gamma IS FIBRE-WISE, HENCE IN THE LOCAL GAUGE GROUP' MAKE IT TRIVIAL? ==
  W-06 corrected N4's mechanism to FIBRE-WISE-NESS: any fibre-wise unitary lies in U(1)^V.
  True of M_gamma.  But lying in the gauge GROUP is not being a gauge TRANSFORMATION of the
  system: a gauge transformation moves (a, s) -> (a^g, g.s); the corpus moves s -> M s with
  a HELD FIXED.  (a, Ms) and (a, s) are gauge-equivalent iff some g stabilises a and equals
  M on supp(s).  The stabiliser of a is the CONSTANT phases; M is not constant.  Test:
  joint invariant  conj(s_v0) U_e4 s_v3 :
     on (a, s)      = -0.148342116+0.089228915j
     on (a, M_dF s) = 0.164318888+0.054465454j     <- CHANGED: not a gauge copy
     on (a, g.s), g a constant phase = -0.148342116+0.089228915j   <- unchanged, as gauge must be
  || (a,Ms) invariant - (a,s) invariant || = 0.314588
  -> the corpus's evolution carries the configuration OFF its gauge orbit.  Fibre-wise-ness
     makes M_gamma an ELEMENT of the gauge group; it does not make the dynamics vacuous.
     (This is the Aharonov-Bohm content: a relative holonomy phase between two branches.)
