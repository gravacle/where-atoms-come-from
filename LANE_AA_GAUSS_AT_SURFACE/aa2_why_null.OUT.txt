== WHY AA1 WAS A ZERO-VARIABLE CONTROL: THE CONSTRAINTS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT ==
   || prod over ALL vertices of G_v  -  I ||  =  0.000e+00
   Every link touches exactly TWO vertices, so each Z appears twice in the product and
   squares to the identity. ON A CLOSED GRAPH THE GAUSS CONSTRAINTS SATISFY ONE RELATION.
   So dropping ONE of them removes NOTHING -- it is implied by the other five.
   'Impose Gauss at the cut vertex' was never an assumption I made. IT IS FORCED.
