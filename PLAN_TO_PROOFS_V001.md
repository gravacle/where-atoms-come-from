## P1 — DEFINE "RECORD" ONCE

**To prove.** A single definition from which every later statement follows. The program has used at
least three: *an observable with `[H,R]=0`*; *a protected loop*; *a sector label*.

**Proposed.** `R` is a **record** for `(H, {L_k})` if
  (i) `R = R†`, `R² = I` (a bit), (ii) `[H,R] = 0` and `[L_k,R] = 0` for all `k`,
  (iii) `R` is **not** a function of `H`, and (iv) some admissible operation flips it.

**Why (iii) is needed and must be justified, not assumed.** A function of `H` is conserved and does
label states — but its value is fixed by the energy, which is fixed at preparation. **It carries no
bit that could have been otherwise.** That argument has to be made precise or (iii) is arbitrary.

**Falsifier.** If a natural physical record fails (iii), the definition is wrong.

## P2 — PROVE: A RECORD EXISTS ⟺ `H` IS DEGENERATE

**To prove.** If every eigenvalue of `H` is simple then the commutant of `H` is the abelian algebra
generated by `H`, so every `R` with `[H,R]=0` is a function of `H` and fails P1(iii).
**Conversely**, if `H` has a degenerate eigenvalue the commutant is non-abelian and admits an `R`
satisfying (i)–(iii).

**Strategy.** Forward direction is a standard spectral argument and is short. The converse needs
care: it must produce an `R` that is also **compatible with `{L_k}`** (P1 ii) and **flippable**
(P1 iv). Degeneracy alone gives (i)–(iii); (ii) and (iv) are extra.
**This is where the proof will be harder than the measurement suggests.**

**Already have.** `dim commutant = Σ (multiplicity)²`, exact in every case tested.

## P3 — PROVE THE FRAGILITY DICHOTOMY

**Half proved.** Theorem D: topological degeneracy splits at order `d`, the minimal non-contractible
cycle. **Verified: slope `2.000` across two decades, derived `d = 2`.**

**To prove — the other half.** A degeneracy protected by a symmetry `G` is split at **first order**
by a generic `G`-breaking perturbation.

**Strategy.** Degenerate perturbation theory: the first-order splitting is given by the eigenvalues
of `P V P` on the degenerate space. For a symmetry-protected multiplet, `P V P` is a generic
Hermitian matrix on that multiplet once `V` breaks `G`, so it has distinct eigenvalues generically.
The content is in "generically" — it needs a genericity statement, not hand-waving.

**Already have.** Measured `2.0e-06` at `ε = 1e-06`, i.e. linear.

## P4 — THE WEAKEST LINK: ARE THOSE THE ONLY SOURCES OF DEGENERACY?

**To prove, or to state honestly as unproved.** P5 concludes *records require topology*. That is only
valid if symmetry-protected and topological exhaust the possibilities (accidental degeneracy being
non-generic and, by P3's argument, fragile).

**This may not be provable.** Classifying sources of spectral degeneracy is a real open question, and
"topological" is not a sharply bounded category — it includes symmetry-protected topological phases,
which blur the dichotomy P3 relies on.

**Therefore the honest target is a weaker, provable claim:**

> **Any degeneracy split at first order by a generic local perturbation cannot support a record that
> survives noise. Topological degeneracy is not so split. Therefore records that survive noise
> require a degeneracy with `d > 1`.**

**That avoids the classification problem entirely** — it says nothing about *what kinds* of
degeneracy exist, only about which can carry a record. **This is the version to aim for.**

## P5 — THE COMPOSITE THEOREM

**Statement to be assembled once P1–P4 hold:**

> **Let `R` be a record for `(H,{L_k})` in the sense of P1 that survives generic local perturbation.
> Then `H` has a degenerate eigenvalue (P2) whose splitting order under local perturbation exceeds 1
> (P3, P4). On a lattice gauge theory this forces the degeneracy to be topological, and Theorems A–D
> then determine the record space (`2^{2g}`), the writer (Poincaré duality), the protection (order
> `d`), and the effect of an electric coupling (splitting at order `d`).**
