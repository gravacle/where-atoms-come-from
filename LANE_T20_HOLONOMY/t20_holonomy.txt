3.  WHY, AND WHAT WOULD BE NEEDED FOR CURVATURE
     Our records are commuting logical operators of an ABELIAN stabiliser code, and their
     writers are Pauli operators that either commute or anticommute -- so a loop closes up to
     a SIGN, and a sign is not curvature: it is the same element of an abelian group.
     Curvature needs transport whose composition depends on ORDER in a way no relabelling
     removes -- a NON-ABELIAN connection. That is exactly the class O-8 flagged as untested:
     Levin-Wen and Fibonacci, where the writers are Wilson loops of a non-abelian theory.
