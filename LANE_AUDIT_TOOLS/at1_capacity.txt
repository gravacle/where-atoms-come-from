  THE CEILING IS THE SITE'S INFORMATION CAPACITY. Crowded chi rises 0.133478 -> 0.267223 ->
  0.336940 as the site grows from 1 to 3 qubits, and the SUM over both records is bounded by
  the site's s bits in every row. C-36's 'an environment site holds a bounded amount and
  records sharing it split it' is TRUE and it is the HOLEVO BOUND -- conventional physics,
  correctly applied, and NOT a discovery of this program.

  THE TOOL MADE AN ORDINARY EFFECT LOOK NOVEL. Every measurement behind C-36 used a site of
  ONE qubit, where capacity and density are indistinguishable. Varying the site dimension --
  one line of the experiment, never run -- separates them immediately.

  WHAT SURVIVES, AND IT IS THE MORE INTERESTING RESULT. Pure capacity splitting would depend
  only on HOW MANY records share the site. O-33a measured a difference of 0.33 in the crowding
  cost depending on whether the partner PAIRS with the record -- at fixed number of records and
  fixed site. Capacity alone cannot produce that, so C-39 is NOT explained by this and stands.
