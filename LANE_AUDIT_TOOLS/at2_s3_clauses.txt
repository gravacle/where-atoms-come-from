  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  THE GROUND SPACE IS DIMENSION 8, matching the 8 gauge classes counted group-theoretically in
  O-29. That identification cross-checks.

  BUT NO RECORD EXISTS ON THIS CARRIER. H = -(A+B) on the minimal torus has eigenspaces of
  dimension 8, 13 and 15. Clauses (i)+(ii) force Tr(P_E R) = dim(E) mod 2, so on a 13- or
  15-dimensional eigenspace it can never vanish, and CLAUSE (iv) -- WRITABILITY -- IS
  UNSATISFIABLE. The control has multiplicities that are all even and yields 1260 records.

  SO O-29 AND O-30 MEASURED TRANSPORT BETWEEN OBJECTS THAT ARE NOT RECORDS BY THIS PROGRAM'S
  DEFINITION ON THAT CARRIER. The transport itself is real group theory and the numbers stand,
  but the word 'record' was imported, not earned. Those rows must be rescoped.

  IT IS A CARRIER DEFECT, NOT A VERDICT ON NON-ABELIAN CARRIERS. The minimal torus has ONE
  vertex and ONE face, so the gauge and flatness projectors have nowhere to act -- H-4's
  small-carrier warning again. A larger S_3 complex is the next thing to build, and whether its
  multiplicities are even is a question about that carrier, not about S_3.
