X2  IS chi AN OBSERVABLE OF THE THEORY, OR A PARAMETER OF THE CONSTRUCTION?

                 complex   V   E   F  chi  Hilbert dim  degeneracy
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
               torus 2x2   4   8   4    0           32           4
               torus 2x3   6  12   6    0          128           4
   cube surface (sphere)   8  12   6    2           32           1

  CHECK 1 -- do different chi give different Hilbert spaces?
                 torus 2x2 (chi=0, dim=32)  vs  torus 2x3              (chi=0, dim=128)   same dimension? False
                 torus 2x2 (chi=0, dim=32)  vs  cube surface (sphere)  (chi=2, dim=32)   same dimension? True
                 torus 2x3 (chi=0, dim=128)  vs  cube surface (sphere)  (chi=2, dim=32)   same dimension? False

  CHECK 2 -- is the record count a function of chi alone?
                 torus 2x2: chi= 0  size (E= 8)  degeneracy=4  ->  2^(2-chi) = 4
                 torus 2x3: chi= 0  size (E=12)  degeneracy=4  ->  2^(2-chi) = 4
     cube surface (sphere): chi= 2  size (E=12)  degeneracy=1  ->  2^(2-chi) = 1
    torus 2x2 and torus 2x3 differ in SIZE but share chi -- and share the degeneracy.

  CHECK 3 -- could chi be measured from inside, at fixed Hilbert dimension?
    chi is not built from the edge variables: it is V - E + F, a count of the CELLS the space
    was assembled from. No operator on the edge Hilbert space has it as an eigenvalue.
    The DEGENERACY reveals it -- but the degeneracy is a property of H, which is itself
    written down from the same cell data. Nothing INSIDE the theory varies chi.

  CONCLUSION
    chi has no eigenvalues, no expectation value, and no equation of motion. It is a
    PARAMETER OF THE CONSTRUCTION. No operation -- unitary, dissipative or otherwise --
    can change it, because every operation is a map on a space BUILT FROM a fixed chi.
    X2 IS THEREFORE NOT A MISSING MEASUREMENT. It is a structural feature of any framework
    in which the complex is fixed, and closing it requires chi to become an OBSERVABLE --
    i.e. a state that is a superposition over topologies.
