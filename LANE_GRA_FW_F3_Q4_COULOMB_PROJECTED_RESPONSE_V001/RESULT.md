# Result

pieces

\[
 Q_{\rm FV-WITNESS}=Q_{\rm pair}^{(0)}
 +Q_{{\rm ring,irr}}^{(6)}
\]

were composed with the exact FO 180-state leading-`H6` sector without adding
a source-off term.  This witness tensor restricts, for one homogeneous query
on this component, to rank five modulo identity.  Its source-to-commutator
rank is three, while the unique-ground-state retarded and first nonzero
commutator-moment ranks are two.

The rank loss is explicit.  The homogeneous `A1` source is

\[
 Q_A=(60\rho\mathbf1+11H)/\sqrt3,
\]

one `E` direction is `16 sqrt(6) rho` times the identity on the selected
component, and one `T2` direction commutes with `H`.  The three remaining
nonconserved operators include one state-specific dark combination.  The
ground response therefore has exactly two orthogonal rank-one residues, at

\[
 \Delta_1=2+2\sqrt2,\qquad \Delta_2=4+2\sqrt2,
\]

and no other `FV-WITNESS` finite-sector spectral support.  This is the first
exact FV rank-witness-to-dynamical-response composition.  It is a finite-
sector, homogeneous-source result and does not contradict FV's family-wise
off-shell rank-six theorem.

FV's generated `Q_diag^(2,4,6)` derivatives and folds are not included and
can change the complete fixed-order response.  Rank two is therefore not an
upper bound on the full FV or CTP packet.  This result is not a Ward identity,
thermodynamic tensor-pole, RGRL-B, gravity, or `G` theorem.
