ATTACK A.  The finding's ONE claimed strictly-collective fact is
           'wall = 2 on the family at every k, where the control needs weight k'.
           wall = w(all-ones pattern) is NOT basis invariant.  Under a record-basis change
           M in GL(k,2) the pattern v transforms as v -> M v, so the all-ones pattern in the
           new basis is the OLD pattern M^-1 (1,..,1), which ranges over EVERY non-zero v.
           Therefore wall over all admissible record bases ranges over [wmin, wmax] on BOTH
           carriers.  Below: the exact weight map's wmin/wmax, and wall measured directly
           over 400 random GL(k,2) record bases.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
