  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  THE PAIRING AND THE DENSITY ARE TWO SEPARATE MECHANISMS, AND THEY ARE NOT INDEPENDENT.

  DISTURBANCE is the pairing, and it is SITE-BLIND. A commuting partner written on another site
  leaves chi at 1.000000 of its alone value -- exactly, at every coupling. A pairing partner on
  another site suppresses it regardless of where it sits. This is C-34's topological pairing
  doing what a topological quantity does: it says WHO interacts, and knows nothing about where.

  CROWDING is the density, and it is SITE-BOUND -- and it is SELECTIVE. Sharing a site with a
  COMMUTING partner costs far more than sharing it with a PAIRING partner, by about 0.3 in
  ratio, stable across a 3x range of coupling. Two records that already interact through the
  pairing cost each other LESS when they share an environment site than two that do not.

  SO THE TRIAD'S PRODUCT IS REAL IN THE SENSE THAT MATTERS: the pairing does not merely sit
  beside the magnitude, IT MODULATES IT. <c_i,c_j> selects who interacts; the density says how
  much a shared site costs; and the first CHANGES the second. That is the join, measured on an
  abelian carrier where no frame rotation exists.

  WHAT IT IS STILL NOT. Nothing here transports anything. The join is between a SOURCE and a
  MAGNITUDE, not yet between either and the path-dependent transport of C-30 -- which needs the
  non-abelian carrier. That remains the destination.
