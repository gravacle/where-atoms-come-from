An eigenspace of dimension m can hold at most log2(m) independent record bits, because k
independent bits demand 2^k joint eigenspaces inside the level.

READ, AND IT IS THE UNFLATTERING READ: every eigenspace of the fully coupled chain has
dimension EXACTLY 2 at every n. All n of the Z_i satisfy clauses (i)-(iv), but inside any
level they are the SAME BIT up to sign -- the chain holds ONE independent record bit however
long it is. The program's own commuting_family returns a family of size 1, independently of n.
The energy spread is extensive; the number of independent records is NOT.

WHY: with distinct couplings, the map s -> (s_i s_{i+1})_i is exactly 2-to-1 onto the bond
strings, so each energy level is precisely a pair {s, -s}. The bond correlations LABEL the
levels; the records live in the two-fold degeneracy inside a level and nowhere else.

READ: the number of levels is exactly 2^(n-1) and every level has dimension 2. The pair
correlations FAIL clause (iii) -- they are constant on every eigenspace. So they are
conserved charges, not records; and no admissible unitary can change any of them, since an
admissible U preserves each level and the correlation is a scalar there.
THAT IS AN EXACT ARGUMENT, and it is stronger than a search: it is not that no cheap writer
of the correlation was found, it is that NONE EXISTS.

READ: cutting the chain into r blocks gives every level dimension 2^r and a bit ceiling of r.
The program's own machinery confirms r independent, INDEPENDENTLY WRITABLE records wherever
the dense object fits. Clauses (iii) and (iv) hold for every Z_i on this carrier too --
clause (iv)'s trace is exact integer zero for all n and all i.
So the number of independent records IS extensive in this venue, at r = n/w for fixed block
size w, and S3 already showed the energy spread is EXACTLY additive over exactly these blocks.

THE PRICE, STATED PLAINLY: in venue 2 the blocks are DECOUPLED, so the extensive spread is
a sum of independent block contributions with no interaction between records in different
blocks. Extensive and additive, yes; interacting, no. That is the same contact-or-nothing
boundary C-47 recorded, met again from the other side.
