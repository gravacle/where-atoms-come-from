READ FROM THE NUMBERS: on the open chain the spread takes exactly ONE value under coupling
permutation, reproducing the lane. On the ALL-PAIRS carrier -- the very variant the finding
says is covered -- the spread takes MANY values under the SAME permutation test, and the
claimed closed form S = 2*sum|J_ij| is FALSE there.

WHY, stated so it can be checked: on the open chain the map s -> (s_i s_{i+1}) is ONTO all
2^(n-1) bond strings, so each bond term can be extremised independently and S = 2*sum|J_i|.
Off the tree that map is CONSTRAINED (t_{ij} t_{jk} = t_{ik}), the extremum is a max-cut
problem, and it depends on WHICH pair carries WHICH coupling.
