READ, FROM THE NUMBERS AND NOT FROM THE EXPECTATION: venue 2 splits into two cases.
  BLOCK SIZE w >= 3, distinct couplings:  |Aut| = 1. Fully rigid. Geometry present.
  BLOCK SIZE w = 2, distinct couplings:   |Aut| = 2^r, NOT 1 -- a two-site block is a single
      bond and swapping its two endpoints preserves it. Those 2^r symmetries act INSIDE
      blocks and never exchange one block with another, so they do not make the r blocks
      interchangeable; but the claim '|Aut| = 1' is FALSE at w = 2 and is not made.
  CONTROL, identical blocks: |Aut| = 2^r . r!, matching the last column exactly -- the block-
      permutation factor r! appears precisely when the blocks are made interchangeable, so
      the counter is live and would have caught a permutation-symmetric venue.
CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS LANE: every venue-2 measurement reported here uses w >= 2 with distinct
couplings, and the quantities measured (spread, variance) were already shown in S4 to be
symmetric functions of the coupling multiset, so no separation claim rests on w = 2 rigidity.

D-22 STATUS: venue 1 has |Aut| = 1 for n = 3..8 by brute force over all n! permutations, and
by the distinctness + non-palindrome obstruction for n = 9..16. Venue 2 has |Aut| = 1 at
block size w >= 3 and |Aut| = 2^r at w = 2, from block-internal endpoint swaps that never
exchange blocks; the identical-block control returns 2^r . r! and so is live. Neither venue
is permutation-symmetric in the sense D-22 forbids. The config-INDEPENDENT observables are
nevertheless blind to placement, which is a fact about the OBSERVABLE, not the carrier --
and it is the reason no separation law can be extracted from them.
