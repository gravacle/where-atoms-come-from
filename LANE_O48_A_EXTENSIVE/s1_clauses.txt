ADMISSIBLE WRITER: SEARCHED, NOT NOMINATED (D-18).

independent Pauli words, [P,H] = 0 forces [P, Z_iZ_{i+1}] = 0 for every bond.  P writes R_i
iff {P, Z_i} = 0.  The search below imposes NO ansatz: it enumerates the group and reports
what satisfies both conditions.
