T4  THE PLACEMENT TEST -- the limitation, reported as one.  Hold n AND the coupling MULTISET
    fixed and only PERMUTE which bond carries which coupling. The carrier genuinely changes
    (S1 established |Aut| = 1, so these are different Hamiltonians).
    LIVE CONTROL in the same table: E(s) at a FIXED configuration s, which must move if the
    permutation is doing anything at all.
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  n  permutations  distinct S  distinct Var  distinct E(s*) [CONTROL]   CONTROL range of E(s*)/D
  6           400           1             1                        10                   1.046024
  8           400           1             1                        35                   1.614719
 10           400           1             1                       120                   2.216626
 12           400           1             1                       269                   2.499008
READ: under permutation of the couplings among the bonds, the SPREAD and the VARIANCE take
      exactly ONE value -- they are SYMMETRIC FUNCTIONS of the coupling multiset and are
      blind to where anything sits. The control column moves freely, so the permutations are
      real. The configuration-INDEPENDENT part therefore cannot carry a separation law of any
      kind: it is strictly more than a count of records, and strictly less than geometry.
      The configuration-DEPENDENT part is the only one of the two that sees placement.
