LANE W-10 B REFUTE 1 — MATHEMATICS LENS — PUBLISHED CONVENTIONS
Everything needed to re-run this lane is on this page or in rlib.py.  Nothing here
imports the lane under attack; b_lib.py is read, never called.

LINEAGE.  Claude Opus 5, like W-07..W-09 and like LANE_W10_B_MULTISET itself.  This is
NOT a lineage-independent check of that lane.  It is one refuter on one lens.

CLASS ORDER (identical to the lane's, so arrays are comparable at the digit):
    0 = class 00 (character 1) · 1 = class 10 (u = conj(W_F)) · 2 = class 01 (v = W_C)
    3 = class 11 (uv).   P(x,y) = p0 + p1 x + p2 y + p3 xy.   lambda = m(P).
    Z_k = P(u^k, v^k) with u = e^{-i f k}, v = e^{i c k} — the lane's own convention.

WHY THIS LANE EXISTS.  Every number in the lane's LEG B — the complex-coefficient
experiment its whole attribution finding rests on — is produced by ONE reduction,
m(P) = (1/2pi) INT log max(|p0+p1 x|, |p2+p3 x|) dt.  Its E1, E2 and E5 are that reduction
(in y, in x, and in float64); its exact E3 covers only real non-negative DOMINATED arrays;
its ergodic E4 was run in LEG A only, i.e. on real arrays.  The complex half is therefore
single-evaluator.  The first job here is to re-run it with evaluators that do not know
what a Jensen pairing is.

EVALUATORS (rlib.py), all written here:
  R1 m_grid      the double integral itself, midpoint tensor grid, Richardson in 1/n^2.
                 No Jensen, no branch analysis, no splitting.  float64.
  R2 m_erg       the corpus's OWN direct method (S4 sec4.2 schedule-B), f=1.0, c=sqrt(2),
                 run on COMPLEX arrays — which the lane never did.  float64.
  R3 m_split     mpmath dps 30, a DIFFERENT decomposition from the lane's:
                 m = (1/2)[log max(a0,a1)+log max(a2,a3)] + (1/8pi) INT |log(SA/SB)| dt,
                 whose smooth half is exact Jensen per branch.  Shares the reduction with
                 the lane, not the code.
  R4 m_finite    average over a finite orbit; used at S1's order-4 connection, where the
                 whole rate is ALSO given in closed form and evaluated in Fractions.
  R5 m_poly_roots  one-variable Mahler measure from numpy.roots; used at S3/S4's exactly
                 resonant connection.
Cross-checks published on the pages: R1 vs R2 vs R3 vs exact log(4/9), log(1/2), log(1/4),
log(2/5); R1/R2/R3 against every block value the lane prints at its B.3.

PRECISION.  R1 and R2 are float64 and are never used for a claim below 1e-6.  R3 is dps 30.
C.1's order-4 orbit structure and C.3(b)'s domination certificate are EXACT in
fractions.Fraction.  Where a sweep decides at 1e-4, every near-tie is re-decided at dps 30
and the count of re-decisions is printed.

SEEDS.  numpy default_rng: 90210 (R2), 5150 (R4 D.2).  R1 and R3 use no randomness.

GRIDS.  R1 Richardson pairs (1024,2048) and (512,1024) as labelled; R3 adaptive.
D.1 runs 1024/2048/4096 explicitly to decide whether a spread is quadrature.

ISOLATION.  Every section names its one variable in its own header and prints the arrays.
The two places where an arm of MINE could not have failed are flagged on the page:
  (i) A.1's B4 row is dominated, so its 24-fold agreement is forced — it is reported as a
      COUNTEREXAMPLE to the lane's biconditional, which is what a forced value is good for,
      and never as a confirmation of the multiset theorem.
  (ii) B.4 is a measure, not a comparison: nothing is held fixed and it is labelled so.

WHAT I DID NOT RE-RUN.  LEG C's carrier construction (B0b/B4 incidence) and LEG C.3's
edge-transport arm: they are not on this lens.  B-05's pushforward identity is checked
algebraically on the page (it is one line) and not re-implemented.
