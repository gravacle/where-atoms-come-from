# Result: degree-pair geometric-strain boundary

The reduced periodic F3/q4 ice parent does **not** presently derive the two
missing `E` directions of the frozen block-strain source.  Its complete FS
source remains exact rank four, `A1+T2`, because the unsplit degree square has
only the scalar weight `W_v=sum_a D_a`, while every FQ17a pair expansion has
only additive weights `D_a+D_b`.

The missing operators are nevertheless already present.  Expanding

\[
 U_d(d_v-2)^2=U_dI+{U_d\over2}\sum_{a<b}Z_aZ_b
\]

shows the six link-pair observables, and ice restriction leaves their two
centered `E` variations.  FK's inherited H6 ring law acts nontrivially on both
block-local `E` directions.  This makes `E` queryable and dynamical; it does
not make it the derivative of physical strain.

The obstruction is exact underdetermination.  The same source-off Hamiltonian
admits both:

- the current FS/FQ17a source, with rank four; and
- a root-pair external source using
  `(n_b-n_a)(n_b-n_a)^T`, with exact rank six.

Both can be applied before Feshbach reduction and both satisfy `H[j=0]=H`.
Therefore source-off dynamics plus observable availability cannot determine
the source derivative.

One compact sufficient physical premise is `DPAR`: the existing degree-pair
energy has one common, prospectively fixed affine-response law

\[
 {U_d\over2}Z_aZ_b\mapsto {U_d\over2}
 g\!\left({|F(n_b-n_a)|^2\over|n_b-n_a|^2}\right)Z_aZ_b,
 \quad g(1)=1,\quad g'(1)\ne0,
\]

with its derivative independently derived, explicitly adopted, or calibrated.
`DPAR` is neither inherited nor adopted in this lane, and the theorem does not
prove it is the unique or necessary closure.  Conditional on it, the root
weights follow by differentiation rather than by fitting, (FT08) replaces
rather than supplements the FS degree deformation, the unchanged one-edge
source is retained, and the full microscopic source has exact rank six with
the same source-off Hamiltonian.  The direct ice-projected pair image itself
has only `A1+E` rank three.  The inherited H6 dynamics supplies nonzero
block-local `E` commutator matrix elements on explicitly constructed
compatible `G_5` ice states.  Full state-dependent CTP rank, a uniform
response, Ward structure, a tensor pole, gravity, and `G` remain open.
