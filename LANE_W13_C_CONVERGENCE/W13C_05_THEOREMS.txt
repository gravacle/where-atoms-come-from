        limsup_N A_N  =  I(eps) - liminf_N E_N(eps)
        liminf_N A_N  =  I(eps) - limsup_N E_N(eps)

  in eps exist and

  >>  liminf_N A_N  =  m(P)  -  lim_{eps->0} limsup_N E_N(eps)
  >>  limsup_N A_N  =  m(P)  -  lim_{eps->0} liminf_N E_N(eps)

    the opposite of the truth.  Replaced by the real Jacobian of P (|det| = 3.531057).
