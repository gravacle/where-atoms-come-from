INSERTED vs INDUCED
-------------------
INDUCED (the venue's own):  everything in Sections 1, 3, 4, 5 -- the main sweep, the
  winding-pair constant, the enclosing sweep, the KP topological constant.  Pure Gamma:
  nothing added to the stabiliser list, no parameter anywhere.
INSERTED, and labelled so in the OUT file where they appear:
  - Section 2 corridor: a REGION deformation (geometry grown, venue untouched).
  - Section 6 range row: ONE extra Z-row added to the rank instrument's generator list;
    NOT a stabiliser code, NOT a Gamma venue -- an instrument-sensitivity control only.
    Its g-dependent shell is the INSERTED row's law; reporting it as Gamma's would be the
    fatal error named in the brief, and the OUT file says so at the point of use.
No mediator law is measured in this lane; the mediator belongs to the coupling tier under
the openness clause and is only PREFIGURED by control (v).

CHOICE LEDGER (every choice that could have gone another way)
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pair-union convention: edge union (disjoint qubit sets) PRIMARY; induced-union variant
   cross-checked at one contact row (equal there, both printed).
2. Blocks row-aligned at fixed offset: justified by D-22 symmetry and checked numerically
   (G10 offset invariance).
3. Corridor width 2: width 1 has no v-edges and cannot even form a straddling seam; width
   2 is the thinnest corridor that can.  Its contact seam is still rank-absorbed -- that
   is a finding, not a tuning.
4. Inserted row anchored in A's row 1 starting at column 1: makes the predicted reach
   threshold exactly ell = g+3; the prediction is checked as a gate, not assumed.
5. Sweep ceilings: L = 12 (n = 288 qubits, largest exact object; F_2 bitmask width 288),
   s = 2..4, all g = 0..L-2s (both seams of the torus included).
6. KP partition by edge midpoints into three pairwise-contacting sectors: any partition of
   the disk's edges works for the KP combination; constancy across q and offsets is gated.
