========================================================================================================
O-44   DOES A PHYSICAL WRITER EXIST WHERE AN ADMISSIBLE ONE DOES NOT?
========================================================================================================
  Writers are SEARCHED over the full Pauli group, never nominated.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  carrier n = 4, dim 16
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  UNMODIFIED   H = -(X^n + Z^n)          [CONTROL]
     ||[H,R]|| = 0.000e+00   record is DURABLE
     max_E |Tr(P_E R)| = 0.000e+00   clause (iv) under DEF-A: HOLDS
     ADMISSIBLE ([W,H]=0)     writer FOUND, weight 2, ||[W,H]|| = 0.000e+00, energy cost +0.000000
     PHYSICAL (any unitary)   writer FOUND, weight 1, ||[W,H]|| = 8.000e+00, energy cost +2.000000

  CONFIGURATION ENERGY   H = H_stab - sum c_i R_i
     ||[H,R]|| = 0.000e+00   record is DURABLE
     max_E |Tr(P_E R)| = 2.000e+00   clause (iv) under DEF-A: FAILS
     ADMISSIBLE ([W,H]=0)     writer: NONE EXISTS in the Pauli group
     PHYSICAL (any unitary)   writer FOUND, weight 1, ||[W,H]|| = 8.352e+00, energy cost +2.600000


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  carrier n = 6, dim 64
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  UNMODIFIED   H = -(X^n + Z^n)          [CONTROL]
     ||[H,R]|| = 0.000e+00   record is DURABLE
     max_E |Tr(P_E R)| = 4.441e-16   clause (iv) under DEF-A: HOLDS
     ADMISSIBLE ([W,H]=0)     writer FOUND, weight 2, ||[W,H]|| = 0.000e+00, energy cost +0.000000
     PHYSICAL (any unitary)   writer FOUND, weight 1, ||[W,H]|| = 1.600e+01, energy cost +2.000000

  CONFIGURATION ENERGY   H = H_stab - sum c_i R_i
     ||[H,R]|| = 0.000e+00   record is DURABLE
     max_E |Tr(P_E R)| = 3.000e+00   clause (iv) under DEF-A: FAILS
     ADMISSIBLE ([W,H]=0)     writer: NONE EXISTS in the Pauli group
     PHYSICAL (any unitary)   writer FOUND, weight 1, ||[W,H]|| = 1.670e+01, energy cost +2.600000

========================================================================================================
  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  WHERE CLAUSE (iv) HOLDS, A FREE WRITER EXISTS AND COSTS EXACTLY ZERO. The search finds a
  weight-2 admissible writer with ||[W,H]|| = 0.000e+00 and energy cost +0.000000. That is the
  control, and it is the carrier this program has always used.

  WHERE THE CARRIER ASSIGNS A CONFIGURATION ENERGY, NO FREE WRITER EXISTS -- the search returns
  NONE over the entire Pauli group -- BUT A PHYSICAL WRITER DOES, at weight 1, and it costs
  +2.600000 of work. The record remains a bit, remains durable, remains non-trivial, and remains
  FLIPPABLE BY AN OPERATOR THE SEARCH ACTUALLY FOUND.

  SO WHAT FAILS IS NOT WRITABILITY. WHAT FAILS IS FREE WRITABILITY.

  O-42's EXCLUSION IS THEREFORE CONDITIONAL ON DEF-A, NOT A FACT ABOUT RECORDS. DEF-A is marked
  PROVISIONAL in the framework and its evidence line reads 'one carrier family, and DEF-A has NO
  working fallback'; the alternative reading, 'or a physical channel', is marked UNTESTED. A
  unitary commuting with H does no work on the state's energy, so DEF-A amounts to requiring
  that writing be FREE -- and a free write cannot distinguish energies. Landauer puts the floor
  on a real write at kT ln 2, not at zero.

  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT DO. It REMOVES A BLOCKER: records can carry a configuration energy
  provided writing is allowed to cost work, which is what writing costs in the world. It does
  NOT show that gravity emerges. It reopens the question that O-42 appeared to close, and it
  reopens C-47 -- whose extensive quantity was registered as bought at the cost of independent
  writability, a cost that under the physical reading is simply WORK DONE.
