# Standard-causal finite-mission coverage theorem

**Claim class:** exact compositional universal-scope theorem

**Not claimed:** `REC -> C & S & J`, full OPCC, empirical authentication,
coverage of every non-CTS architecture, A5, reset, objective outcome
actualization, a sealing trigger, or a complete dynamical-gravity theory

## 1. Sealed dependencies

This lane uses three results without modifying them.

1. The sealed record-to-CTS re-entry theorem proves

   \[
    \operatorname{REC}(r)\land C(r)\land S(r)\land J(r)
    \Longrightarrow \operatorname{CTS}(r).             \tag{1}
   \]

   Its proof derives T5: once every source occurrence is before the cut, local
   standard sewing holds, and the joint separator contains every crossing path
   and port, no future module can retain a separate
   \(\operatorname{src}(x)\) argument.

2. The sealed Cauchy/time-slice normal-form theorem proves

   \[
    \operatorname{CTS}(r)
    \Longrightarrow
    \exists\mathcal C_r\,
    \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,\mathcal C_r).          \tag{2}
   \]

3. The sealed faithful-transcription theorem proves

   \[
   \begin{split}
    \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,\mathcal C_r)
    \Longrightarrow \exists\Xi\,[
       &\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)\\
       &\land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}(\Xi)]
    \Longrightarrow \bigwedge_{i=1}^{4}A_i(\Xi).       \tag{3}
   \end{split}
   \]

The conclusion of (3) includes the existing Coverage-U factorization as an
objective physical statement. It does not include C5 authentication.

## 2. Independently defined physical domain

For an independently specified finite-mission candidate, define

\[
 \operatorname{SCREC}_{\rm FM}(r)
 :=\operatorname{REC}(r)\land C(r)\land S(r)\land J(r), \tag{4}
\]

where the clauses have the following physical meanings.

**C -- chronological complete-ancestor cut.** The complete registered ancestor
process lies in a prospectively bounded stably causal mission domain and admits
one branch-independent event-before-query macro-cut. In a spacetime theory the
relevant domain is globally hyperbolic; in a finite operational theory the
actual external incidence frontier is acyclic.

**S -- standard exact sewing.** The actual theory on both sides of the cut has
a well-posed initial-value law and at least one existing K, W, A, or I
composability branch. It consists of finitely many prospectively bounded exact
macro-modules with normalized kernels, CP maps/instruments, UCP or normal-UCP
maps, or deterministic completely CP-preserving higher-order maps as
appropriate. A continuum propagator may be one exact module; no discretization
is inferred.

**J -- compatible complete joint separator.** One actual apparatus-inclusive
law, state, algebraic state, or higher-order strategy on the cut contains every
crossing carrier, memory, controller, reference, center/edge variable, external
port, constraint datum, outcome history, and joint correlation.

All four conjuncts in (4) are facts of the physical episode. Their referents and
content are fixed independently of scored contrast, a selected latent model,
or success of (1)--(3). They are ontic rather than evidential: (4) does not say
that a surviving C5 certificate exists.

Let

\[
 \mathfrak R^{\rm bf,SC}_{\rm FM}
 :=\{r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM}:
          \operatorname{SCREC}_{\rm FM}(r)\}.           \tag{5}
\]

## 3. Theorem SCU1 -- exact standard-causal URFT scope

For every independently specified candidate,

\[
\boxed{
 \operatorname{SCREC}_{\rm FM}(r)
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists\mathcal C_r,\Xi\,\left[
 \begin{aligned}
  &\operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,\mathcal C_r)\\
  &\land\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)\\
  &\land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}(\Xi)\\
  &\land\bigwedge_{i=1}^{4}A_i(\Xi)
 \end{aligned}\right].}                                \tag{6}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf,SC}_{\rm FM}\;
 \exists\mathcal C_r,\Xi\;[
  \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}
  \land\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}
  \land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}
  \land A1\land A2\land A3\land A4].}                 \tag{7}
\]

All predicates in (7) carry the same arguments as in (6).

### Proof

Assume (4). Equation (1) gives \(\operatorname{CTS}(r)\). This step uses the
record predicate to supply T0 and the real event-bearing lineage, C to supply
T1, S to supply T2--T3, and J to supply T4. Source-before-cut chronology, local
sewing, separator completeness, and complete registered histories then force
the common source-independent future object in T5.

Apply (2) to construct the actual ontic complete causal cover
\(\mathcal C_r\). Apply (3) to that same cover to obtain a faithful physical
encoding \(\Xi\) in at least one existing K, W, A, or I architecture. The
faithful-transcription theorem supplies A1--A4 and exact Coverage-U
factorization. Conjoining the two existential witnesses gives (6). Universal
generalization over the independently defined set (5) gives (7). QED.

## 4. What is genuinely added by the composition

Equation (6) is not obtained by defining `SCREC` as “has an OCC” or “admits an
encoding.” C, S, and J are physical chronology, dynamics, and joint-boundary
facts. In particular, they do not assume A1--A4 or the successful factorization.
The chain adds two consequences not named in (4):

1. T5 is forced from complete causal custody rather than assumed as a separate
   future-channel premise; and
2. the physical episode can be transcribed into the existing four-architecture
   union with exact referent, law, boundary, and source provenance.

The result is nevertheless compositional, not new microscopic physics. S and J
deliberately carry most of the standard composability and separator content
needed by CTS. The theorem's advance is an exact domain-level coverage statement
and a sharp residual universal obligation.

## 5. Remaining universal obstruction

Full actual-world coverage would require either

\[
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM},\quad
 \operatorname{REC}(r)\Longrightarrow C(r)\land S(r)\land J(r), \tag{8}
\]

or a separately proved coverage route for records outside that subclass.
Nothing in (1)--(7) proves (8). Standard classical stochastic systems, ordinary
finite-dimensional operational quantum systems, suitable fixed-background
AQFT/gauge systems, and bounded internally indefinite-order systems can enter
the theorem when their exact physical episodes satisfy C, S, and J; branch
names alone do not establish those facts.

The implication is lifecycle-neutral inside its antecedent. A maintained/LIVE,
distributed, copied, migrating, relational, or destructive-read candidate is
covered when it independently satisfies REC and its complete episode satisfies
C, S, and J. Naming one of those lifecycle types does not itself prove the
three physical gates.

## 6. CTC and non-CTS boundary

The sealed compact Deutsch-style distributional CTC witness satisfies REC but
has physical support in both directions between its named writer and query.
It therefore violates C and shows

\[
 \operatorname{REC}\not\Longrightarrow
 \operatorname{SCREC}_{\rm FM}.                        \tag{9}
\]

Equation (9) blocks derivation of (6) from REC alone on the present domain. It
does not prove that no broader architecture, generalized-causal encoding, or
future non-CTS notion of cover could represent that record. This lane proves
sufficiency of the standard-causal route, not maximality of the route.

## 7. Authentication and strong-URFT ceiling

OCC is ontic. Without C5, (6) does not yield `IACC`, `AuthEnc`, any authenticated
`*_ADMIT` packet, or A5. It also says nothing about when unitary evolution ends,
which outcome actualizes, or what objective trigger seals a quantum record.
Those are strong outcome-as-record obligations and remain separate.
