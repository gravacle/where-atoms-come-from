# Boundaries and non-implications

## 1. REC alone does not supply the chronological cut

The sealed record-to-CTS lane gives a compact finite-state record in an
explicitly Deutsch-style distributional CTC theory. A real blank-to-bit write
and stable carrier produce positive complete-history contrast, while a
controller interface connects the named query region back to the named writer
region. No branch-independent writer-before-query macro-cut exists.

Thus REC alone cannot enter SCU1. This is a model-theoretic boundary, not a
claim that physical CTCs occur in our universe.

## 2. A nominated carrier is not J

A source may influence a query through a genuine nominated carrier and through
an additional controller, reference, broadcast, center variable, or boundary
port. The carrier can satisfy the record predicate while a separator containing
only that carrier is incomplete. Enlarging the separator may recover J, so this
example rejects carrier-only completeness rather than existential J.

## 3. Endpoint positivity is not S

A normalized finite response table does not identify the actual propagating
state, prove CP under arbitrary ancillary extension, establish a common
C*-observable algebra, or provide a deterministic completely CP-preserving
higher-order module. S is an independently physical dynamics premise. FM1's
score-table simulator cannot be substituted for it.

The sealed transpose boundary illustrates the distinction but is not an OPCC
counterexample: a basis-only transpose record has a faithful classical diagonal
K recoding. Tomography distinguishes transpose from identity but does not by
itself eliminate every contextual classical cover.

## 4. Ontic coverage is not surviving certification

An episode can satisfy C, S, and J as physical facts even when no independent
complete certificate of them survives. SCU1 then yields OCC and PhysEnc/ARCH,
not IACC, AuthEnc, or an authenticated admission packet. Unique inaccessible
natural records prevent a universal all-ontic certification claim.

## 5. The CTC witness does not close every architecture

The CTC record lies outside the standard-causal subclass and outside the CTS
route. That fact does not establish a theorem about every conceivable
generalized-causal or non-CTS cover. SCU1 makes no maximality claim and does not
turn failure of its antecedent into `NO_RECORD` or `NO_COVER`.
