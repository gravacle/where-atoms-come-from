MAHLER.  Jensen reduction in x, grouping P = (p00 + p01 y) + x (p10 + p11 y):
  m(P) = (1/2pi) INT_0^{2pi} log max( |p00 + p01 e^{it}| , |p10 + p11 e^{it}| ) dt.
  For REAL coefficients both branches depend on t only through cos t, so the integrand is even
  and m(P) = (1/pi) INT_0^{pi} ... dt.  The two branches cross at most twice in [0,2pi); the
  crossing is the single root of a linear equation in cos t.  This lane splits at that root and
  applies 400-node Gauss-Legendre on each smooth arc.  When one branch dominates on all of
  [0,pi], the integral is EXACT by Jensen a second time: m(P) = log max(|p00|,|p01|) or
  log max(|p10|,|p11|).  Both four-class carriers are in that exact case (proved in w10d_2).

HULL TEST (W-01's criterion).  0 lies in conv{unit-modulus characters} is decided TWICE by two
independent algorithms and the two are required to agree exactly:
  (H1) sorted-angle max-gap <= pi + 1e-12
  (H2) brute force over all 2- and 3-element subsets: 0 in a segment through the origin, or 0
       inside/on a triangle by the three barycentric sign tests.
W-01's criterion is read as the EXISTENCE statement it is (SOME ready state on that carrier
fires).  FIRE => HULL is unconditional; HULL => FIRE is false pointwise for a FIXED p and this
lane exhibits that on a four-class carrier's own published weights.

THE ONE VARIABLE.  Every comparison in this lane holds the evaluator, the grid, the seed, the
k-range and the code path fixed.  The ONLY thing that moves between arms is WHICH CLASSES THE
CARRIER'S INCIDENCE OCCUPIES (and, where stated once and labelled, the FIELD of the coefficients:
non-negative real -> real -> complex).  Every script PRINTS A DIFF OF ITS ARMS before reporting,
because the corpus's commonest fatal defect is a control whose two arms are byte-identical.
