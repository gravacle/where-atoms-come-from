EXACT PHASE ARITHMETIC (this refuter's own, independent of lane C's).
  For a rational connection alpha = A/M, beta = B/M the orbit phase at step k is (k A mod M)/M.
  Computed by CHUNKED modular accumulation:  within a chunk of length n the phases are
  (base + j*A) mod M for j = 1..n, and base <- (base + n*A) mod M.  The largest int64 product
  is n*A, so with n <= 2^20 and M < 2^42 there is no overflow AT ANY K.  Lane C instead forms
  k*A directly, which is why its own K = 1e7 sits at 88% of the int64 ceiling (R2_01 sec 3).
  For an irrational connection the phase is carried in mpmath at 60 dps and only the final
  fractional part is demoted to float64.
