  READ -- from the numbers above

  THE CONTROLS SEPARATE TWO EXPLANATIONS THAT S_3 ALONE CONFOUNDS.
  S_3 is non-abelian AND has order 6, which is not a power of two. Z_3 is ABELIAN with order 3;
  D_4 is NON-ABELIAN with order 8 = 2^3. Whichever of those two fails identifies the cause.

    Z_2  abelian  ord 2   1x2    odd eigenspace present: False  -> records are possible
    Z_3  ABELIAN  ord 3   min    odd eigenspace present: True   -> no record can exist
    Z_3  ABELIAN  ord 3   1x2    odd eigenspace present: True   -> no record can exist
    Z_4  abelian  ord 4   1x2    odd eigenspace present: False  -> records are possible
    D_4  NONABEL  ord 8   min    odd eigenspace present: False  -> records are possible
    S_3  nonabel  ord 6   min    odd eigenspace present: True   -> no record can exist
    S_3  nonabel  ord 6   1x2    odd eigenspace present: True   -> no record can exist

  THE CONTROL OVERTURNS THE DIAGNOSIS. Z_3 is ABELIAN and has NO records. D_4 is NON-ABELIAN
  and HAS them. So the obstruction is NOT non-abelianness -- it is whether |G| is a POWER OF
  TWO. Z_2, Z_4 and D_4 have orders 2, 4 and 8 and pass; Z_3 and S_3 have orders 3 and 6 and
  fail, at both carrier sizes tested.

  WHY: clause (i) makes a record a BIT, R^2 = I, so Tr(P_E R) = dim(E) mod 2 and every
  eigenspace must be EVEN-dimensional. |G|^E is a power of two exactly when |G| is. A group
  whose order carries an odd factor produces odd eigenspaces and no record can be written on it.

  D_4 IS A NON-ABELIAN CARRIER WITH RECORDS. It is what O-34's join needs and what AUDIT 2
  showed D(S_3) could not supply. Had this lane tested only S_3, it would have registered
  'non-abelian carriers cannot have records', which the D_4 column shows is FALSE.
