LANE W-13 / Z — ISOLATION LEDGER
One line per comparison.  HELD FIXED | THE ONE THING THAT MOVES | WHERE | ARMS DIFFED.
W-08's isolation audit found the commonest FATAL defect in this program is not "two variables
moved" but "ZERO variables moved": arms that are byte-identical, reported as a confirmation.
A ledger records what an author INTENDED to vary, so W-08 says to treat a ledger as evidence of
nothing and read the code.  This ledger therefore prints, for every comparison, the measured
difference between the arms' INPUTS and their OUTPUTS.
