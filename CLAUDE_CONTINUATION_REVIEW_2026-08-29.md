## 3. Physics correction

The massless-helicity-two route was deliberately introduced as a sufficient
shortcut to recognizable Einstein gravity.  It is not a premise of URFT and
is not the microscopic definition of Gravity Formation.  Linearized
Einstein gravity must eventually exhibit two transverse tensor modes, but
that fact does not require the first record, or the formation mechanism, to
contain a graviton or a two-photon bound state.

The load-bearing program returns to the record-first chain

\[
\text{formed physical memory}
\to\text{record-conditioned relational metric}
\to\text{compatible curvature}
\to\text{complete conserved-stress response}
\to\text{two-way back-reaction}
\to\text{Newton/Einstein weak-field gravity}.
\]

Particle/helicity tests remain downstream consistency and falsification
tests of the linearized macroscopic metric.  They are not permitted to define
the microscopic mechanism or to close the broader program when one narrow
composite channel fails.
