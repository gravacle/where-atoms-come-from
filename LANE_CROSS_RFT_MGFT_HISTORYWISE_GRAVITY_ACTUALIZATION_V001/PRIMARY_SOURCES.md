# Primary-source constraints on gravity and actualization

## Scope

These sources constrain the physical interpretation of the lane's exact finite-
group results.  They do not prove the lane's group-action theorems, which are
self-contained mathematics.  The classifications below distinguish experiment,
conditional theorem/constraint, and proposal/construction.  In particular, no
source below establishes that a branch-conditioned gravitational field
deterministically chooses the branch that generated it.

## Experiments

### E1. Page--Geilker torsion-balance test

**Classification:** experiment.

Don N. Page and C. D. Geilker, ["Indirect Evidence for Quantum
Gravity," *Physical Review Letters* **47**, 979
(1981)](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.979),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.47.979.

**Exact result and relevance:** a macroscopic source configuration was chosen
using radioactive-decay outcomes, and the measured field was inconsistent with
the simplest semiclassical Einstein equation sourced by an expectation value.
The result supports branch-correlated rather than naive ensemble-mean gravity in
that setting.  It does **not** prove that gravity selected the decay outcome, does
not exclude every classical--quantum or stochastic semiclassical theory, and
does not prove that gravity is quantized.  It therefore supports the lane's
separation between a branch-following field and a branch selector, while giving
the reduced-mean-field discussion an empirical ceiling.

### E2. Gran Sasso test of a Diósi--Penrose implementation

**Classification:** experiment plus model-specific inference.

Sandro Donadi et al., ["Underground test of gravity-related wave function
collapse," *Nature Physics* **17**, 74--78
(2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-020-1008-4),
DOI 10.1038/s41567-020-1008-4.  The publisher also provides source data for
the reported spectra.

**Exact result and relevance:** the experiment searched for spontaneous
radiation generated by the stochastic diffusion of the tested Diósi--Penrose
model and excluded its natural parameter-free implementation.  It does not
exclude every gravity-related collapse theory.  It shows that any gravity-
related actualization proposal must expose its noise/cutoff law and quantitative
side effects; an abstract orienting variable and a unique algebraic minimum are
not enough.  An irreducibly stochastic DP/CSL-type law is classified as GARH-Q
in this program, not GARH-D.

## Conditional theorems and no-go constraints

### T1. No-signalling and completely positive operational dynamics

Christoph Simon, Vladimir Buzek, and Nicolas Gisin, ["The no-signaling
condition and quantum dynamics," *Physical Review Letters* **87**, 170405
(2001)](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0102125),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.87.170405.

**Classification:** conditional theorem.

**Exact result and relevance:** under the paper's assumptions that states are
Hilbert-space rays, probabilities obey the trace rule, and superluminal
communication is excluded, density-operator dynamics must be linear and
completely positive.  This constrains a GARH-D completion at the unconditioned
operational level: it must establish preparation/decomposition independence,
ensemble no-signalling, and ancillary composability, or explicitly identify
which premise it replaces.  The theorem does not forbid nonlinear or stochastic
*conditioned* histories whose ensemble map is CPTP, and it is not an
ontology-independent prohibition on every deterministic hidden-variable law.

Joseph Polchinski, ["Weinberg's nonlinear quantum mechanics and the
Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen paradox," *Physical Review Letters* **66**, 397
(1991)](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.397),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.66.397.

**Classification:** model-specific no-go analysis.

**Exact result and relevance:** the Weinberg nonlinear framework leads to EPR
communication or communication between wave-function branches.  It reinforces
the need for an explicit operational no-signalling/composition proof, but it
does not convert the lane's finite symmetry result into a general ban on all
nonlinear or history-dependent physics.

### T2. Backreaction, decoherence, and classical diffusion

Jonathan Oppenheim et al., ["Gravitationally induced decoherence vs
space-time diffusion: testing the quantum nature of gravity," *Nature
Communications* **14**, 7910
(2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-43348-2),
DOI 10.1038/s41467-023-43348-2.

**Classification:** theorem within time-local completely positive Markovian
classical--quantum dynamics.

**Exact result and relevance:** nontrivial backreaction in that framework
requires quantum decoherence together with loss of predictability/diffusion in
the classical phase space, with an experimentally constrainable tradeoff.  It is
not an outcome-selection theorem.  A physical historywise-gravity packet in
this class therefore owes the corresponding decoherence/diffusion accounting;
the finite orbit theorem neither supplies nor rules it out.

### T3. Spatial collapse has diffusive mechanical consequences

Sandro Donadi, Luca Ferialdi, and Angelo Bassi, ["Collapse Dynamics Are
Diffusive," *Physical Review Letters* **130**, 230202
(2023)](https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.230202),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.130.230202.

**Classification:** conditional theorem.

**Exact result and relevance:** under spatial-translation covariance and
no-signalling, spatial collapse must change the system's average momentum
and/or momentum spread.  Thus a spatial-collapse actualization proposal owes a
quantitative diffusion/heating or momentum-transfer prediction and a complete
conservation ledger.  An irreducibly stochastic such law lies on the program's
GARH-Q branch; only a separately supplied deeper deterministic mechanism could
be assessed as GARH-D.  The result does not say that every possible
actualization law is spatial collapse.

## Proposals and explicit constructions

### P1. Stochastic dynamical reduction

Lajos Diósi, ["Models for universal reduction of macroscopic quantum
fluctuations," *Physical Review A* **40**, 1165
(1989)](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.40.1165),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.40.1165; and Gian Carlo Ghirardi, Philip Pearle, and
Alberto Rimini, ["Markov processes in Hilbert space and continuous spontaneous
localization of systems of identical particles," *Physical Review A* **42**,
78 (1990)](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.42.78),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.42.78.

**Classification:** proposed stochastic collapse dynamics, with mathematical
reduction/amplification results internal to the proposed models.

**Exact relevance:** worked dynamical-reduction models obtain individual
reductions through stochastic laws.  They are counterexamples to any suggestion
that all viable collapse constructions must be deterministic, but they do not
derive a realized stochastic draw from an invariant gravity score.  Their noise
law, probability law, conservation cost, and empirical deviations are physical
content that the lane's symbolic boundary bit does not provide.  When the draw
is irreducible, these are GARH-Q candidates in the present program rather than
GARH-D mechanisms.

### P2. Penrose gravitational instability scale

Roger Penrose, ["On gravity's role in quantum state reduction," *General
Relativity and Gravitation* **28**, 581--600
(1996)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02105068),
DOI 10.1007/BF02105068.

**Classification:** physical proposal and heuristic lifetime criterion.

**Exact relevance:** the proposal associates instability of a superposition of
different mass geometries with a gravitational self-energy scale and a lifetime
of order \(\hbar/E_G\).  The scale is not by itself a complete stochastic
outcome law, a derivation of Born weights, or a rule choosing one member of a
symmetric outcome orbit.  It therefore does not evade HGA1 without additional
physical structure.

### P3. Stochastic semiclassical gravity in the Newtonian limit

Antoine Tilloy and Lajos Diósi, ["Sourcing semiclassical gravity from
spontaneously localized quantum matter," *Physical Review D* **93**, 024026
(2016)](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.93.024026),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.93.024026.

**Classification:** explicit stochastic proposal/construction.

**Exact relevance:** the paper constructs a consistent stochastic
semiclassical-gravity model in the Newtonian limit by sourcing gravity from a
spontaneous-localization signal.  It illustrates how stochastic collapse can
avoid the naive expectation-value source problem.  It is not a relativistic GR
completion, a historywise deterministic selector, or evidence that nature uses
the model.

### P4. Collapse driven by stochastic metric fluctuations

G. Gasbarri, M. Toros, S. Donadi, and A. Bassi, ["Gravity induced wave
function collapse," *Physical Review D* **96**, 104013
(2017)](https://journals.aps.org/prd/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevD.96.104013),
DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.96.104013.

**Classification:** proposed stochastic metric-noise model, with collapse and
amplification proved inside the model.

**Exact relevance:** this construction uses complex stochastic fluctuations of
the spacetime metric.  It supports the lane's insistence that an outcome-capable
gravity mechanism needs more than a branch-indexed metric map, but it neither
establishes a deterministic selector nor supplies a completed covariant theory
of physical GR.

### P5. Relativistic stochastic collapse on a fixed spacetime

Roderich Tumulka, ["A Relativistic GRW Flash Process With
Interaction"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.00482) (2020; revised 2022),
DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-46777-7_23.

**Classification:** explicit relativistic stochastic-collapse construction for
the declared particle class.

**Exact relevance:** the model is nonlocal but no-signalling, accommodates
interaction-local dynamics and curved fixed backgrounds, and is limited to a
fixed number of distinguishable particles; the author states that the analogous
model for indistinguishable or variable-number particles is not known.  It shows
that stochastic actualization and relativistic covariance are not logically
incompatible in every restricted model.  It is not dynamical gravity and does
not establish GARH-D; as an irreducibly stochastic collapse model it belongs on
the GARH-Q side of this lane's taxonomy.

### P6. Covariant classical--quantum gravity frameworks

Jonathan Oppenheim and Zachary Weller-Davies, ["Covariant path integrals for
quantum fields back-reacting on classical space-time"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.07283)
(2023; revised 2025).

**Classification:** proposed covariant completely positive
classical--quantum framework.

**Exact relevance:** the authors construct completely positive path integrals
that can impose Lorentz or diffeomorphism invariance and propose an application
with a classical spacetime metric.  This is relevant to the lane's covariance
and composability gates, but it is not a proof of objective single-history
selection and does not turn classical diffusion/decoherence into a derived
realized outcome.

Anirudh Gundhi, Lajos Diósi, and Matteo Carlesso, ["Toward relativistic
generalization of collapse models," *Physical Review A* **114**, 012203
(2026)](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/g7xn-bwfs),
DOI 10.1103/g7xn-bwfs.

**Classification:** Lorentz-invariant non-Markovian collapse proposal.

**Exact relevance:** the paper proposes a local field collapse operator with
Lorentz-invariant non-Markovian noise while explicitly noting that a full-
fledged relativistic collapse extension remains missing.  It is not a
gravity-induced selector or a completed covariant GARH-D theory.  It confirms
that microcausality, vacuum particle creation, and energy-growth control remain
live admission obligations.

## Source-constrained interpretation of this lane

The primary literature supports the following narrow reading:

1. Page--Geilker constrains the simplest expectation-value mean-field source;
   it does not show that a branch-conditioned field selects its own branch.
2. The exact HGA1--HGA3 results are symmetry theorems, not collapse or
   no-signalling theorems.
3. A proposed physical GARH-D mechanism must separately demonstrate
   unconditioned no-signalling/decomposition independence and appropriate
   ancillary composability, in addition to covariance and conservation.
4. A gravity-related stochastic actualization proposal must specify its noise,
   probability, amplification, diffusion/heating, and outcome-resolved exchange
   laws.  If the draw is irreducible, the proposal is GARH-Q here.  A stipulated
   random orienting bit only relocates that unfinished physics and does not earn
   GARH-D.
5. Existing covariant or semiclassical constructions are proposals with stated
   domains and limitations; none supplies the physical historywise selector,
   Born derivation, record join, and full GR completion left open by this lane.
