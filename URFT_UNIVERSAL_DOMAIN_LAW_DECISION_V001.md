## 1. What the remaining sentence means

The current RFT theorem is universal on every record episode that has four
physical structures:

1. a prospectively bounded, branch-independent directed separator between the
   completed source/write ancestry and the registered query, with no return or
   bypass path;
2. one complete apparatus-inclusive joint state or probability law on that
   separator; and
3. exact normalized composable dynamics and complete instruments on both sides
   of the separator; and
4. primitive locality and source-provenance rules under which no future module
   receives the old source label or a remote free setting through an undeclared
   path.

With the exact clauses below, these structures provide one concrete sufficient
specialization of `C`, `J`, and the K/W branches of `S`. The sealed theorem then
supplies the exact record cover and A1--A4 factorization. The open quantifier is
not another algebraic step. It is the claim that **every actual bona-fide
finite-mission record has those structures**.

## 2. Candidate foundational law

First define the local **Directed-Composition predicate** `DCL(r)`. For one
independently admitted candidate

\[
 r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm actual,bf}_{\rm FM},
\]

`DCL(r)` means that the following objects and catalogs are frozen before the
observed outcome, contrast score, or Coverage-U result.

### D1. Directed frontier

One prospectively bounded operational incidence structure has a primitive
past/frontier/future partition with frontier `G`, common to every event arm and
complete registered outcome. Every authorized source/write support and its
declared ancestry is strictly pre-`G`; every registered query is post-`G`;
every authorized source-to-query path crosses `G`; no declared ancestor
edge/map returns from post-`G` to pre-`G`; and the frozen port census contains no
bypass.

### D2. Complete joint separator

Exactly one existing sufficient state category is nominated prospectively:

- a standard-Borel state space with, for every positive-probability arm,
  fixed exogenous context, and pre-`G` history available at `G`, one normalized
  joint probability measure whose marginals are the actual component laws; or
- one unital C*-algebra with, for every such arm, context, and pre-`G` history,
  one
  normalized positive state whose restrictions are the actual component states.

The joint object contains every crossing degree, correlation, charge, dressing,
boundary datum, reference, apparatus, controller, environment, classical
pre-`G` history, and external port. The available history is encoded as a
register in the joint object. The separator state may not be conditioned on a
post-`G` registered outcome. Joint existence is a physical ontology premise; it
is not inferred from marginals.

### D3. Exact two-sided sewing

The actual pre- and post-`G` dynamics are built from finitely many
prospectively bounded exact stochastic modules and complete stochastic
instruments, or exact UCP modules and complete CP instruments. Identity,
normalization, sequential/parallel composition, and every failure/no-click/
saturation/adaptive outcome are retained. All memory crossing `G` is explicit.
Any admitted exact continuum or bounded higher-order module must first contract
to the corresponding ordinary K/W macro-map on its side of the external
frontier; no endpoint-fitted channel is admitted.

### D4. Locality and provenance

Every module respects the frozen directed incidence. No module receives a
remote freely chosen setting without a declared physical path. Post-`G`
adaptive behavior may depend on the source arm only through the actual
separator/history wires; the future law has no direct source-label argument.
Every later fresh input is either prepared independently of the source arm or
already represented in the complete separator object. In the classical branch,
incomparable local-laboratory kernels factor conditionally on the separator
variable and their respective local settings. In the quantum branch,
incomparable local-laboratory algebras commute (or are explicitly tensor
separated), and localized maps obey causal factorization.

The global **Universal Directed-Composition Law** is then

\[
 \operatorname{U\!DCL}:\Longleftrightarrow
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm actual,bf}_{\rm FM},
 \quad DCL(r).                                         \tag{UDCL}

This is metric-free and pre-Lorentzian, not causal-assumption-free. Its directed

## 4. Why RFT cannot prove DCL from recordhood alone

The repository has exact logical countermodels:

- a distributional closed-timelike-curve record is readable but has no ordinary
  chronological macro-cut; and
- a chronological PR-resource-plus-register model is readable but violates the
  frozen local classical bound and complex-quantum C*/UCP bound while retaining
  free settings and no declared cross-laboratory path.

The first violates D1. With D4's locality/free-setting clause and D2--D3's
frozen positive K/W categories, the second violates the nominated local
composition class. They prove that `REC -> DCL` is not a theorem of bare record
logic on the broad model domain. They do not show that either countermodel is
physically realized. No expansion of the current K/W/A/I representation
machinery can decide which physical processes nature permits.

That is normal for a physical theory. Mathematics proves consequences from its
physical laws; experiment tests whether the laws describe nature. General
relativity does not prove from logic that nature uses Lorentzian metrics, and
quantum theory does not prove from logic that nature uses positive Hilbert-space
states. Their domain laws are stated, consequences are derived, and failures are
searched for.

## 6. Exact falsifier

U-DCL is false if one actual bona-fide record, admitted before this test under
the existing finite-mission `REC` criteria, has no faithful DCL realization in
a prospectively frozen audit category. Before data are inspected, the test must
freeze:

1. the mission interval, source/write and query supports, complete outcomes,
   free settings, and physical incidence;
2. the apparatus/environment/boundary/charge/dressing/reference port census and
   a rule for independently evidenced census amendments;
3. every DCL-admissible classical standard-Borel or quantum C*/UCP category
   applicable to the episode, its locality/factorization conditions, and any
   independent physical warrant that makes one category exclusive; and
4. the complete instrument family whose joint statistics must be reproduced.

A decisive witness is then a proof that no normalized positive joint measure or
C*-state plus exact local composable maps in **every applicable** frozen category
reproduces the complete statistics while respecting the declared incidence and
free settings, or—when one category is independently and exclusively physically
warranted—that the episode fails that category. An independently bona-fide
record whose registered query is irreducibly in a source/write causal loop with
no branch-independent no-return frontier is also decisive.

Quasiprobability negativity in one chosen representation, an omitted port, a
fitted marginal, or an unregistered outcome is not a falsifier. A census may be
reopened only by an independently measured missing carrier, after which the
test is prospectively refrozen; it may not retreat indefinitely in response to
failure.

## 7. The decision and recommendation

The genuine decision is:

> Should U-DCL be adopted as the working foundational, empirically falsifiable
> physical-domain postulate of URFT over actual bona-fide finite-mission records,
> thereby predicting that no such record violates the directed-frontier,
> complete-separator, exact-sewing, and locality/provenance clauses?

The bona-fide domain remains independently defined. One actual admitted
exception falsifies U-DCL; it may not be removed from the domain after failure.

**Recommendation:** yes—adopt U-DCL explicitly as a **working universal physical
postulate/conjecture**, not as a theorem secretly derived from `REC` and not as
an earned law of nature, while continuing to seek a deeper common fundamental
dynamics compatible with both the RFT and gravity lanes.
Then state the scientific status in two layers:

- the URFT coverage theorem is mathematically proved conditional on the declared
  postulate; and
- the claim that nature obeys U-DCL is a universal physical conjecture open to
  experimental or observational refutation.

Do not build complement architectures for mathematical exceptions in advance.
Open one only if a physically realized failure, or an independently established
fundamental dynamics, identifies the structure that the complement must cover.
