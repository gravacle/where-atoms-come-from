  2. IS IT THE SHARING, OR THE SIZE OF THE CARRIER?  hold m = 2 records on site 0 and add

========================================================================================================
  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  chi(record 0) falls with m, the number of records SHARING ITS ENVIRONMENT SITE:
    0.789366 -> 0.164650 -> 0.081033 -> 0.081489, saturating.
  THE CONTROL IS FLAT TO 0.000000: the identical k-record carrier with every record on its own
  site holds chi = 0.789366 at every m. And SPECTATOR records -- which enlarge the carrier
  without crowding the site -- leave chi unmoved to six decimals.

  SO THE DENSITY VARIABLE IS RECORDS PER ENVIRONMENT SITE. Not the number of records: adding
  records changes nothing. Not the size of the carrier: spectators change nothing. SHARING is
  the whole effect, and it is the first quantity in this program that knows how much is
  enclosed rather than merely what is where.

  IT IS A CAPACITY CEILING, NOT A WEAK COUPLING. Across a 7x range of lam the spread record
  climbs to 0.60 while the crowded record SATURATES near 0.135 and stops. The suppression
  STRENGTHENS as the coupling grows -- ratio 0.7530 at lam = 0.20 down to 0.1980 at lam = 1.40.
  An environment site holds a bounded amount about the records written on it, and records
  sharing a site SPLIT it.

  WHAT THIS IS NOT. It is a suppression law, not yet a transport law: nothing here has been
  shown to curve anything or to move a record. What it supplies is the missing ingredient the
  topological quantities could not -- a magnitude that responds to enclosed density.
