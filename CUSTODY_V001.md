# WHERE ATOMS COME FROM — CUSTODY CONVENTIONS V001 — 2026-08-16

Every rule below exists because its absence cost the predecessor something nameable.
Cited: the predecessor's Q-1178 (the documentation failure and its mechanism) and Q-1180
(its assignment form).

## 1. THE POINTER RULE — THE ONE THAT MATTERS MOST

**No term may enter a governing clause without a pointer.** A pointer is a digest, a
`file:line`, or a named ruling. A term quoted into a discipline block **without** one is
flagged, not inherited.

*Why:* the predecessor's dominant failure was that things travelled by quotation and lost
their anchors — thirteen instances in one day, including a governing term quoted twenty-nine
times with **zero** definitions anywhere, which entered from a brief that was never on disk.
Every one would have been caught at first use by this rule.

**Corollary — assignments carry owners.** A delegated task names its owner and its deadline
condition. The predecessor lost one that sat undone across an entire campaign.

## 2. ONE CONVENTION PER THING

- **Sidecars:** `<filename>.sha256` and `<filename>.seal.sha256`. One form. If a second ever
  appears, normalize immediately and additively.
- **Rulings:** one filename prefix. Never two.
- **No non-citable carriers.** Nothing that governs may live in a file that forbids citing it,
  or outside the declared corpus.

*Why:* 282 files carried only a variant sidecar form and a lane refused a valid sealed artifact
because of it; two ruling prefixes caused universal negatives to be run over half the rulings,
twice; and a principal ruling sat invisible for three weeks in a self-declared non-citable
carrier.

## 3. SWEEP DISCIPLINE

- Report the **actual hit count** for every pattern run. Listing a pattern is not running it.
- **Open what you count.** If a sweep returns a set you rely on, open it or declare exactly
  which members you did not open and why.
- **Normalize newlines** before any count. A line-based grep missed seven wrapped matches and
  three whole artifacts in one commission.
- **Declare the universe.** State what was swept and reconcile the headline against the sweep
  block before sealing.

## 4. BUILD AND AUDIT

Every build is paired with a **default-refute audit** whose default verdict is REFUTED. The
audit re-derives at bytes; testimony carries no weight.

**Known limitation, stated rather than hidden:** brief-writer, builder and auditor are
routinely the same model. The pairing is adversarial in **prompt** and not in **lineage**, and
a failure mode shared by that model passes through all three roles invisibly. Independent
lineage is the remedy where available; where it is not, findings are graded
**adversarially-checked**, never **independently-corroborated**.

## 5. THE DISCIPLINE THAT REPLACES THE PREDECESSOR'S FENCES

**Alpha may constrain STRUCTURE. Alpha may not SELECT structure.**

- Legitimate: "the construction must carry a free dimensionless coupling of this type, entering
  here, with these consequences"; using that alpha is dimensionless, is the U(1) coupling, and
  is small.
- Barred: choosing a construction because it lands on a known value; inserting a measured
  number as a parameter and reading off what follows.

*Why:* alpha is an **input** to this project, not a target. There is nothing to fit. The
predecessor's three fences and its frozen release condition existed to guard a computation this
project does not perform, and carrying them forward would forbid *considering* what it should
only forbid *selecting*.

## 6. THE METRIC

**Progress is measured in constructions completed, never in questions closed.** A closed route
is recorded and does not increment. The predecessor closed eleven in one day without moving.

## 7. INHERITANCE

Predecessor material is **cited by digest, never copied.** Its artifacts stay where they are.
Nothing is inherited as foundation; everything inherited is cited as premise, with its grade —
derived, adopted, or authored — carried with it.

## 8. THE REGISTER

Question-indexed, append-only, one row per settled question, each carrying: the question as
someone would re-ask it, the ruling, **where the proof is**, and the exact reopen condition.
A row with no reopen condition is closed permanently. This form is salvaged from the
predecessor and is the best thing it built.
