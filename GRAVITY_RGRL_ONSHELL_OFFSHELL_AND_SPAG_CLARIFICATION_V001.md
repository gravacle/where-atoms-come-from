full-rank, or declared dense-range, ancestry coordinate on the six-component
local spatial-metric **variation tangent**. That is a variational and
constitutive statement. It does not, without an additional physical source or
solution law, predict two different stationary metric solutions when the two
arms have:

1. identical complete stress-energy;
2. identical admitted remainder;
3. identical gravitational Cauchy, incoming, asymptotic, and boundary data;
   and
4. no detector, readout, or analysis bias.

The frozen RGRL adoption, RGRL infrared endpoint theorem, Gravity Formation
Theory closure, and prospective SPAG protocol remain preserved as custody
artifacts. This packet recommends that an off-shell RGRL-C tangent column not
be reported as an on-shell force or common-freefall column unless the physical
term moving the solution is independently located in the field equations or
their data. Principal adoption would be required to make that recommendation
program policy.

## 4. Recommended prospective classification of a nonzero SPAG outcome

Every modeled contribution to a reproduced nonzero ancestry-correlated SPAG
residual should be classified into one primary bucket before a physical gravity
claim is made. Distinct contributions may occupy distinct buckets, but the
same term should not be counted twice. If the surviving residual cannot be
uniquely decomposed, its headline bucket is the unclassified anomaly in
section 4.6.

### 4.1 Lineage contribution to complete stress-energy

If an independently specified lineage action contributes

\[
 T^{\rm lineage}_{\mu\nu}
 :=-{2\over\sqrt{-g}}\,
 {\delta\Gamma^{\rm retained}_{\rm lineage}\over\delta g^{\mu\nu}},
 \qquad
 \delta T^{\rm lineage}_{\mu\nu}
 :=T^{\rm lineage}_{K\mu\nu}-T^{\rm lineage}_{B\mu\nu},          \tag{C09}
\]

then it belongs inside \(T^{\rm complete}\), exactly once. A calibrated
\(\delta T^{\rm lineage}\ne0\) can produce a leading-Einstein response without
changing the Einstein equation. The arms are then matched in ordinary
stress-energy, not in complete stress-energy. This is a physical source
result, not a source-free effect of a historical label.

### 4.2 Bounded lineage-dependent remainder

A difference \(\delta\Delta^{\rm rem}\ne0\) is an admitted correction or
bounded extra-mode response only if it satisfies the frozen EIR-5 ownership,
finite-band, and subdominance rules. It does not establish a leading
Einstein-source column. Its RGRL relevance requires an independent type join
from the qualified lineage intervention to the remainder operator.

### 4.3 Unsuppressed extra mode or varying law

A lineage-dependent \(G_{\rm eff}\), \(C_R\), kinetic coefficient,
nonmetricity mode, second metric, or other unsuppressed field changes the
leading endpoint law unless it is derived to be one constant on the connected
phase. Such a result is outside the current EIR-4/EIR-5 closure and requires a
successor action with its own equations, Ward identities, constraints, and
stability audit. It is a possible discovery, not an RGRL-C force-column pass
under the frozen endpoint.

### 4.4 Changed gravitational Cauchy or boundary data

A nonzero homogeneous contribution belongs to changed gravitational initial,
incoming, asymptotic, or apparatus-boundary data. It is a
history-conditioned gravitational-data response. It is not a local
lineage-source coefficient. Approach-direction, hold-time, route-cut,
termination-distance, and boundary crossover tests are recommended to separate
this bucket from a persistent local source term.

### 4.5 Ordinary collateral

Ordinary collateral is an experimental interpretation bucket, not a separate
physical right-hand-side term. A physical force, stress, mass-placement error,
heat or electromagnetic field, support motion, gas or charge effect,
controller response, termination field, or geometry change should be owned in
\(T^{\rm complete}/S_{\rm dr}\), \(R_{\rm dr}\), or
\(\mathbb C_{dL}\), according to its action and data class. Only detector,
readout, and analysis error belongs in \(q_{\rm coll}\). Either kind of
ordinary collateral earns no lineage-gravity result even if it correlates with
KEEP/BREAK.

### 4.6 Unclassified anomaly

A reproduced residual for which none of the preceding sources has been
independently identified is an ancestry-correlated but unclassified anomaly.
It shows that the joint physical model or matching ledger is incomplete. It
may motivate a successor theory, but it is not yet confirmation of RGRL-C,
Gravity Formation Theory, a varying \(G\), or any named microscopic mechanism.
