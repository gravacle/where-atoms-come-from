# Counterexamples and exact boundary

## 1. A finite score table is not a physical carrier

For any normalized finite law \(P(h\mid x,z,j)\), introduce an artificial node
\(C=X\) and a child kernel

\[
 K(h\mid C=x,z,j)=P(h\mid x,z,j).
\]

This finite DAG reproduces every registered outcome exactly.  It says nothing
about which physical degree acquired the distinction.  If the actual source is
destroyed after writing a film grain, spin, trap, neural state, or distributed
relation, the synthetic \(X\)-wire is not the later carrier.  Treating it as one
would satisfy response equality by putting the answer inside the representation.

This counterexample separates **representability** from **faithful physical
admission**.  The former is automatic for finite tables; the latter is the
scientific Coverage-U claim.

## 2. Hidden bypass twins refute response-only cut authentication

Let \(X\) be a fair bit.  In both worlds the visible target and query satisfy
\(C=X\) and \(Q=X\):

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccc}
 & C&H&Q\\ \hline
 H_0&X&0&C\\
 H_1&X&X&H.
\end{array}
\]

Every passive registered statistic of \((X,C,Q)\) is identical.  But under the
common replacement \(C\leftarrow0\), the query loses all event contrast in
\(H_0\) and retains perfect contrast in \(H_1\).  Thus \(\{C\}\) is a complete
event-to-query cut only in \(H_0\).

No rule using only their common response law can certify the visible cut in the
first world without making the same false certification in the second.  A
physical port/netlist certificate or a valid spanning intervention family is new
information and can distinguish them.  Strong visible correlation cannot.

A hidden post-cut analyst label, radio link, shared log, or controller input is
the same logical attack.

## 3. Separate port marginals can omit the record

Let \(U\) be fair, \(C=X\oplus U\), \(D=U\), and \(Q=C\oplus D\).  Both
crossing marginals are event-blind, while their joint parity is exactly \(X\).
Consequently a cover authenticated port by port can omit an event-bearing joint
correlation.  `IACC` requires a complete joint crossing object or one common
joint future extension.

This does not demand that every local marginal be informative.  The faithful
carrier can be distributed or relational.

## 4. Endpoint positivity does not prove complete composability

On \(M_2\), let \(T(a)=a^{\mathsf T}\).  Identity and \(T\) agree on every
diagonal preparation and diagonal effect, so a finite classical-looking endpoint
test can give identical probability tables for them.  Nevertheless \(T\) is not
completely positive.  Its Choi matrix is the swap operator \(F\), and the
antisymmetric vector \(v=|01\rangle-|10\rangle\) obeys

\[
 v^*Fv=-2.
\]

Thus a response law on an incomplete test family cannot establish that a module
is safe under ancillary extension or finite quantum sewing.  C4 requires
independently authenticated standard composability; endpoint agreement is not a
substitute.

## 5. Finite operational resolution does not make a coarse box exact

A laboratory may register one bit during a one-second window while its black-box
controller has unresolved open-ended feedback, unknown external inputs, or an
unbounded ancestor process.  Drawing one box around the controller and one arrow
to the bit creates a finite diagram, but does not establish a bounded exact
physical module or complete port census.

The record claim can still be meaningful at finite resolution.  What fails is
membership in the present authenticated-cover class.  The correct token is
`OUTSIDE_IACC_DOMAIN`, not `NO_RECORD`.

## 6. A unique inaccessible record refutes universal certification

In the one-shot record world

\[
 X:=U_X,\qquad R:=X,\qquad Q:=R,
\]

the event physically writes the surviving carrier and the later query reads it
without recreating the distinction.  Afterward, destroy or make inaccessible the
event, writer, provenance traces, intervention opportunities, transported
calibrations, custody records, and negative controls.

The same surviving law is generated by

\[
 U\sim P_X,\qquad X:=U,\qquad R:=U,\qquad Q:=R,
\]

where no \(X\to R\) write occurs.  Even the full accessible
\(P(X,R,Q)\) is identical.  A certification rule must return the same result in
both worlds.  Accepting falsely certifies the second; rejecting leaves the genuine
first record uncertified.

Therefore an all-ontic theorem with independently authenticated `AuthEnc` in
its consequent is false.  Record truth does not manufacture missing custody
evidence.  The surviving distinction is:

\[
 \text{ontic physical coverage}
 \quad\text{versus}\quad
 \text{an authenticated URM verdict on available evidence}.
\]

The ontic complete-cover proposition remains open; it does not justify an
empirical `PASS`.  An incomplete unique archive returns
`INDETERMINATE` or `OUTSIDE_IACC_DOMAIN`, not `NO_RECORD`.

## 7. A finite process need not have the required macro-cut

A finite source/read pair with operational signalling in both directions has no
one fixed source-before-read cut.  Internal indefinite order is already covered
when the whole module lies on one side of a definite external macro-cut.  If the
nominated event and query themselves lack that orientation, finiteness alone
does not put the mission in `FICO-DM-ADMIT`.  A later persistent log can define a
different derivative-read mission; it does not retroactively order the internal
pair.

## 8. Coverage of alternatives does not produce one alternative

For an endogenous quantum event, a conditional family of branch record laws can
have a faithful complete boundary after the event.  This proves neither that one
branch becomes objectively actual nor why its frequency has the Born form.  The
finite-mission admission theorem starts once the alternative and physical record
mission are defined.  The unconditioned single-run event law remains additional
strong-URFT physics.

## 9. Unbounded all-future coverage remains false

If one event is broadcast to countably many retained sites and the claim includes
every possible future read, no bounded cut need contain all descendants.  A
finite resolved event alphabet does not make that all-future claim a bounded
mission.  The independently frozen domain excludes it rather than weakening
`complete` until any finite subset passes.
