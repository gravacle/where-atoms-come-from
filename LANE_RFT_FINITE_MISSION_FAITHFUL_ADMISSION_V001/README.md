## Result first

successful representation.  A candidate is specified by physical referents, a
bounded event-to-query mission, finite resolved event alternatives, contexts,
registered instruments and complete outcome histories, a nominated physical
lineage, a closure regime, and an error contract.  A bona-fide record is then a
physical episode in which an interaction creates new counterfactual predictive
distinguishability in that lineage and the distinction reaches the registered
label-blind query without being recreated there.  Neither definition mentions
`FCN_ADMIT`, an algebra, a quotient rank, or constructor success.

Every normalized finite registered response law has a trivial finite classical
score-table model.  That observation does **not** solve coverage: the model can
place the event label itself on a synthetic boundary wire and reproduce all
registered probabilities while misidentifying the actual carrier and causal
paths.

The exact positive result is a faithful-transcription theorem.  If a bona-fide
finite-mission record has a true complete physical cover whose modules obey one
of the already proved composability branches, the same physical modules, ports,
maps, source custody, and cut give an ontically faithful encoding.  When that
cover is also independently authenticated, it transcribes into one of

1. `FCN_ADMIT`;
2. `ACN_CP_ADMIT`;
3. `CSTAR_CP_ADMIT` (the C*-UCP lane); or
4. `FICO-DM-ADMIT`.

The transcription preserves the exact resolved registered law, physical
referents, event-bearing paths, complete outcomes, and no-hidden-label

## Ontic target and certification boundary

Two quantifiers must be separated.

\[
 \underbrace{\forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM}\ \exists{\cal C}\,
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C})}_{\text{ontic complete physical cover}}
                                                               \tag{O}
\]

Here `OCC_UNION` means that the actual episode has a true complete causal cover
in one of the four standard physical composability branches, without requiring
that evidence of that fact survives.  Equation (O) is the remaining ontic
physics proposition and is **open**.

By contrast,

\[
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM}\ \exists\Xi\,
 [\operatorname{AuthEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)\land
  \operatorname{ADM}_{\cup}(\Xi)]                        \tag{A}
\]

is **refuted** if the all-ontic domain includes unique inaccessible natural
records.  In an exact counterexample, \(X\) really writes \(R\) and a later query
reads it, but every independent provenance trace and intervention opportunity is
then destroyed.  A latent-common-cause world with no \(X\to R\) write has the
same surviving law.  No sound rule can independently authenticate the genuine
world while rejecting its twin.

The surviving certification theorem is record-by-record:

\[
 \operatorname{IACC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C})
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists\Xi\,[\operatorname{AuthEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)
              \land\operatorname{ADM}_{\cup}(\Xi)].
\]

Missing authentication returns `INDETERMINATE` or
`OUTSIDE_IACC_DOMAIN`, not `NO_RECORD`.

The ontic premise is not a consequence of a positive finite response law.  An
honest-carrier/hidden-bypass twin gives the same visible probabilities while
common replacement screens only the honest world.  A second exact witness shows
that identity evolution and matrix transposition agree on a finite diagonal test
family even though transposition is not completely positive.  Consequently
neither physical cut completeness nor standard composability can be manufactured
from response agreement.

This isolates the smallest physical obstruction: a true complete standard causal
cover must exist; independent evidence is additionally required for a URM
certification.  More material categories or another response-fitted
representation cannot supply either fact.

## Boundary

This lane does not prove that every actual finite-mission record has an ontic
complete cover.  Modules with unresolved ancestry or ports, missions with no
definite event-to-query macro-cut, exact gravitational/background-dynamical
records, and processes outside the standard classical/quantum composability
branches remain outside the proved physical union.  Inaccessible natural archives
also decisively prevent universal independent certification even when an ontic
cover exists.

It also does not derive the unconditioned single-run actualization law.  For an
endogenous quantum outcome it covers the physical record contrast once the event
alternative and a valid record mission exist; producing exactly one alternative
per run remains the separate strong outcome-as-record problem.
