## 1. Is the record domain defined by admission success?

No.  D0--D4 identify a physical episode, finite operational contract, formation
question, complete registered outcomes, and representation-independent RFT
predicate before any encoding is built.  `RECORD_FM` uses the physical claims of
novel predictive distinction, lineage, closure, and noncreating read.  It does
not use an algebra, causal quotient, rank, or one of the four admission tokens.

The record predicate does contain causal physical content.  That is appropriate:
correlation alone is not a record-formation theory.  But it does not specify all
registered ancestors, an exact bounded module decomposition, one complete
separator, standard CP composability, or an independently authenticated port
census.  It therefore assumes neither `OCC` nor `IACC`.

Disposition: domain is independently specified and noncircular.

## 2. Does finite mission/resolution imply a finite exact cover?

No.  D1 limits the claim and outcome alphabets, not the ontology hidden inside a
coarse module.  C1 separately excludes unresolved infinite ancestry, unbounded
feedback, and unknown label-bearing ports.  This prevents the false proof in
which the whole universe is called one finite slot.

Disposition: operational and architectural finiteness remain distinct.

## 3. Is FM1 being mistaken for universal physical coverage?

No.  FM1 is explicitly labeled a weak score-table simulator.  It places the
event variable directly on a synthetic boundary and proves response equality
only.  E0 and E2 prohibit promotion of that construction to a physically faithful
encoding; E4 separately prohibits promotion to an authenticated encoding.

The hidden-bypass twins demonstrate the difference exactly: the same finite law
admits the same simulator in both worlds although the visible physical cut is
complete in only one.

Disposition: no table-representation overclaim.

## 4. Is the transcription theorem tautological?

FM2 is a reduction theorem.  C4 deliberately states the physical composability
properties already handled by the four proved admission lanes, while C0--C3 state
physical identity, completeness, and provenance.  C5 is the strictly additional
evidential authentication clause.  The theorem proves that no additional
representation step is needed once the relevant facts are supplied and that the
resulting packet is faithful rather than merely response-equivalent.

It does **not** derive C0--C5 or call their conjunction a proof of itself.  Its
scientific gain is exact localization of the universal quantifier: further
algebraic packaging cannot close the remaining physical-cover proposition.

Disposition: valid scoped reduction; ontic `OPCC_FM` remains open, while
all-ontic independently authenticated coverage is refuted.

## 6. Faithfulness audit

Exact endpoint equality alone is insufficient.  E0 preserves physical referents;
E2 preserves every event-bearing route and crossing degree; E3 preserves
interventions and forbids hidden post-cut label input; E4 requires external
physical authentication.  C0--C4 provide ontic physical faithfulness; C5 upgrades
it to authenticated admission.

An encoding may contract event-independent internal physics inside a genuinely
exact bounded module.  It may not delete a physical bypass, replace a joint
correlation by its marginals, or add an outcome-defined event wire.

Disposition: the proof earns ontic faithful encoding from `OCC` and
authenticated encoding from `IACC`.

## 9. No-go audit

The observational-twin theorem has a limited and sufficient conclusion.  It does
not say that architecture can never be authenticated.  It says the finite
registered law and visible target labels alone cannot supply a sound-and-complete
certificate of physical cut completeness.  Replacement interventions, port
inspection, shielding tests, source custody, and independently calibrated
architecture are additional inputs and can break the equivalence.

The transpose witness likewise does not claim that complete positivity is
empirically unknowable.  It proves that agreement on a finite diagonal endpoint
family does not entail CP under ancillary extension.

The unique-archive construction is stronger than ordinary response
nonidentifiability.  It builds a true one-shot record and removes every independent
provenance/intervention route by premise.  Its latent-common-cause twin has the
same surviving law and no write edge.  Therefore the genuine record lacks E4,
refuting the universal authenticated consequent while leaving ontic cover
existence untouched.

Disposition: exact impossibility boundary and exact all-ontic certification
counterexample, no ontic nonexistence claim.
