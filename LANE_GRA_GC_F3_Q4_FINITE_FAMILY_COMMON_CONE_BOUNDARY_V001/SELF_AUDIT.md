# GC finite-family/common-cone self-audit

## Family and source discipline

- `G_(2L)->G_L` is a graph cover, not an inclusion or isometry of ice Hilbert
  spaces.
- Only FV's bounded local pair/ring coefficients are promoted across the
  family.  FY's complete nonzero-momentum diagonal ledger is not copied from
  its 30-cell quotient to `G_L`.
- Local rank six is an off-shell coefficient/operator-witness statement, not
  a retarded spectral rank or residue.
- Counts and cover fibers are reconstructed without enumerating a many-body
  thermodynamic Hilbert space.
- The FS-to-FD join is an explicit vertex embedding whose four edge
  displacements are `-a_* n_a`; FD's volume is not attached by analogy.

## Momentum and refinement discipline

- Bravais translation characters and reciprocal norms are exact.  Within-cell
  native form factors remain part of the declared FY-to-`G_L` ledger lift.
- Momentum labels are classes modulo `L`; minimum-norm Brillouin-zone
  representatives are used.  The first shells follow from the positive
  integer form `q=sum n_i^2+sum_(i<j)(n_i-n_j)^2` and exact equality
  classification, not a finite-box inference.
- Cover pullback preserves old characters as `n->2n`; new odd cover modes are
  explicitly not called inherited responses.
- Fixed-volume affine refinement is separated from the infrared limit.
- A joint continuum/IR claim requires both `a->0` and `La->infinity`.
- TT projection and A3 isotropy are called kinematics, not a Ward identity or
  a pole.

## Response discipline

- `2 Delta R` is tied specifically to the declared even-Euclidean cosine
  transform.  It is not called a convention-independent or necessarily
  constant infrared residue.
- Spectral labels are full degenerate-energy projectors, and a controlled
  coalescing cluster of two finite-volume polarizations is also admitted; one
  eigenvector is never asked to carry a rank-two residue.
- Linear coalescing gaps, isotropy, finite-momentum rank-two visibility, raw
  weight exponent, atomic stability, physical Ward/contact ancestry, common
  calibrated speed, and higher-term stability are retained as separate gates.
- FY's `1/sqrt(2L^3)` convention is called count normalization.  Physical or
  canonical amplitude claims require a prospectively derived `Z_Q(a_*,k)`;
  post-result reweighting is disallowed.
- FL's photon provides the explicit counterexample to a nonzero-constant raw
  numerator requirement.  A controlled vanishing power of `2 Delta R` does
  not refute a massless pole.
- A disappearing source overlap is not overclaimed as exclusion of every
  possible invisible tensor sector.
- FL's no-go is kept at the Gaussian-Maxwell direct-composite baseline;
  inherited non-Gaussian binding or a distinct same-parent rank-two sector is
  not excluded.
- Three sizes are labeled a minimum trend/falsification packet, not proof of
  an asymptotic limit.
- The three-size spectral screen is explicitly unable to supply GB's missing
  local momentum/recoil/controller/port ownership and stress Ward identity.

## Public-data discipline

- Current public custody supports the Maxwell phase through flux scaling and
  a static structure factor.
- It is not claimed that qualifying dynamic TT data are publicly available.
- A public substitute must expose the required operator insertions,
  normalization, imaginary-time covariance, and multiple volumes.
