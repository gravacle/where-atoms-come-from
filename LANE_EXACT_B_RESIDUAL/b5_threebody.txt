(2) THE GENERALISED C-38 THEOREM, AND ITS TEST.
    CLAIM.  Let every partner commute with the read record R, let none of them be written
    on R's bath site, and let there exist a record W anticommuting with R and commuting
    with every partner (here W = Z1).  Then chi(R) is EXACTLY the alone value.
    PROOF.  Every coupling operator commutes with R, so H_tot preserves each R-eigensector
    and the maximally mixed code state splits as (1/2) sum_r Pi_r/(d/2) (x) rho_th.  In
    sector r the bath qubit carrying R decouples and evolves under E_0 Z_0 + lam*r*X_0,
    so rho_B(r) = sigma_0(r) (x) tau(r).  W (x) I_B maps sector + to sector -, commutes
    with every partner coupling and acts trivially on the bath, so tau(+) = tau(-) = tau
    EXACTLY.  Both S(average) and average-of-S then shift by the same S(tau) and chi is
    unchanged.  NOTE what the claim does NOT require: the partners may pair with EACH
    OTHER, and they may be piled onto one site or spread over many.
