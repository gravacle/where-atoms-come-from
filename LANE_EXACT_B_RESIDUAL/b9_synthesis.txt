  POWER LAW chi ~ q^p fitted over q = 2..8 (the crowding family; q = 1 is the alone point
  and is excluded because no site is shared there):
    exponent p                                     = -1.5438
    1-sigma uncertainty (5 dof, from the fit residual) = +- 0.0206
    rms of the power-law residual, in chi          = 8.164e-04
    FLOOR-V for comparison                         = 1.852e-02
    -> the power-law residual is 0.04x the venue floor, i.e. at or below it.
       Within the pure-crowding family a single power in the occupancy is as good as the
       data can distinguish; there is no room there for an extra term.

  WHAT p = -1 WOULD HAVE MEANT.  Equipartition of a FIXED site capacity -- the natural
  reading of C-36, that q records sharing a site split its bits -- gives chi ~ 1/q, i.e.
  p = -1 exactly.  The measured exponent is -1.5438 +- 0.0206, which is 26 sigma steeper.
  The q records here are mutually commuting and mutually exchangeable by a logical Clifford,
  so each holds the same chi and the TOTAL the site holds about them is q*chi:
     q    chi per record    q * chi (the site total)
     2    0.136408688972              0.272817377944
     3    0.071783219844              0.215349659532
     4    0.045162574990              0.180650299960
     5    0.033530458642              0.167652293210
     6    0.024307043226              0.145842259356
     7    0.020221343237              0.141549402659
     8    0.015623024871              0.124984198968
  -> the site total FALLS with q (as q^{p+1} = q^-0.544).  A site is not dividing a
     fixed budget among its records; the budget itself shrinks as the site fills.  That is a
     26-sigma departure from the equipartition reading, inside the ONE family where the
     explained model is otherwise perfect.

  the M1 residual in this family, as a FRACTION of chi (q = 2..8):
    mean -0.2521   spread 0.0868   min -0.2865   max -0.1998
  the same at q = 1 (the alone point):  +0.0306
  -> across a 33x fall in chi (0.5215 -> 0.0156) the M1 residual stays a roughly CONSTANT
     FRACTION of chi, about a quarter of it, with no trend toward zero.  The residual is
     not an additive term of fixed size that could be swamped at large N; it is a fixed
     RELATIVE misfit, and there is no regime in the reachable range where the explained
     model becomes adequate.

  These bounds are LARGE -- comparable to the leading coefficients themselves.  That is the
  honest report: at reachable N this venue does NOT constrain an extra term to be small.
  The reason is not the venue's size, it is that the EXPLAINED MODEL ITSELF MISFITS BY 25%,
  so there is a great deal of room underneath it.  The bound that IS tight is the one on the
  terms proved exactly zero in Part 1, where the bound is 1e-15 and holds at every N.
