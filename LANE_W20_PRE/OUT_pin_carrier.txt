     d : C_1 -> C_0 ,  d(edge) = its two endpoints        (over GF(2))
and its transpose, the coboundary
     delta : C^0 -> C^1 ,  delta(v) = star(v) = the edges at v .
Two subspaces of C_1 are therefore FORCED, and nothing else is:
   Z_1 = ker d      the CYCLE space      (Z-strings that are gauge invariant)

dim Z_1 (cycle space)   = L - V + 1 = 5      |Z_1| = 32
dim B^1 (cocycle space) = V - 1     = 7      |B^1| = 128   (nonzero: 127)

intersection-form pairing <x,y> = |x AND y| mod 2 .  Verified exhaustively below.
pairs (z in Z_1, c in B^1) with |z AND c| ODD : 0   of 4096 checked
