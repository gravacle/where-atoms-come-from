DERIVATION.  P = (p00 + p10 x) + y (p01 + p11 x).  For |x| = |y| = 1 the y-phase is free,
so min_y |P| = | |p00+p10 x| - |p01+p11 x| |.  Hence P has a zero on T^2 iff
     exists t :  |p00+p10 e^{it}|^2 = |p01+p11 e^{it}|^2 ,
i.e.  A + B cos t = 0 has a solution with
     A = p00^2+p10^2-p01^2-p11^2 ,  B = 2(p00 p10 - p01 p11).
Since cos t sweeps [-1,1] exactly, that is A^2 <= B^2, i.e. (A-B)(A+B) <= 0, and
     A-B = (p00-p10)^2-(p01-p11)^2 = (p00-p10-p01+p11)(p00-p10+p01-p11)
     A+B = (p00+p10)^2-(p01+p11)^2 = (p00+p10-p01-p11)(p00+p10+p01+p11).
The total p00+p10+p01+p11 = 1 > 0 divides out, leaving  D <= 0  with
     D = (p00+p11-p10-p01)(p00+p01-p10-p11)(p00+p10-p01-p11).
D is the product over the three ways of splitting the FOUR classes into two PAIRS.

AT p00 = 0 THIS COLLAPSES TO M3-1: D = (p11-p10-p01)(p01-p10-p11)(p10-p01-p11) and, for
non-negative weights, at most one factor can be positive, so D <= 0 iff all three are <= 0,
iff the triangle inequality holds.  (Checked exactly below.)
