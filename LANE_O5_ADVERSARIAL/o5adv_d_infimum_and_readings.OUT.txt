(c) DOES THE LIFETIME DERIVATION ADJUDICATE BETWEEN THE TWO READINGS?
    For ANY R:  ||[H,R(t)]|| = ||[H,R]|| for all t (unitary conjugation), so
        ||R(t) - R||  <=  t * ||[H,R]||   EXACTLY, with no cluster and no projector.
    So 'epsilon is an inverse time' is equally true of READING 1.  The Ehrenfest argument
    does not choose between them.  What must be shown is that Reading 1's motion is BOUNDED
    (never accumulates) while Reading 2's is SECULAR.  The lane never tests this.  Do it:
====================================================================================================
    TOPOLOGICAL p=1e-03  eps_fixed = 5.6685e-04   Reading-1 bound says eta=0.01 by T = 1.764e+01
