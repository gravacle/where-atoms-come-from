WHAT IS EARNED vs WHAT REMAINS EXTRA DATA (the honest boundary, stated exactly):
  EARNED:  (1) unitary writer ensembles -> doubly stochastic induced kernels, for
           arbitrary ensemble weights (the algebra, not the weights, conserves measure);
           (2) measure conservation -> criticality (exact singular witness), with the
           trivial-writer/lazy share renormalizing out exactly;
           (3) measure conservation + ensemble invariance under the venue's
           edge-transitive automorphisms -> per-crossing amplitude == 1/deg identically;
           (4) the venue supplies edge-transitivity and the writer set is ONE orbit of
           carrier-lifted symmetries (no admissible-writer FACT distinguishes any link);
           (5) non-measure-conserving ensembles land computably off criticality.
  NOT EARNED (extra data, named, not smuggled): the symmetry of the ensemble WEIGHTS.
           The surface supplies a symmetric SET of writers; nothing computed here forces
           the MEASURE over that set to respect the symmetry.  A biased measure stays
           measure-conserving and critical but breaks the one-parameter mu model
           (anisotropic critical kernel) -- its venue-limit coupling class is beyond
           this lane's scope and is the named next step.
