== T1(b)  IS THERE A RECORD TO SELECT FOR? ==
  R_delta and the SBS condition are REDUNDANCY criteria: they ask how many DISJOINT
  environment fragments independently carry the system's information.
  system = link 1.  environment = links 2,3.  DISJOINT fragment count available: 2
  all fragments (incl. overlapping): 3  ->  [[1], [2], [1, 2]]

  quantum Darwinism's signature is a PLATEAU in mutual information versus fragment size,
  and R_delta counts how many independent fragments reach (1-delta) of the system entropy.
  With TWO environment links the fragment-size axis has exactly TWO points.
  A plateau cannot be exhibited on two points, and R_delta cannot exceed 2.
