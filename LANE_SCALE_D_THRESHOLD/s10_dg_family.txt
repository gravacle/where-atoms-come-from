READ, from the rows above:
  * for every ABELIAN 2-group the minimal torus gives ONE eigenspace -- conjugation is
    trivial so A = I, and every commutator is e so B = I, leaving H proportional to the
    identity.  Clause (iii) is then satisfied by any non-scalar involution and the carrier
    carries no dynamics at all: it is degenerate, not a scaling family.
  * the non-abelian case D_4 has the three-eigenvalue structure C-43 used, but its order is
    fixed; the next non-abelian 2-group of order 16 puts dim at 256 with more than 20
    minimal projections, so RecordModel.records() hits O-28.
  * hence the two stabiliser families A and B, where k scales and every clause stays
    checkable, and not D(G).  This is a measured reason, not a preference.
