=== RECORD 6: photograph (AgBr latent image) ===

=== THE TWO-SIDED WORK INEQUALITY (the real (iv)+(v)) ===
  bath writes spontaneously at rate ~ nu exp(-DeltaF/kT); an agent writes by paying W >= DeltaF
  PROTECTED: nu t_m exp(-DeltaF/kT) <= delta  <=>  DeltaF >= kT ln(nu t_m/delta)
  WRITABLE:  a driven protocol supplies DeltaF transiently and dissipates >= kT ln 2 (Landauer)
  -> durability and writability are reconciled by WORK and NONSTATIONARITY, not by symmetry.
  Crooks: P(bath does a work-W write)/P(driven reverse) = exp(-W/kT) -- the protection IS the
  fluctuation theorem, an improbability, never an impossibility.

  1 mm^2 ink mark in sunlight scatters ~1e+15 photons/s -- copies/s of the bit
