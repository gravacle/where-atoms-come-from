# Audit -- Axiomatic URFT closure

## Logical audit

- The quantified domain is independently fixed and entails `REC` before DCL or
  coverage is evaluated.
- `DCL_phys` is ontic and distinct from an evidential certificate.
- One typed witness supplies `C`, `S`, and `J`; branch or interface mixing is
  forbidden.
- `REC` remains load-bearing through the imported sealed coverage chain.
- U-DCL is not derived from record semantics and can be refuted by one exhaustive
  actual counterexample.
- FHBC support is correctly typed as
  `FHBC -> DCL_phys` and `REC & FHBC -> COV_union`.

No circularity was found in AURFT-1.

## Scope audit

- Closure is limited to the independently declared actual bona-fide
  finite-mission domain and the Coverage-U formation-contrast target.
- Natural U-DCL validity is not reported as data or empirical confirmation.
- Outcome selection, Born probabilities, collapse, A5, reset, gravity, and
  unrestricted empirical decidability are excluded.
- The alpha result is labelled a joined companion theorem with independent
  action-custody and empirical premises, not a corollary of U-DCL.

## Custody audit

The closure packet directly pins the adoption, typed U-DCL theorem, standard
causal theorem, three transitive sealed theorem/manifest pairs, FHBC theorem and
manifest, and alpha inheritance theorem and manifest. The verifier freezes the
exact dependency set, rejects malformed, duplicate, extra, or escaping paths,
checks every direct hash, and recursively checks every pinned manifest entry.
