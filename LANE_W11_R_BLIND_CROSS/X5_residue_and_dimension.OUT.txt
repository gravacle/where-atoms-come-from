X5.4  *** HOW MUCH OF THE STATE EACH CONVENTION KEEPS -- RANK OF THE STATE MAP ***
      s (2V real coordinates, one of them a global phase both maps ignore) mapped to
        (i)  pi           -- 4 real numbers   [what the CIRCUIT advance can see]
        (ii) {c^(r)}_r    -- 8P real numbers  [what the EDGE advance can see]
      Numerical Jacobian rank at a generic state, central differences h=1e-6, SVD
      cut at 1e-6 of the top singular value.
   K1          V=5  2V=10  (max possible rank after the global phase = 9)
        rank d(pi)/ds        = 3   -> the CIRCUIT advance factors through a 3-dimensional summary;
                                      it DISCARDS 6 of the 9 state dimensions.
        rank d({c^(r)})/ds   = 9   -> the EDGE advance's class-indexed data has rank 9;
                                      it DISCARDS 0.
   B0b/reg     V=9  2V=18  (max possible rank after the global phase = 17)
        rank d(pi)/ds        = 4   -> the CIRCUIT advance factors through a 4-dimensional summary;
                                      it DISCARDS 13 of the 17 state dimensions.
        rank d({c^(r)})/ds   = 10   -> the EDGE advance's class-indexed data has rank 10;
                                      it DISCARDS 7.
      READING, STATED AT THE PRECISION THE NUMBERS SUPPORT.  Both conventions' functionals
      factor through CLASS-INDEXED data: the edge advance does not abandon the class
      structure, it REFINES it from one real 4-vector to P complex ones.  But the refined
      object is far bigger: rank 9 of 9 on K1 (it determines the ready state up to the
      global phase, so the factorisation is VACUOUS there) and rank 10 of 17 on B0b (it
      still discards 7 dimensions, so on B0b the edge advance also has a genuine, larger
      class summary).  'The functional depends on the state only through a class summary'
      is therefore INFORMATIVE under the circuit advance (3 of 9, 4 of 17) and either
      vacuous or much weaker under the edge advance -- carrier-dependently so, which is
      itself a result: the SIZE of the summary is not carrier-independent.
