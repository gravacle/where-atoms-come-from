OPERATORS.  T = the edge tick (P.D).  M = the circuit operator, = T^L exactly (X6.1, in exact
Fractions).  S = diag(W^{1/L}) on the loop, the 'smeared' L-th root.  R = a diagonal L-th root of
M whose labels differ WITHIN a class.  All four satisfy A^L = M_gamma exactly.

ADVANCE.  Y(a,b) = <T_F^a s, T_C^b s>.  CIRCUIT = the line (L_F k, L_C k); EDGE = the line (n,n).

ARMS.  Three ready states with EXACTLY equal pi, differing only (i) in the within-class split of
|s_v|^2 or (ii) in phases.  equal_pi_triple() ASSERTS pi agreement to 1e-14 AND asserts the arms
are not byte-identical, and auto_moves() derives the within-class moves FROM THE CARRIER'S OWN
CLASS PARTITION so an arm cannot silently cross a class boundary.  (This guard fired on the first
run of X1 and caught a cross-class move on B0b/reg; recorded, not hidden.)  Where a stronger test
is wanted (X4) I sample 16 random states per cell at fixed pi instead of using three arms.

PRECISION.  numpy float64 (double precision) is the default everywhere.  T^L = M is checked in
exact Fractions (X6.1).  Mahler measures are Jensen-reduced quadratures reported with their grid

domination, verified on 2^22 points.  Rates are finite-N Birkhoff averages, NOT proved limits.
