========================================================================================================
O-42   THE DURABILITY-GEOMETRY EXCLUSION
========================================================================================================

1. DO THE RECORDS OF A STABILISER CARRIER GENERATE THE FULL LOGICAL ALGEBRA?
   A record is a +-1 logical operator. The logical Pauli group on k logical qubits is F_2^{2k};
   it generates the FULL algebra M_{2^k} iff its symplectic form is NON-DEGENERATE.
   carrier                n   k  logical dim   symplectic rank   non-degenerate?  generates full?
   [[4,2,2]]              4   2            4                 4              True             True
   [[6,4,2]]              6   4            8                 8              True             True
   [[8,6,2]]              8   6           12                12              True             True
   [[10,8,2]]            10   8           16                16              True             True
   [[12,10,2]]           12  10           20                20              True             True
   -> if the symplectic form on N(S)/S is non-degenerate, the records generate M_{2^k} and by
      SCHUR the commutant of that algebra is the SCALARS.

2. THE THEOREM DIRECTION, CHECKED ON ACTUAL OPERATORS (exact integer Pauli matrices).
   carrier          dim  code dim   H|code scalar?   max off-scalar  records used
   [[4,2,2]]         16         4             True        0.000e+00   all logical
   [[6,4,2]]         64        16             True        0.000e+00   all logical
   [[8,6,2]]        256        64             True        1.776e-15   all logical
   -> H is EXACTLY scalar on the protected space in every case: the same energy for every
      record configuration, so no configuration can cost more than another.

3. THE ESCAPE: CAN A CARRIER'S DURABLE RECORDS GENERATE ONLY A PROPER SUBALGEBRA?
   Clause (iv) needs a WRITER; it does NOT require the writer to be a record. So consider a
   carrier where the durable records are mutually COMMUTING (Z-type only) and the writers are
   X-type and NOT records. Then H may depend on the records and be non-scalar on the code space.

   construction                        records commute?   H|code scalar?  energy spread
   n=4 Z-type family, H from records               True            False       1.400000
      durable against this H: True    records in family: 2
   n=6 Z-type family, H from records               True            False       3.600000
      durable against this H: True    records in family: 4

   CONTROL IN THE SAME TABLE: does that H still admit a WRITER for each record (clause iv)?
   n=4: eigenspaces of the NEW H: 12   max |Tr(P_E R_0)| = 2.000e+00   clause (iv) FAILS -- these are no longer records
   n=6: eigenspaces of the NEW H: 35   max |Tr(P_E R_0)| = 3.000e+00   clause (iv) FAILS -- these are no longer records

========================================================================================================
  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  THE THEOREM DIRECTION HOLDS AND IS SCHUR. The symplectic form on N(S)/S is NON-DEGENERATE at
  every n tested, so the records generate the full logical algebra M_{2^k}, whose commutant is
  its centre -- the scalars. Checked on actual operators: H is EXACTLY scalar on the protected
  space, 0.000e+00 at n = 4 and 6 and 1.776e-15 at n = 8. EVERY RECORD CONFIGURATION HAS THE
  SAME ENERGY, so no configuration can cost more than another and no separation can matter.

  THE ESCAPE WAS ATTEMPTED AND IT FAILS ON CLAUSE (iv). Restricting to a mutually COMMUTING
  Z-type family and building H from those records does exactly what was hoped: the records stay
  durable (True), H is no longer scalar on the code space (False), and an ENERGY SPREAD APPEARS
  -- 1.400000 at n=4 and 3.600000 at n=6. Geometry is there.

  AND THE RECORDS STOP BEING RECORDS. max |Tr(P_E R_0)| goes to 2.000 and 3.000 against the
  requirement of exactly 0, so CLAUSE (iv) -- WRITABILITY -- FAILS. They are durable, they are
  bits, they are non-trivial, and they cannot be written.

  THE STATEMENT THIS EXHIBITS, PRECISELY:
      clause (iv) requires Tr(P_E R) = 0 on EVERY eigenspace of H -- the record's value must
      be BALANCED at every energy. A record that costs energy to hold one way rather than the
      other has Tr(P_E R) != 0 and is NOT WRITABLE.
      So WRITABILITY REQUIRES ENERGETIC NEUTRALITY, and GRAVITY REQUIRES ENERGETIC DISTINCTION.
      A record cannot both be writable and carry a configuration energy.

  SCOPE, STATED PLAINLY. The theorem direction is PROVED for any carrier whose records generate
  the full logical algebra -- that is Schur and it needs no large N. ONE escape route was tried
  and closed exactly. It is NOT a proof that every possible escape fails: a carrier whose
  durable records generate a proper subalgebra AND that still satisfies clause (iv) has not been
  ruled out by construction, only unfound here. That remains the open edge.
