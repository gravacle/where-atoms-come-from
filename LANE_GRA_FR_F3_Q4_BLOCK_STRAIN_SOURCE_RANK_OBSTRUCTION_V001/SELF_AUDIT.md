No.  The verifier reconstructs the character of the four-label permutation

five `S4` conjugacy classes, exactly the frozen `T2` convention.

No.  It checks both the Frobenius Gram rank of the four dyads and the separate
four-by-six contraction map.  The displayed diagonal-traceless matrices are

## 3. Can a long flip word or an order-eight loop manufacture `E`?

Not under FQ17a.  Every permitted multi-edge weight is an additive sum of the
same four dyads.  The verifier exhausts all nonnegative label multiplicities
through total degree eight, while the theorem proves the unrestricted linear
statement. Products of the four invariant contractions can generate higher

are covered separately below.

## 4. Did the proof weight loops after Feshbach?

No. It starts with the microscopic source invariants before applying the
Schur complement. Their finite-shift identity proves the invariant-contact
case; for a general `O(j^2)` contact, its zero first derivative proves the
source-off linear null before the same Schur complement. This is the
source-before-Feshbach order required by FQ's hostile correction.

## 5. Could energy-dependent folds or resolvents restore the missing rank?

Not at linear order about source off. Their first derivative is an
algebraic/analytic chain-rule functional of the microscopic first derivative,
which vanishes in an `E` direction. The exact representative
Schur-complement replay confirms the stronger invariant-contact identity.
General `O(j^2)` contacts can affect higher derivatives and are not erased.

## 6. What about BS20a's `O(j^2)` contacts?

The packet does not hide them. Contacts built from the same four microscopic
invariants inherit the two nulls at every derivative order. A general
`E`-dependent quadratic contact is treated separately: it can have a rank-two
seagull Hessian, and may be a legitimate same-parent contact if derived and
frozen prospectively, but its first derivative at `j=0` is zero. It cannot
create the missing linear operator, its commutator moments, its spectral pole,
