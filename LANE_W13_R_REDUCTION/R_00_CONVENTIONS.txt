3.  CHOICE LEDGER
================================================================================
L1  DEFINITION OF "ONE-VARIABLE REDUCTION".  Alternatives considered:
    (a) ANY function G with F(k omega) = G(k t + s0)  -- REJECTED AS VACUOUS.  k -> k omega is
        injective for irrational alpha, so such a G always exists on the countable orbit and
        the "reduction" could not have failed.  A control of exactly the kind W-08's isolation
        audit convicts.  Recorded here so it cannot be mistaken for content.
    (b) G Riemann-integrable / continuous off finitely many points  -- adequate but awkward.
    (c) CHOSEN: log|P| restricted to the CLOSURE of the orbit is the restriction of log|Q|
        for a one-variable Laurent polynomial Q, and the Birkhoff sum is then literally a
        one-variable Birkhoff sum over a circle rotation.  This is the only reading under
        which "the existing one-variable theory answers our question" could be true.
L2  WHICH BADLY-APPROXIMABLE PAIR.  Alternatives: (a) (2^{1/3}, 4^{1/3}) -- the classical
    badly-approximable pair in dimension two, and the one M1_06 used, so the comparison to
    the sealed lane is direct; (b) random algebraic pairs; (c) THE CORPUS'S OWN PUBLISHED
    GENERIC CONNECTION f=1, c=sqrt(2).  ALL THREE ARE RUN.  (c) is the one that decides
    anything about the corpus.
L3  QUADRATURE FOR m(P).  Grid evaluation of log|P| on T^2 is noise-limited near the zeros
    (brief; min|P| on 2048^2 is 2.0e-04, reproduced here).  CHOSEN: the Jensen reduction
    m(P) = (1/2pi) INT log max(|p00+p10 e^{it}|, |p01+p11 e^{it}|) dt, whose integrand is
    continuous and, at K1's pi, real-analytic except at two points.  Shown across four
    decades of node count, not at one node count.
L4  PRECISION.  Phases are reduced by the exact-integer split of M1_06 (An = floor(alpha*2^39),
    dA = alpha - An/2^39; (k*An) mod 2^39 in int64 plus k*dA in float64).  Residual phase
    error at k = 10^7 is ~2e-21.  Where an offset is smaller than float64 can hold, the
    exact Fraction offset is used with a VALIDATED local expansion of P (M1_06's method,
    revalidated here at K1's pi).  High-precision constants are computed in pure Python
    integer fixed-point (Machin pi, isqrt, argument-halving arctan) and CROSS-CHECKED against
    mpmath; mpmath is used only as a check, never as the source of a reported number.

4.  ISOLATION LEDGER — AND THE ARMS-DIFF GUARD
================================================================================
THE ONE VARIABLE THIS LANE MOVES IS THE ARITHMETIC TYPE OF THE ROTATION VECTOR omega.
Held fixed in every comparison: pi = (0,0.3,0.3,0.4); the polynomial P; the estimator
(1/N) sum_{k<=N} log|Z_k|; the N-grid; the phase-reduction code path; the local-expansion
cutoff; the seed (none is used -- every arm is deterministic).
W-08's isolation audit found that the commonest FATAL defect is ZERO variables moved.
EVERY comparison in this lane therefore prints, from the ARMS THEMSELVES AND NOT FROM THE
LEDGER, (i) the first ten orbit points of each arm, (ii) a sha256 of the full vector of
per-k values log|Z_k| for each arm, and (iii) max_k |log|Z_k|^armA - log|Z_k|^armB|.
A comparison whose arms hash equal is reported as VOID, not as a confirmation.
W-10 N-6 records an arms-diff guard that hashed INPUTS while the collapse was in the OUTPUTS.
The guard here hashes the OUTPUTS.
