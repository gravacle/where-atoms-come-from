== C4  THE THEOREM THAT KILLS THE DICHOTOMY (no computation required) ==
  For any K, any n <= K and any d > 0 there is an IRRATIONAL theta with
      #{k <= K : |exp(2pi i k theta) - 1| < d}  =  floor(K/n).
  Proof: at theta0 = 1/n the count is floor(K/n), the qualifying terms are exactly 0 and the
  non-qualifying ones are bounded below by 2|sin(pi/n)| > 0.  Both conditions are open in
  theta and finite in number, so they persist on a neighbourhood of 1/n; irrationals are
  dense in it.  QED.  Finiteness of ord(rho) is therefore neither necessary nor (C1 rows
  4001, 8000) sufficient for the effect W-07 measures at fixed K and fixed threshold.

  WHAT IS TRUE, AND IT IS THE ONLY SURVIVING FORM:
   (i)  D_k = 0 EXACTLY for some k <= K   <=>   ord(rho) is finite AND ord(rho) <= K.
        Not 'finite versus infinite' — finite AND at most K.  Rows 4001/8000 are the
        counterexamples to the version W-07 registered.
   (ii) D_k < d for some k <= K  is governed by min_{k<=K} ||k theta||, a DIOPHANTINE
        quantity that is continuous in theta and blind to rationality.
