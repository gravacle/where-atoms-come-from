== G1/G2  THE RESOLUTION: recurrence DENSITY as a function of TOLERANCE ==
  D_k = 2 amp |sin(pi k theta)|.  Two regimes, both computed on the same code path:
  amp = 0.271776443 (W-07 leg E's), theta_generic = 1/phi^2 = 0.381966011250, theta_pub = 1/4

     tolerance |   ord=4: density      count/K |  irrational: density      count/K
         1e-01 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.178e-01    1.178e-01
         1e-02 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.171e-02    1.171e-02
         1e-03 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.171e-03    1.171e-03
         1e-04 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.171e-04    1.170e-04
         1e-06 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.171e-06    1.000e-06
         1e-09 |         0.250000     0.250000 |            1.171e-09    0.000e+00

  ATTAINED (ord finite = n): density of {D_k < tol} is 1/n for EVERY tol > 0, including 0.
  APPROACHED (irrational):   density is 2 arcsin(tol/2amp)/pi ~ tol/(pi amp), -> 0 as tol -> 0.

  RESOLUTION.  The distinction is REAL but W-07 states it at the wrong place.  It is not
  'zero versus not-zero' (unobservable at any finite precision, and unobservable in W-07's
  own float64 output, which prints 2.565e-16 / 6.729e-19 / 1.349e-16 for the same exact 0).
  It is the SCALING OF RECURRENCE DENSITY WITH TOLERANCE: constant 1/n versus O(tol).
  At any fixed tolerance tol and any K < ~1/tol the two are indistinguishable — which is
  exactly W-07's regime (tol = 1e-9, K = 4000) and why its table splits 1000 / 0 so cleanly.
  So: A DISTINCTION WITH A DIFFERENCE, but the difference is Diophantine, not categorical,
  and it is measurable only by varying the tolerance — which W-07 never does.
