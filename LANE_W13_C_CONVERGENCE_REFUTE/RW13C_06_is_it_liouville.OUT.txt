(A) THE MINIMAL POLYNOMIAL OF theta_1.  EXACT INTEGERS.
   the target lane's own build_theta(L=500):  DEN = k1 * 10^L has 505 digits
   n1 has 504 digits;  M = 10^6
   theta_1 = n1/DEN + 10^-M sqrt(2)   =>   (theta_1 - n1/DEN)^2 = 2 * 10^-2M
   =>  DEN^2 * 10^2M * theta_1^2  -  2 n1 DEN 10^2M * theta_1  +  (n1^2 10^2M - 2 DEN^2) = 0
   ALL THREE COEFFICIENTS ARE INTEGERS, so theta_1 is a QUADRATIC IRRATIONAL in
   Q(sqrt 2).  Likewise theta_2 in Q(sqrt 3).  BY LAGRANGE ITS CONTINUED FRACTION
   IS EVENTUALLY PERIODIC, ITS PARTIAL QUOTIENTS ARE BOUNDED, AND ITS
   IRRATIONALITY MEASURE IS EXACTLY 2.  A LIOUVILLE NUMBER HAS UNBOUNDED MEASURE.
   ==> THE PAIR IS BADLY APPROXIMABLE.  'LIOUVILLE' IS A MISNOMER.
