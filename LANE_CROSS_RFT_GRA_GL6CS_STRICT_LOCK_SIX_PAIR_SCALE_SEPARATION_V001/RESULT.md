# GL6CS result

Every active locked ring move changes a nonzero pure-`E2` diagonal
pair-memory coordinate.  On the same finite `H6` flip component, the
complete tensor first vertex through sixth order is instead the H6 future
writer.  Their leading connected-response scales are

\[
 K_{EE}:K_{ET}:K_{TT}^{\rm contact}:K_{TT}^{\rm writer}
 \sim r^{-6}:r^0:r^2:r^6,qquad r=h/U_d.
\]

Therefore one fixed-frame finite component cannot satisfy the rotational
condition `h_E/2=h_T` in the strict-lock limit.  This locates the required
macroscopic mechanism: authenticated orientation accumulation can rotate the
dominant diagonal response into the full tensor, or a nonuniform
collective/critical limit must enhance the tensor contact by `r^-8` or the
writer susceptibility by `r^-12`; finite-coupling reorganization or another
derived same-order block are the remaining alternatives.  The theorem does
not choose among them or yet prove gravity.
