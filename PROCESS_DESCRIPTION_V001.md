## THE DYNAMICS, COMPLETELY

**CARRIER.** A finite complex: vertices, links, plaquettes. The field lives on links.
**CONSTRAINT.** A Gauss law at every vertex, fixing which configurations count as states. Imposed
exactly; it carries **no coupling constant**.
**HAMILTONIAN.** `H = -(1/g^2) sum_plaquettes W  -  g^2 sum_links Z` — a magnetic (curvature) term
and an electric (field energy) term. Optionally a `theta` term, which **does nothing at `Z_2`** (the
Wilson loop is its own adjoint) and gives a **sense of rotation** at `Z_3`.
**ENVIRONMENT.** Lindblad coupling at rate `gamma` to some observable. **Required**: a closed pure
system has `S = 0` forever and can pay for nothing, while every physical account of record formation
involves paying.
**RECORD.** A quantity conserved by `H` that the environment can select.

### THE GRAVITY LINE IS EMPTY, AND THE TEMPTING FILL IS WRONG

The Gauss constraint could be assigned to gravity: no coupling constant, universal, determines the
arena rather than acting in it, and makes the boundary flux equal the enclosed charge — which rhymes
with energy being a boundary term in general relativity. **That assignment is wrong. Gauss's law is
Maxwell's first equation. Calling it gravity counts EM twice.**

### WHAT IS ACTUALLY MISSING, STATED PRECISELY

> **THE CARRIER IS STATIC.** The graph is fixed and nothing the field does changes it.

Every gravitational functional signature on the principal's own list reduces to this — *the arena
rather than a force in it*, *backreaction*, *what happens changes what can happen next*. Here the
field lives on a stage that never moves. **A gravitational term would be the carrier responding to
the field**: links reweighting, appearing or vanishing according to what the field is doing.

### AND IT EXPLAINS WHY THE WRITE STEP FAILED (W-29)

A record must be able to change and then stay changed. **With a fixed carrier the only conserved
quantities are the ones the frozen geometry hands you, and those are exactly the ones nothing can
write to.** W-28: the record survives dissipation **iff** it commutes with `H`. W-29b: it can be
written **only** by something that does **not** commute with it. **Writable and durable are
conjugate — and they are conjugate BECAUSE THE GEOMETRY IS FROZEN.**
