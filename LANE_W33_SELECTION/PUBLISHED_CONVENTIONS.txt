LANE W33/W34 -- SELECTION. 3x3 patch, Z_2, physical dim 16, gamma=0.5. CUT = the 4 links at the
centre vertex; RIM/PERIM = the other 8. All results come from the LINDBLADIAN SPECTRUM, exactly:
the generator is vectorised to 256x256 and diagonalised. t -> infinity is taken by projecting the
initial state onto the zero modes (no integrator, no final-time choice).
w33 : (1) counts steady states -- a UNIQUE steady state forbids selection outright, so this is
      decisive before any dynamics is run. (2) reframes W-29c: a maximally mixed start carries NO
      information, so <R>=0 is the correct answer there, not a failure. Scans initial bias and reads
      the slope d<R>(inf)/d<R>(0): 0 = erases, 1 = preserves, >1 = amplifies (genuine selection).
w34 : the PREDICTABILITY SIEVE. Nominates NOTHING. The left-eigenmodes of the Liouvillian are the
      observables with definite decay rates, so the slowest ones ARE the pointer observables. Each
      is identified against the 16 magnetic operators (products of subsets of the 4 plaquettes; the
      full subset is the rim loop by discrete Stokes) and against electric (diagonal) character.
