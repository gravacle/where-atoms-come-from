Phi near 1 = recurrent (no durable record); Phi near 0 = durable.

=== THE NUMBER ===
   span(unnamed s axis, arm held fixed)   = 0.884887
   span(named L axis, INSIDE -> OUTSIDE)  = 0.000015
   R = 57694.3

R >> 1.  Crossing the entire binary -- from a 4-dimensional carrier all
the way to the infinite inductive limit, i.e. the full distance from
ARM A to ARM B -- moves the decision-relevant functional by 1.5e-05.
Moving along one coordinate the question never names, without leaving
ARM A at all, moves it by 0.885.

CONTROL, AND IT COULD HAVE FAILED: had Phi fallen monotonically with
dim at s = 0, the named axis would have been the controlling one and F1
would have returned SOUND.  It does not: Phi is flat to 6 decimals
across a 4096-fold increase in dimension at s = 0.
