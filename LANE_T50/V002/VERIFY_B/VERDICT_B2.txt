  "the point-band claim (certified reads -- state OK): beta_WU = 1 within [0.9, 1.1],

   beta_WU = 1 + d log(medf)/d log N = 1 + slope/(medf * ln10),

tolerance permits an unbounded exponent shift: at medf = 0.04 the amplification is
~11x, so a slope of 0.019 (UNDER tolerance) becomes an exponent shift of ~0.21.
