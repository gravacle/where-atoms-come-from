  WHERE THE PROOF STOPS, ASSESSED NOT TESTED:
    T3 needs the boundary map to be LINEAR over a field, which needs an ABELIAN group.
    For a non-abelian group the holonomy of a region depends on ORDERING and BASE POINT,
    so 'the product of the plaquettes' is not a linear function of the region and the
    kernel argument does not apply. WHETHER AN ANALOGUE HOLDS IS UNTESTED AND OPEN.
