## A. Causal KEEP/BREAK lineage packet

### Exact request

> Please release an event-keyed `MATCHED_ENDPOINT_QUERY_TABLE` from randomized,
> paired KEEP/BREAK routes on the same specimen.  Each route endpoint must bind
> the block, randomized route, assignment probability and order, endpoint event,
> common pre-state/reset and carryover status, independently authenticated target
> lineage \(L\) and intervention-fidelity result, separately measured conventional
> state \(C\), writer state and settling interval, common query identifier and
> parameters, query response \(Y_q\), and its joint covariance/noise identifier.
> Please include the complete route-intervention/adherence protocol, the
> route-assignment ledger, field definitions, units, event clocks, and the
> covariance/noise object keyed back to the table rows.

The minimum load-bearing row is

\[
({\rm block},{\rm route},{\rm assignment/order},{\rm endpoint\ event},
 {\rm prestate/reset/carryover},L,{\rm fidelity},C,
 {\rm writer\ state},{\rm query\ id},Y_q,{\rm covariance}),
\]

with `route` equal to `KEEP` or `BREAK`.  The block metadata must identify the
specimen, randomization pair, assignment probabilities, execution order, and
the verified common pre-state or reset.  It must either exclude cross-block
carryover/interference or supply its predeclared model.  \(L\) must contain the event-bound
groove-state/transition ancestry and its authentication method.  \(C\) must be
reported independently of \(L\), with values and units sufficient to match or
bound extension, baseline load, stress/strain, geometry or damage, support and
actuator state, energy, work, heat, acoustic loss, read disturbance, and EM
conditions.  `writer_state` must establish writer-off status and the registered
settling interval.  `query_id` must resolve to the same subthreshold future
query in both routes.

The intervention/adherence packet must show that assigned KEEP and BREAK
actually realize the two scored \(L\) states.  Promotion from a randomized
route contrast to a lineage contrast further requires the predeclared
exclusion premise that route assignment has no unmatched path to \(Y_q\)
outside \(L\) and the declared exactly matched or bounded \(C\).  A failed
fidelity or exclusion gate leaves a route-level intention-to-treat result; it
does not identify a lineage effect.

These are minimal causal fields, not optional diagnostics.  Raw high-rate force
traces, full-route images, extra queries, and additional constitutive variables
are useful optional attachments, but they cannot substitute for authenticated
\(L\), matched or bounded \(C\), paired intervention, a common later query, or
joint noise custody.

### Estimator unlocked by an admitted packet

For matched block \(b\), query \(q\), and admitted conventional-state stratum
\(c\), form

\[
\Delta_b(q;c)=Y_{b,q}^{\rm KEEP}-Y_{b,q}^{\rm BREAK}.
\]

If \(\boldsymbol\Sigma_\Delta\) is positive definite on the scored contrast
space, the covariance-weighted randomized-route intention-to-treat estimator
and standard error are

\[
\widehat\beta_{\rm ITT,route}(q;c)=
\frac{\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\Sigma_\Delta^{-1}\boldsymbol\Delta}
     {\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\Sigma_\Delta^{-1}\mathbf 1},
\qquad
{\rm SE}(\widehat\beta_{\rm ITT,route})=
\left(\mathbf 1^{\mathsf T}\boldsymbol\Sigma_\Delta^{-1}\mathbf 1\right)^{-1/2}.
\]

If the covariance is estimated rather than supplied as a known calibration
object, the release must include its estimation method, effective trial count,
and any finite-sample correction used for the displayed uncertainty.  A
singular covariance requires a separately declared estimable-subspace analysis
with satisfied deterministic null-space constraints and is not admitted to the
simple formula above.

The displayed estimator identifies the randomized route contrast.  Conditional
on intervention fidelity, common pre-state/reset or valid carryover control,
no interference, and the exclusion premise above, that route contrast equals
the frozen causal lineage target

\[
\beta_L(q;c)=
\mathbb E[Y_q\mid {\rm do}(L=L_{\rm KEEP}),C=c,Q=q]
-\mathbb E[Y_q\mid {\rm do}(L=L_{\rm BREAK}),C=c,Q=q].
\]

Exact matching admits the displayed primary estimator.  If declared components
of \(C\) are only bounded rather than matched, the result must carry a
predeclared confound-sensitivity bound; an unbounded mismatch does not admit the
causal estimate.

### Claim ceiling

This packet first identifies the paired route effect.  It can identify a
retained-lineage contribution to the specified mechanical query only when the
fidelity, reset/carryover, no-interference, and exclusion gates also pass.  It
does not by itself test URFT,
\(\gamma\), RGRL, a metric response, or gravity.  A gravity-history version
additionally requires a remote, compositionally distinct force or clock probe,
event-keyed source energy-stress and support custody throughout KEEP, BREAK,
hold, and query, and predeclared Newtonian and environmental nuisance channels.
Those fields are mandatory for a gravity claim, not optional refinements to the
mechanical estimate.

## B. Minimal HUST harmonic-remainder packet

### Exact request

> Please provide, for at least one released HUST-2018 AAF or ToS row, an
> independently reconstructible normalized physical-harmonic remainder \(r_i\)
> with released-row binding, units, sign convention, harmonic/configuration and
> coordinate-frame definitions, campaign and calculation version, and direct
> physical-source provenance.  The provenance must show that \(r_i\) was
> obtained from the omitted physical apparatus contributions rather than by
> subtracting the published processed kernel or rearranging a published value
> of \(G\).  Please expose every load-bearing measured physical input and
> calibration used to compute the scalar, with values, units, frame, uncertainty
> or resolution, campaign binding, and version, together with the reproducible
> component sum or calculation artifact.  Alternatively, provide the measured
> maps and coordinates themselves.  An executable artifact whose physical
> inputs remain hidden is not an independently owned source reconstruction.

The scalar convention must be explicit:

\[
K_i^{\rm physical}=K_i^{\rm partial}+r_i,
\]

where the existing public calibrated source model supplies
\(K_i^{\rm partial}\).  One admitted row-bound \(r_i\) is the **minimal
numerical object** sufficient for a point evaluation of that row.  Row binding,
units, sign, normalization, version, and independent provenance are custody
requirements; they do not add physical degrees of freedom.

The calculation that becomes executable is

\[
G_i^{\rm AAF}=
\frac{\alpha_i f_{m,i}}{K_i^{\rm partial}+r_i},
\qquad
G_i^{\rm ToS}=
\frac{\Delta\omega_i^2(1+c_i^{\rm anel}+c_i^{\rm mag})}
     {K_i^{\rm partial}+r_i}.
\]

Here \(\alpha_i\) is the released AAF angular-acceleration response, not the
fine-structure constant.

One remainder permits one independent source-side row evaluation using the
released response summary.  Remainders
for all released rows permit an all-row AAF/ToS consistency check.  Without a
released joint uncertainty or covariance object, the row values must not be
combined into a coverage-certified estimate.

### Optional physical-map route

Instead of exporting \(r_i\) directly, the following larger packet can make it
independently calculable: measured planar density; source-sphere density maps
and run-specific orientations; residual three-dimensional CMM coordinates,
including the two AAF shear coordinates; attachment, coating, clamp, and
ferrule CAD or mass maps; and the AAF shelf, deformation, and compensation maps,
all with units, frames, row/run binding, and versions.  These maps are optional
only as an alternative to an independently reconstructible scalar remainder;
some such provenance is required for independence.

### Larger optional raw-refit packet

A source-model-free raw-response reanalysis requires a separate, larger bundle:

- campaign-bound encoder or time-series samples;
- event, exclusion, and run-selection custody;
- correction operators, conventions, and software or table versions;
- fit design matrices; and
- empirical covariance, sufficient statistics, or the inputs needed to
  reconstruct them.

This bundle is **not required** for the first independent source-side row
evaluation.  It is required to refit the historical response and audit its raw
covariance rather than reuse the released response summaries.

### Claim ceiling

An admitted \(r_i\) closes the missing source scalar for its released row and
permits an independent **source-side** row evaluation using the authors'
published response summary, without importing CODATA \(G\).  It is not an
independent raw-response measurement of \(G\).  It does not by itself recreate the full apparatus,
refit raw response data, supply empirical covariance, or establish a
deterministic or coverage-certified interval.  Neither the scalar packet nor
the optional raw packet automatically supplies evidence for GR, RGRL, record
lineage, gravity emergence, non-Newtonian gravity, or a common metric.
