3. THE TWO THINGS THAT TABLE SHOWS
   (a) EVERY functional that MOVES under a write has orbit mean 0 (or, for the ones built
       from squares and absolute values, moves not at all).  Part 1 proved why: the only
       logical observable fixed by all of G_W is the IDENTITY, so every linear state
       functional -- diagonal or OFF-diagonal -- averages to exactly zero over the writers.
   (b) THE WRITER AMBIGUITY.  Xbar1 and Ybar1 are BOTH admissible writers of record 1 and
       both leave record 2 alone.  They send the same coherence to OPPOSITE values.  So a
       coherence is not even a well-defined function of 'record 1 was flipped'.

4. THE COMPLETE INVARIANT: which state functionals survive every write?
   over 20 random mixed code states x all 16 writers: max change in |<P>| for EVERY logical
   Pauli P = 0.00e+00; max change in purity = 2.22e-16
   and the signature |<P>| is constant on every writer orbit in 600/600 checks.
   EXACT CHARACTERISATION: conjugation by G_W = the full logical Pauli group multiplies each
   logical Pauli coefficient by +-1 and fixes the identity coefficient.  Hence THE COMPLETE
   ALGEBRA OF WRITE-INVARIANT STATE FUNCTIONALS IS THE ALGEBRA OF FUNCTIONS OF THE ABSOLUTE
   VALUES |<Pbar>|.  Every one of them is blind to WHICH configuration the state is in and
   sensitive only to HOW SHARP the record is.  Escape (2) yields exactly one kind of
   survivor: an INTEGRITY functional, never a VALUE functional.

6. CLAUSE (v) IS ITSELF A RECORD-BLINDNESS THEOREM FOR LOCAL STATE FUNCTIONALS.
   rho_A is fixed by the expectations of the Paulis supported in A.  For a stabiliser
   codeword those vanish except on the stabiliser group of the state, Stab = <S, R1, R2>.
   CLAUSE (v) SAYS EXACTLY: for a contractible A, every element of Stab supported in A
   already lies in S -- so its expectation is +1 in EVERY codeword and the signs, which are
   the record values, never appear.  THEREFORE rho_A IS IDENTICAL FOR EVERY CONFIGURATION,
   AND FOR EVERY SUPERPOSITION OF THEM.  Every functional of a contractible region is
   RECORD-BLIND, by protection itself.  Measured, exactly:
     L                region  contractible  dim Stab_A in S  dim Stab_A in <S,R>  rho_A sees s?

   AND THE C-61 CONTROL, for completeness: the zero-record carrier of C-61 has a PRODUCT
   ground state, S_A = 0 for every region.  It differs from the toric code in far more than
   record content, so it cannot by itself certify record-sensitivity.  The sharp control is
   the contractible/wrapping comparison above, which differs ONLY in whether the record is
   inside the region.

   VERDICT ON ESCAPE (2): state functionals split exactly as the configuration functionals
   did.  Responsive ones (coherences, off-diagonals, <Pbar>) have orbit mean EXACTLY zero
   AND are not even well defined given only 'record i was flipped' -- two equally admissible
   writers give opposite signs.  Invariant ones are exactly the functions of |<Pbar>|:
   record INTEGRITY, not record VALUE, and they do not respond to writing at all.
   NOT AN ESCAPE from responsiveness.  It IS the cleanest source of a non-record-blind,
   sign-definite, non-cancelling quantity -- which part 7 scores against all five standards.
