2. THE ONE CARRIER CLASS THIS LANE CANNOT REACH -- AND WHY IT DOES NOT MATTER.
   A NON-ABELIAN anyon model (D(S_3), D(D_4)) would not give a Z_2^k torsor, and D-21
   warns that record_model.commutant() silently returns a SHORT basis there (512 against
   an exact 736 on D(D_4)), so no verdict could rest on a computation of that kind here.
   BUT THE ORBIT-AVERAGING LEMMA OF PART 2 USES NO STRUCTURE AT ALL -- not abelianness,
   not transitivity, not even a group action on a product set.  Whatever the writer group
   is, its orbit average is invariant and the remainder has orbit mean zero.  A non-abelian
   carrier can only make the INVARIANT space BIGGER, i.e. add more NON-responsive
   quantities.  It cannot create a responsive non-cancelling one.  ESCAPE (5) CLOSED BY
   ARGUMENT, not reached by computation.

5. THE TRICHOTOMY, WHICH IS THIS LANE'S ACTUAL RESULT.
   Every quantity definable on a record carrier falls into exactly one of three classes:
   (Stated under the hypothesis that admissible writes form a GROUP and the configuration
    measure is the uniform orbit measure.  Part 7 shows that both fail together once the
    writer is irreversible, and that this is the only place they can fail.)
     I.   RESPONSIVE to writing.  Then it is a non-trivial part of the group algebra, its
          orbit mean is EXACTLY zero, it takes both signs equally often, and it cancels
          like k^(-1/2).  Fails standard (d).  [parts 2,3,4,5 -- exact]
     II.  INVARIANT and expressible as a formula in +-1 observables, states and baths.
          Then it has an exact twin on a carrier with ZERO records, because that carrier
          has +-1 observables too.  Record-blind.  [part 5 sec 3 -- measured to 6 digits]
     III. INVARIANT and defined only via the clauses: the record count k and the
          protection distance d.  These survive the control -- and they are a COUNT and a
          TOPOLOGICAL LENGTH, which standard (c) excludes, and they do not respond.

   A SOURCE MUST BE IN CLASS I (it responds to its own matter) AND HAVE CLASS III's
   IMMUNITY TO THE CONTROL.  THE CLASSES ARE DISJOINT.

6. WHAT WOULD FALSIFY THIS -- stated so the next lane can aim at it.
   (F1) A carrier on which some admissible write is NOT invertible by another admissible
        operation.  The orbit-averaging lemma needs the writers to form a GROUP.  If the
        admissible set were a SEMIGROUP -- irreversible writes -- orbits would not exist and
        the mean-zero conclusion would fail.  Clause (iv) as written gives a unitary, hence
        a group; a DISSIPATIVE writer would not.  TESTED IN PART 7: such a writer EXISTS on
        the torus, both its Kraus operators commute with H, and under the biased ensemble it
        produces the cancellation law fails completely (coherence 1.000000 at m=1).
   (F2) A quantity defined via the clauses that is NOT a count and NOT topological.  Class
        III has exactly two members here; a third with continuous, non-topological content
        would break the trichotomy.
   (F3) A record configuration space that is NOT a group torsor -- non-abelian anyons -- IF
        it also made the admissible set fail to be a group.  Genus and record number alone
        do not do it (section 1).
