   EXACT STRUCTURE (the symplectic form proves what the table shows):
     * the writers of record i form the COSET  Xbar_i + span{R_1..R_k}: 2^k of them, all
       equally admissible.  THE WRITER OF A RECORD IS NOT UNIQUE.
     * together they generate ALL of N(S)/S -- the full logical Pauli group, order 2^(2k).
     * the action on the 2^k configurations is the quotient by span{R_i}: Z_2^k, SIMPLY
       TRANSITIVE.  The theorem candidate's hypothesis HOLDS on the torus.
     * THE KERNEL of the configuration action is span{R_1..R_k} -- THE RECORDS THEMSELVES.
       It is non-trivial and it acts non-trivially on STATES.  That is escape (3)'s content.
