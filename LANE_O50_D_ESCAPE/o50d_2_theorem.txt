2. WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE -- AND IT NEEDS NO HYPOTHESIS AT ALL.
   LEMMA (orbit averaging).  Let ANY group G act on ANY finite configuration set X, and let
   f : X -> R.  Define (Pf)(x) = (1/|Gx|) sum_{y in Gx} f(y), the orbit average.  Then
       (1) Pf is G-INVARIANT, hence UNCHANGED by every admissible write;
       (2) f - Pf has mean EXACTLY ZERO on every orbit;
       (3) f is responsive to writing  <=>  f != Pf  <=>  f - Pf != 0.
   Therefore: THE PART OF ANY FUNCTIONAL THAT RESPONDS TO WRITING AVERAGES TO EXACTLY ZERO
   OVER THE WRITE ORBIT.  Proof: (1) and (2) are the definition of an average over a group
   orbit; (3) is immediate.  NO simple transitivity, NO independent writability, NO
   abelianness, NO structure on X is used.  EXACT.

   SIMPLE TRANSITIVITY ADDS EXACTLY ONE THING: the orbit is all of X, so Pf is a GLOBAL
   constant and the invariant space is 1-dimensional (Part 1 section 6 measured dim = 1).
   Without it the invariant space is larger -- one dimension per orbit -- and that is the
   only door the hypothesis opens.  Section 5 walks through it.
