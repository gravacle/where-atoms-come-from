# Principal clarification 001 — non-geometric origins remain admissible

**Parent decision:** `GRA-S-PRINCIPAL-JOINT-SEED-V001`  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Clarification owner:** Brian  
**Prompting question:** `Are we sure that gravity doesn't emerge from something that isn't source geometry?`

## Binding clarification

The program shall not assume that microscopic gravity, its seed, or its source is
geometric. `JOINT_SEED` may begin in non-geometric transition constraints,
information-bearing retained modes, currents, causal order, collective codes,
topological sectors, or other physically measured operators.

`Source-responsive geometry` is an infrared identification condition **if and when**
one common geometric description is earned. It is not a required microscopic
ontology and it is not the sole admissible intermediate representation.

The corrected emergence ladder is:

```text
measured non-geometric or geometric Gamma / JOINT_SEED
  -> calibrated collective response kernel and operator ancestry
  -> scale-stable universal relational behavior
  -> common metric/connection description only if multiple probes require it
  -> classical gravitational endpoint if the remaining characteristics close
```

Intermediate response may be graph-like, algebraic, topological, hydrodynamic,
constraint-based, nonlocal, or not yet geometrically representable. None of those
forms is promoted to gravity merely by being called a geometry.

## Source discipline

At the classical endpoint, total stress-energy is the operational source appearing
in Einstein gravity. That does not establish stress-energy as the fundamental
microscopic source. Effective stress-energy and effective geometry may both descend
from a deeper common substrate.

Full material, writer, bath, support, and known-force metrology remains mandatory for
three narrower reasons:

1. to prevent ordinary forces or hidden material changes from being relabeled as a
   record-derived response;
2. to determine SI normalization and conservation/exchange at each measured scale;
3. to test whether a universal effective source and coupling emerge in the infrared.

Metrology is therefore an identification and falsification requirement, not a
microscopic-source axiom.

## Operational consequences

- GF0/GF1 may not require a metric, spatial tensor, stress-energy source, Lorentz
  symmetry, spin 2, attraction, or a `1/r` law.
- Early ancestry is scored on calibrated operator identity, causal transport,
  refinement invariance, intervention response, and full known-force ownership.
- A relational graph, distance, cone, or acoustic metric is a precursor observable,
  not gravitational proof.
- A metric description becomes admissible only when different clocks, matter, light,
  and probe families exhibit one compatible causal structure and connection with
  powered alternatives excluded.
- GE2 may require the classical gravity characteristics in the applicable endpoint
  domain; it may not retroactively impose them on the record scale.
- Direct microscopic classical behavior remains an allowed special case, not a
  premise and not a preferred path.

measurement completeness, not geometric resemblance. Mechanical memory ranks highly
because B/C/D and known-force channels are measurable, not because a mechanical
