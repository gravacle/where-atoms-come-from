## Decision

The first operator-genealogy program shall use `JOINT_SEED`.

For every admitted platform, the ancestry-eligible seed is a finite, prospectively
declared typed union containing:

1. at least one nonredundant **B** transition-support, constraint, leakage, or
   probability-current direction;
2. at least one nonredundant **C** physical closure contrast, retained-record mode,
   or slow readable subspace direction; and
3. at least one nonredundant **D** physically identified current, flux, work/heat,
   matter, momentum, stress, activity, or entropy-production direction supported by
   a measured physical dilation or operational balance.

A finite predeclared dictionary of within-family and cross-family composites is
allowed. No response outcome may select an operator, support, rank, composite, or
calibration after access.

## Required invariants

- The measured per-surface process container `Gamma_rec` is required independently;
  the selection does not define it by fiat.
- B, C, and D retain their physical types and units. They are not summed into one
  arbitrary scalar.
- Every block must add at least one calibrated direction not linearly redundant with
  the other blocks. A missing mandatory block makes that platform unscoreable for
  `JOINT_SEED`; it may not be silently reduced to a primary alternative.
- Physical D operators require a measured dilation/exchange ledger. Probability
  current alone is not energy, momentum, stress, or entropy current.
- Record-sufficient support and response-complete support remain distinct.
- Scale transport uses the support-qualified calibrated map
  `Btilde_(ell'<-ell)=W_(ell')^(1/2) F_(ell'<-ell) W_ell^(+1/2)` with rank/null,
  covariance, direct/sequential composition, and composite checks.
- RELABEL, SUBDIVIDE, and DUPLICATE refinements must commute with raw and calibrated
  transport and may not manufacture physical rank, power, or ancestry.
- DEVELOPMENT selection, powered rules, and platform parameter maps freeze before
  any held-out VALIDATION response access.

## What this decision does not claim

This decision does not assert that all three blocks survive coarse-graining, that
their coefficients are universal, that a gravity-like response exists, or that any
GF/GE/UGE proof predicate has passed. It authorizes a falsifiable search in which
nature may retire individual directions, mixtures, composites, platforms, or the
entire `JOINT_SEED` hypothesis.

## Ordered exploration policy

Candidate paths shall be ranked before outcome access using only:

1. complete observable formation and physical closure;
2. independently measurable B, C, and D blocks;
3. at least three physical scales with a direct edge and two nested sequential
   routes;
4. calibrated raw-to-operator maps and uncertainty;
5. complete material, writer, bath, support, and known-force custody;
6. a joined response channel or a credible path to one;
7. public raw-data completeness and executable reconstruction;
8. availability of a genuinely disjoint, previously unused real-world validation
   dataset.

Explore all candidates that clear admission, in descending estimated probability of
closing the complete packet. Record a fail-closed reason when a path cannot proceed;
do not weaken the packet to rescue a favored surface.

## Immediate consequence

The next executable work is to rank candidate platforms, instantiate the highest
ranked complete `JOINT_SEED` packet, finish the derived-status GF contract, and only
then implement and run the public gamma-flow scorer. Reserved validation outcomes
remain closed until the platform map, powered rules, and DEVELOPMENT result are
frozen.
