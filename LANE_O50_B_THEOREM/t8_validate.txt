READ: on random carriers that are neither stabiliser codes nor quantum doubles, the writer
      group computed from the dimension table alone agrees EXACTLY with the group verified
      by explicitly constructing each unitary and testing three operator norms.  The
      reduction Part 4 rests on is therefore sound on carriers built with no structure at
      all, and the forbidden cell is empty here too.

D-22: these carriers are generated from a HAAR-random basis, so their automorphism group is
      trivial -- there is no permutation symmetry to hide behind, and equally no geometry to
      detect.  Nothing geometric is read from them; only the writer group, which needs none.

============================================================================================================================================
THE THEOREM, AS IT SURVIVES O-50-B
============================================================================================================================================

SETTING.  A carrier (H, {L_k}).  ADMISSIBLE U := unitary with [U,H] = 0 (O-4).  R_1..R_k a
family of mutually commuting RECORDS -- each satisfying clauses (i)-(iv) ON ITS OWN, with NO
even-splitting demand.  V(E,sigma) their joint eigenspaces inside energy shell E, and
d(E,sigma) = dim V(E,sigma).  G_W := the group of flip patterns eps realisable by an
admissible U with U* R_i U = (-1)^{eps_i} R_i for every i.

LEMMA W  (proved, Part 2).  eps in G_W  <=>  d(E,sigma) = d(E,eps.sigma) for all E, sigma.
          Both directions.  A negative verdict is a NON-EXISTENCE proof over all unitaries.

LEMMA B  (proved, Part 2).  If chi_S is not G_W-invariant then Tr(P_E R_S) = 0 on every
          shell: every non-invariant character has EXACTLY zero shell average.

THEOREM 1  (proved, Parts 1-2).  If the records are INDEPENDENTLY WRITABLE then G_W contains
          every coordinate flip, acts SIMPLY TRANSITIVELY on the 2^k configurations, and the
          space of G_W-invariant functionals is EXACTLY ONE-DIMENSIONAL -- the constants.

THEOREM 2 -- THE CANCELLATION LAW  (proved, Parts 1, 2, 6; NO hypothesis of independent
          writability).  Every functional f of the record configuration splits uniquely as
               f  =  Pi_{G_W} f   +   (f - Pi_{G_W} f)
          where Pi_{G_W} f is UNRESPONSIVE (no admissible family-preserving write changes it)
          and f - Pi_{G_W} f has EXACTLY ZERO shell average (it cancels).  Hence NO functional
          of the record configuration is both RESPONSIVE TO WRITING and NON-CANCELLING.

THEOREM D  (proved, Part 6).  k independently writable records force 2^k | dim E on every
          eigenspace; so k_indep <= min_E v_2(dim E), a bound read off the SPECTRUM alone.

TRADE LAW  (exact identity, Part 6).  dim(invariants) = 2^(k - rank G_W).

WHAT IS FALSE.  The candidate's literal second clause -- 'every non-constant functional is
G_W-odd, hence has mean exactly zero' -- is REFUTED by f(sigma) = #{i : sigma_i = +1}, which
is non-constant, responsive, and has mean k/2.  Non-constant does not imply odd.  The split
in Theorem 2 is the correct repair.

WHAT IS NOT PROVED, and is the honest open edge.  G_W is built from admissible unitaries that
PRESERVE the family.  Clause (iv) gives each record a writer, but that writer need not
normalise the family: at k >= 3 there are families in which NO admissible unitary flips any
one record while fixing the others (Part 7), so the flip that clause (iv) guarantees must
SCRAMBLE the rest.  After such a write there is no record configuration left for a functional
to be a functional of -- which is why Theorem 2 is stated for family-preserving writes.  A
source built on family-scrambling writes is not excluded by anything proved here.  That is
the one route this lane did not close.
