PART 6 VERDICT
========================================================================================================================================
  * The trade is an IDENTITY: dim(invariants) = 2^(k - rank G_W).  Each unit of writer-group
    rank surrendered doubles the invariant space.
  * THEOREM D bounds k_indep by min_E v_2(dim E) -- readable off the SPECTRUM.  It forces
    D(D_4) and D(Q_8) into the non-independently-writable regime (22 = 2 x 11).
  * What is bought is NOT a source.  It is a configuration-dependent quantity that no
    admissible operation PRESERVING THE FAMILY can move.  At k=2 that freezing is ABSOLUTE
    (Part 7 proves R_S then fails clause (iv) outright).  At k>=3 it is RELATIVE: the
    invariant can be flipped, but only by a write that destroys the other records' values,
    after which there is no record configuration left for it to be a functional of.
  * The dichotomy 'responsive XOR non-cancelling' needs NO hypothesis at all.  Independent
    writability only decides whether the unresponsive part is BLIND (constant) or FROZEN.
