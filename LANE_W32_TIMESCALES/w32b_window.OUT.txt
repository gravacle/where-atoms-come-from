CHECK 3 -- does the bath LOCATION change the exponent, or is it generic golden-rule counting?
  bath on CUT :  d ln(RATIO)/d ln(g2) = -0.996    d ln(Gamma)/d ln(g2) = +1.996
  bath on RIM :  d ln(RATIO)/d ln(g2) = +1.310    d ln(Gamma)/d ln(g2) = -0.310

  RATIO ~ 1/g^2 (slope -1) with Gamma ~ g^4 (slope +2) is second-order golden-rule protection:
  the bath can only reach the record THROUGH the H-mixing, so decay is one order higher than drive.
  If the two baths give DIFFERENT exponents, the exponent is a fact about where the environment
  couples. If they give the same, it is generic weak-coupling counting and must be said so.
