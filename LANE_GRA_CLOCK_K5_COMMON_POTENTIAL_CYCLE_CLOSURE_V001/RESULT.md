# Result

The official Fig4.csv values are ten separately analyzed pairwise inferred
clock-height outputs proportional to ten processed gravitational-redshift
comparisons. They were not generated algebraically from one fitted five-node
vector: the displayed $2\!-\!3\!-\!4$ triangle misses closure by
$0.09\ \mathrm{cm}$, six times the maximum $0.015\ \mathrm{cm}$ error that
independent conventional nearest rounding of its three displayed inputs to
$0.01\ \mathrm{cm}$ could create. This rounding statement does not recover or
constrain unpublished digits. Statistical independence is neither present nor
assumed—the pairs share clocks and runs, and their joint covariance was not
deposited.

For the complete graph K5, the exact oriented incidence matrix has rank 4. Its
cut space therefore has dimension 4 and its cycle space dimension 6. All 37
simple cycles were enumerated: 10 triangles, 15 quadrilaterals, and 12
pentagons. The largest absolute residual is $0.29\ \mathrm{cm}$; the largest
residual divided by the covariance-free marginal envelope
$\sum_e|c_e|\sigma_e$ is $0.32926829268292684$.

The exact simultaneous marginal-box fit gives

\[
 \rho_*=\min_z\max_e |y_e-(Bz)_e|/\sigma_e
 ={27\over82}=0.32926829268292684<1.
\]

Thus one five-node scalar height representation intersects all ten reported
marginal $1\sigma$ intervals. Under the source's assumed common-$g$ GR mapping
this is proportional to a relative-potential representation, but that physical
interpretation is not independently established here. The result is an exact
geometric compatibility statement, not a chi-square, $p$-value, confidence
region, or joint-coverage statement.

The result is only a necessary downstream common-node-scalar check. The source
already assumes GR and a common measured $g$ when converting redshifts to
heights, so this does not independently test GR, gravity, a common metric, or
$g$. It supplies no record-lineage, beta_TM, Gravity Formation Theory, or
gravity-emergence evidence.
