  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  THE LEADING TWO-BODY RECORD QUANTITY IS TWO-SIGNED AND IT CANCELS MORE AS RECORDS ARE ADDED.
  The integer intersection numbers span both signs at every carrier size, and the fraction that
  survives summation FALLS: |sum| / sum|.| = 0.3333, 0.2222, 0.1698 at k = 2, 4, 6 records.

  A QUANTITY THAT CANCELS CANNOT DOMINATE AT 1e20 CONSTITUENTS, whatever its form and however
  it scales. This is the structural reason gravity, not electromagnetism, is what a planet
  exerts: charge comes in two signs and neutralises in bulk, mass has one sign and adds. The
  pairing behaves like the first kind.

  AND THE MEASUREMENT HAD NEVER BEEN POSSIBLE BEFORE. Every measurement of the pairing in this
  program was taken over F_2, where the values are {0,1} and THE QUESTION OF SIGN CANNOT EVEN BE
  POSED. The signed object is the INTEGER intersection number, and it had not been computed.

  THIS REFINES C-35 RATHER THAN OVERTURNING IT. C-35 ruled the pairing out as a density because
  it is topological -- true, and it is why the pairing does not grow with the extent of a record
  carrier. But the integer values' RANGE does widen with k, from [-2,2] to [-8,8]. What kills it
  is not failure to grow, it is CANCELLATION.

  THE DISCRIMINATOR THIS GIVES: the test for a gravity-role quantity is not 'does it grow' but
  'does it FAIL TO CANCEL'. A quantity with |sum| / sum|.| = 1 accumulates without bound however
  small each term is, which is exactly how a 1e-36 coupling wins at 1e20 constituents.
