IS THE CROWDING SUPPRESSION SIGN-DEFINITE?
========================================================================================================
  signed difference  d = chi(spread) - chi(crowded).  d > 0 means sharing the site COST the
  record; d < 0 would mean sharing HELPED it. Both are a priori possible.

     n   k    lam   pairs  d > 0 (cost)  d < 0 (helped)      min d      max d  |sum|/sum|d|
     4   2   0.40       9             9               0   0.058002   0.151767        1.0000
     4   2   0.80       9             9               0   0.151118   0.394621        1.0000
     4   2   1.20       9             9               0   0.228130   0.335398        1.0000
     6   4   0.40      21            21               0   0.058002   0.151767        1.0000
     6   4   0.80      21            21               0   0.151118   0.394621        1.0000
     6   4   1.20      21            21               0   0.228130   0.335398        1.0000

  A ratio |sum d| / sum|d| of 1.0000 means EVERY term has the same sign -- it ACCUMULATES.
  A ratio well below 1 means the terms CANCEL, as the integer intersection pairing does
  (0.3333, 0.2222, 0.1698 at k = 2, 4, 6 -- cancelling MORE as records are added, which is
  screening).
