  1. THE INSTRUMENT AGREES WITH AN EXHAUSTIVE PAULI SEARCH.  Section 4.0: across 4..8 sites,
     both coupling sets, every block of every size and every record inside it -- 315 cases per coupling set, 630 in all --
     the exact criterion and the exhaustive Pauli search return the SAME count, and the
     'Pauli says yes, exact says no' column is 0 everywhere.  Note the counts are also equal,
     so on this carrier the extra freedom of non-Pauli unitaries buys nothing.

  2. ON THE OPEN CHAIN THE RECORD IS PROTECTED AGAINST EVERY REGION SMALLER THAN THE WHOLE
     CARRIER, AND AGAINST NOTHING ELSE.  Section 4.1: for n = 3..14 and both coupling sets,
     every cell at w < n is 0 and the cell at w = n is n.  The smallest region size whose
     admissible operations can flip a record is EXACTLY n, at every n.  This covers ALL
     unitaries supported on the region, not only Paulis.

  3. SO CLAUSE (v) FAILS ON THE OPEN CHAIN.  In one dimension a contiguous block of sites is
     contractible, and the whole chain IS a contiguous block.  Section 4.3 exhibits the
     operator: X^(x)n, ||[U,H]|| = 0, U-dag Z_0 U = -Z_0 exactly, dE = +0.000000, and it flips
     all n records at once.  A single contractible region -- the entire carrier -- writes the
     record for free.  THEREFORE THE Z_i ON THE OPEN CHAIN ARE NOT RECORDS IN THE PROGRAM'S
     FULL FIVE-CLAUSE SENSE, and O-47, and Parts 1-3 above, are scoped to clauses (i)-(iv).

  4. ON THE RING, UNDER THE ARC CONVENTION, CLAUSE (v) HOLDS.  If a contractible region is a
     PROPER ARC -- the reading that matches T-11's 'a forest, i.e. no cycle' -- then no
     contractible region flips a record at any w up to n-1, for n = 3..14 and both coupling
     sets, and the flipping operator at w = n is the whole ring, which wraps and is not a
     contractible region at all.  The carrier's answer to clause (v) therefore turns entirely
     on a convention about what 'contractible' means on a 1D lattice.  That is CARRIER DATA,
     exactly as the model warns, and it is a decision, not a measurement.

  5. THE CONTROLS REGISTER.  Section 4.2: cutting the chain by setting one coupling to zero
     drops the smallest flipping region from n to the size of the SMALLER COMPONENT -- 3 at
     n = 8, 4 at n = 10, 5 at n = 12.  With H = 0 it drops to 1.  With only the left half
     coupled it is 1, on the uncoupled sites.  So the zeros in 4.1 are measurements.

  6. WHAT PROTECTION ACTUALLY COSTS HERE.  The protection distance equals the writer weight,
     n, which Part 1 measured independently.  They are the same number for the same reason:
     the only admissible operators that flip the record have full support.  Protection and
     non-locality of the writer are not two properties -- they are one.
