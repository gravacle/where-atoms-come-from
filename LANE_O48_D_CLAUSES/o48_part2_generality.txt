  1. IT IS NOT SPECIAL TO 1D AND NOT SPECIAL TO TWO-BODY ZZ.  The 2D ZZ lattice reproduces the
     chain exactly: N records, all four clauses, a free admissible writer with dE = 0, and
     every pair correlator carrying energy.  The three-body ZZZ chain also works: n records,
     free writers, pair correlators carrying energy.  Random commuting-Pauli Hamiltonians work
     at every seed tried -- 24, 48 and 96 records at n = 4, 5, 6, free writer in every case.

  2. AND THE WRITER'S WEIGHT IS NOT A CONSTANT OF THE MECHANISM.  On the 2D ZZ lattice it is
     exactly N, the whole lattice.  On the ZZZ chain it is about 2n/3, a period-3 pattern, and
     it flips only the records the pattern touches -- not all of them.  On the DISCONNECTED
     control it drops to 3, the size of one component.  On random commuting-Pauli carriers it
     is as low as 1.  So the free writer is generic; its LOCALITY is not.  What fixes the
     weight is the carrier's connectivity, not the mechanism.

  3. THE MECHANISM DOES NOT REQUIRE COMMUTING TERMS.  The XY chain, whose ZZ and XX terms
     ANTICOMMUTE across neighbouring bonds, has NO record at n = 4 and n = 6 -- and THREE
     records at n = 5, namely Z^(x)5, X^(x)5 and Y^(x)5, each with a free admissible writer
     of weight 5 and dE = 0.  At odd n those two parity operators ANTICOMMUTE WITH EACH OTHER
     while both commuting with H, which is exactly the structure the mechanism needs, and it
     forces every eigenspace to be 2-fold degenerate (multiplicities are all 2 at n = 5, all
     1 at n = 4 and n = 6).  A non-commuting Hamiltonian can carry records.

  4. AND COMMUTING TERMS ARE NOT ENOUGH.  C2, the dimerised XX+ZZ carrier, has commuting terms
     and NO records: at n = 6 fifty-six operators pass clauses (i),(ii),(iii) and every one of
     them FAILS clause (iv).  C3, which promotes the free writer X^(x)n into H as a term, also
     has commuting terms and kills every record: 62 pass (i)-(iii) at n = 6, none passes (iv).
     Promoting the symmetry into the Hamiltonian destroys the very records it was writing.

  5. THE PATTERN ACROSS ALL SEVEN CARRIERS.  Records exist exactly where H has a PAIR OF
     ANTICOMMUTING SYMMETRIES, one of which is the record.  Where that pair exists the writer
     is automatically free.  Where it does not -- C2, C3, the XY chain at even n -- there is no
     record at all, and clause (iv) is what fails.  Part 3 tests that statement directly.
