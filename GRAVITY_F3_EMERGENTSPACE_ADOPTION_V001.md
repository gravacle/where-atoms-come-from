## 3. E-EMERGENTSPACE recovery obligations

Adoption of an evasion class does not evade the recovery burden. A successful
construction must derive, from the same parent:

1. **Proto-causal input:** one bounded or locally finite incidence/composition
   structure with complete physical ports and no hidden background metric.
2. **Causal map:** an explicit map from microscopic incidence and response to
   the emergent causal ordering and continuum domains.
3. **Locality window:** a controlled infrared regime in which effective
   observables satisfy causal factorization or an explicitly bounded
   nonlocal correction.
4. **No acausal signalling:** operational proof that allowed microscopic
   interventions cannot signal outside the recovered physical cone.
5. **Continuum and dimension:** derivation of a common \(3+1\)-dimensional
   Lorentzian regime rather than insertion of its dimension or signature.
6. **Protected tensor pole:** one positive massless helicity-\(\pm2\) pole,
   with no unsuppressed scalar, vector, ghost, or continuous-spin sector.
7. **Common cone:** matter, light, clocks, the tensor mode, and registered
   probes share the same infrared cone within derived corrections.
8. **Universal coupling:** the candidate tensor couples to complete conserved
   stress, including its own interaction and self-stress, with no source-class
   refit.
9. **Nonlinear gauge closure:** the inverse kernel, source vertex, cubic and
   higher vertices satisfy one emergent diffeomorphism Ward/BRST hierarchy.
10. **Complex response:** the same parent supplies the complete complex CTP
    functional or every equivalent complex/Keldysh/contact derivative needed
    for connected and 1PI reconstruction. Gamma alone is insufficient.
11. **Operational energy ownership:** writer, reservoir, record, interaction,
    support, reader, boundary, and active gain are owned exactly once even
    though a local covariant microscopic stress tensor is not assumed.
12. **SI embedding:** microscopic clock, length, energy, and source
    calibrations determine the infrared residue and \(G_*\) without inserting
    accepted \(G\).
13. **Stability and cutoff:** bounded-below or fully powered microscopic
    dynamics, positive spectral weight, retarded analyticity, controlled
    strong-coupling scale, and a lawful domain of the effective theory.
14. **Same-parent record ancestry:** the record/order coordinate, tensor
    response, scale map, Ward genealogy, and validation predictions belong to
    one parent; results from unrelated systems may not be assembled into proof.

## 4. Choices deliberately not made

### Historical ancestry

AY-C3 is not settled by this adoption. Non-temporal micro-to-IR scale emergence
is not the same assertion as a local repeatable historical switch or a one-time
global cosmological phase transition.

The program does **not** presently assert:

- a dated gravity-formation event;
- a gravity-off epoch;
- local creation of the ordinary graviton at each record surface;
- an operational historical coordinate \(X_{\rm hist}\); or
- a cosmological relic of a transition.

### Relic marker

AY-C6 is not triggered because the global-history branch is withheld. If a
future action requires that branch, the action must emit a prospectively frozen
marker family before observational anomalies are inspected.

### Write-time exchange

AY-C4 remains action-specific. The eventual microscopic action must own every
writer/reservoir/record exchange and interaction current. Whether a reduced
writer-off effective sector is separately conserved is to be derived from that
action, not imposed in advance.

### Mixed families

No F1+F2+F3 mixed extension is adopted. Source-side and modulation-side controls
may be calculated, but evidence cannot be borrowed across families without one
separately authorized action and full-rank causal design.

## 5. Decisive falsifiers and refusals

The adopted route fails for a proposed microscopic parent if any required item
below survives the controlled infrared limit:

1. the microscopic construction secretly assumes the metric, Einstein-Hilbert
   action, diffeomorphism redundancy, accepted \(G\), or a background
   \(3+1\)-dimensional Lorentzian manifold it claims to derive;
2. every Weinberg--Witten premise is restored microscopically without a lawful
   evasion;
3. no explicit incidence-to-causal/locality map exists;
4. the candidate pole is absent, negative, massive without a controlled limit,
   purely Gaussian, or accompanied by extra unsuppressed modes;
5. the recovered tensor and matter/light/clock cones disagree;
6. source, cubic, self, static, and radiative vertices fail one no-refit Ward
   genealogy;
7. the complete complex response or graded BRST packet is missing;
8. the SI residue requires measured Newton \(G\) as a calibration input;
9. writer, reservoir, boundary, support, interaction, or active energy is
   omitted or double counted;
10. a local analogue, endpoint GR calculation, tensor-shaped correlator, or
    finite-data pole fit is promoted to gravity origin; or
11. a historical global transition or relic is inferred without a separately
    adopted C3/C6 packet.

Failure of one candidate action falsifies that action, not every possible
F3/E-EMERGENTSPACE realization.
