WHAT THAT MEANS ROW BY ROW.
  A row with NO unimodular root:  log|Q| is CONTINUOUS on the circle H, the orbit
  z^k is equidistributed there by Weyl (the rotation number is irrational), so the
  Birkhoff average converges to m(Q) WITH NO DIOPHANTINE HYPOTHESIS.  The row is a
  theorem.
  A row WITH a unimodular root: log|Q| has a log singularity on H and the Birkhoff
  average needs an inhomogeneous Diophantine condition on the rotation number 
  relative to that root.  The row is NOT a theorem; it is a numerical observation.
