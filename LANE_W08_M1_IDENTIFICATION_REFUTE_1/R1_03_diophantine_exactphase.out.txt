  The two schemes agree to 1.3e-13 at N = 1e7.  The lane's reported value -0.767507816121
  is NOT a precision artefact at the size of its own residual deviation (+6.4e-08):
  the true-irrational orbit gives -0.767507816121, deviation +6.424e-08.
  SELF-FLAG 4 IS THEREFORE RESOLVED IN THE LANE'S FAVOUR ON THE NUMBER, AND AGAINST IT
  ON THE REASON GIVEN: the phase representation is NOT good to 1e-17 against 2^(1/3);
  it happens not to matter at this N because the orbit stays 2.0e-04 away from Z(P).
