# Self-audit: FRSEWO

## 1. Did the lane silently identify FZ's embedding contraction with the native divergence?

No.  Every positive mismatch statement is conditional on the supplied
embedding contraction or a later native divergence proved proportional or
equivalent on the scored source.  GD's auxiliary L/R incidence is tested
separately and is not called diamond space.

## 2. Is the direct GD momentum density really diagonal?

Yes.  On the encoded code,
`P_L=pI-(hbar kappa/2)Z` and
`P_R=pI+(hbar kappa/2)Z`.  Arbitrary local or Fourier weights preserve the
`I,Z_e` span.  The verifier checks nontrivial weights on all sixteen q4
configurations and the six-state ice subset.

## 3. Was the off-diagonal recoil current ignored?

No.  The verifier reconstructs it, checks `dot P_L+J=0` and the opposite
reservoir balance, and confirms that mistyping it as a density could produce a
diagonal next commutator.  The theorem rejects that evasion by operator type:
GD derives it as flux, and a one-link flux has zero direct ice projection.

## 4. Does the proof assume a special H6 matrix?

No.  `diag([H,D])=0` for every finite `H` and configuration-diagonal `D`.
The exact FY/FZ dependencies are needed to establish that the spatial side
has a nonzero diagonal, not for the commutator identity.

## 5. Is the FZ diagonal witness numerical?

No.  The verifier reconstructs `Phi_240`, reduces the exact rational
polynomial, and multiplies it by the exact complete coefficient
`2415673/113400`.

## 6. Could the ring source cancel it?

No in the configuration diagonal.  FY fixes the ring source as strictly off
diagonal.  This says nothing about cancellation after changing the complete
source parent.

## 7. Could contacts repair the mismatch?

Not the source-off operator identity.  An `O(j^2)` term has zero first
spatial derivative at `j=0`; a mixed `h_0j` term contributes neither direct
operator when both sources vanish.  Their nonzero Hessians belong to the
response contact identity and remain required in the positive construction.

## 8. Could a boundary repair it?

An active boundary with the needed operator could change the balance, but GD's
hold is port-off.  Activating one changes the parent and requires complete
charge, momentum, work, placement, and source custody.  The theorem does not
exclude it.

## 9. Was observable Feshbach dressing discarded?

It is explicitly open.  The lane tests the direct projected GD density only.
A complete pre-projection `h_0i` source can generate additional effective
operators through virtual histories; that is the next positive calculation.

## 10. Does this refute F3 or gravity emergence?

No.  It eliminates one incomplete flip-recoil-only join.  Pair-field/support
momentum, the native divergence, the complete source family, continuum
control, gravity, and `G` remain open.

## 11. Does the obstruction prove pair-field/support momentum is necessary?

No.  It makes that the recommended positive construction because the frozen FU
pair interaction needs a physical momentum owner in a complete source family.
The theorem itself excludes only the bare, directly projected,
scalar-weighted GD `P_L/P_R` assignment under the frozen FY source and FZ
supplied embedding contraction.  Dynamical position-weighted localization,
interaction terms in `T^{0j}`, source-before-Feshbach dressing, a modified
spatial source, native `Delta_m`, and active boundaries are untested.
