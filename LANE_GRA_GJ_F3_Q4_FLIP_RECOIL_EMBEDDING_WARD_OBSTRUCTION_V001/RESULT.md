# Result: bare direct GD `P_L/P_R` density does not close the frozen supplied-embedding Ward test

GD's encoded half-kick states is an
exact linear combination of `I` and the link `Z_e` operators.  It is therefore
configuration diagonal for every kick, reservoir phase, and local/Fourier
weight.  Its commutator with the FY H6 Hamiltonian has zero configuration
diagonal.

FZ's exact `m=1` supplied-embedding contraction has a nonzero diagonal
longitudinal entry.  At `x=2/5`, its complete H2/H4/H6 coefficient is

\[
 \rho f_E=\frac{2415673}{113400}\ne0,
\]

multiplying a nonzero polynomial in `Q(zeta_240)`.  The independent ring
source is off diagonal.  Consequently the direct GD density cannot close

\[
 \frac{i}{\hbar}[H_6,T^{0j}_m]+(\Delta_m)_iT^{ij}_m=0
\]

when `Delta_m` is the supplied embedding contraction, or a native divergence
proved equivalent to it on this source.

is the flux in the auxiliary link-support/reservoir balance, not a momentum
density; its direct ice projection vanishes because one link flip exits the
degree-two fiber.  A nonzero Feshbach-dressed effective momentum density could
still arise from a complete `h_0i` source calculation and remains open.

The result is not an unconditional no-go for every GD completion.  GD does
not define the native diamond-space divergence or its reservoir placement.
An active boundary, pair-field/support momentum, or a source-complete
Feshbach reduction changes the parent and must be calculated.  `O(j^2)` and

first-source, source-off operator mismatch.
