    THE OPERATIVE VARIABLE, NAMED.  Not 'the number of classes'.  It is whether the
    unit-modulus coefficients have FREE RELATIVE PHASES.  With three coefficients
    {uv, u, v} the two independent ratios are uv/u = v and uv/v = u -- both free, so the
    coefficient triple sweeps ALL of T^3 modulo global rotation and 'zero-sum of three
    vectors with prescribed lengths' is unobstructed: triangle inequality.  With four
    coefficients {1, u, v, uv} the three ratios from the base point 1 are u, v, uv -- and
    the THIRD IS THE PRODUCT OF THE FIRST TWO.  One relative phase is spent.  That single
    spent phase is the entire difference, and it is exactly what p00 = 0 hides on K1.
