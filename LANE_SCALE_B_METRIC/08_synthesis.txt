  READ: the operational relation between two records is fixed by the pair's ANTICOMMUTATION
  BIT and the two records' SUPPORT PROFILES.  Those are exactly the ingredients of relation
  (a) and relation (d).  The operational measurement adds no independent relational content;
  it re-weighs the same two ingredients.

  RELATION (b1), THE DRIVE-READ MATRIX (step 5): EXACTLY DIAGONAL at every n from 4 to 20 and
  at lam = 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 -- max |off-diagonal| between 3e-16 and 7e-15, i.e. machine zero.
  Its distance transform d = sqrt(Mmax - M_sym) is therefore a regular simplex on 2k points,
  d90 = 2k - 1, the largest dimension the point count allows.  The D-15 positive control in
  5C shows the SAME instrument returning 0.026 / 0.108 / 0.257 as the record overlap is
  dialled to 0.25 / 0.50 / 0.75, so the zero is a real zero and not a dead instrument.

  READ, filled from the numbers above and never in advance:
  Under EVERY relation definition tried, and on the basis-free class object as well, the
  intrinsic dimension GROWS LINEARLY with the number of records.  The only two columns that
  saturate -- (d)GS and (e)GS -- lose their saturation the moment the arbitrary Gram-Schmidt
  basis is replaced by another equally valid basis of records on the SAME carrier.
