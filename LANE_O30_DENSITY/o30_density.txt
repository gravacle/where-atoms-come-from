  READ
  Every abelian control is identically zero at every k: no amount of enclosed record density
  produces any transport at all. The density question does not even arise there.
  On the non-abelian carriers transport is non-zero, but T(k) does NOT grow monotonically with
  the number of enclosed records -- it saturates, or oscillates with the parity of k. So the
  signature H-9 names, 'stronger with enclosed record-density', is NOT PRESENT as a monotone
  law in the counting of same-kind records on any finite group tested.
  A count of records is the wrong density variable. What varies monotonically in a group is not
  how MANY elements are multiplied but WHICH class the product lands in.
