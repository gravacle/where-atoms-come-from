# Hostile audit of same-sector alpha inheritance

## Verdict

The unqualified sentence “a selected alpha forbids every record with a different
alpha” is false.  The exact result is narrower and stronger where it applies:

> After complete context alignment, a physically inequivalent base coupling
> cannot be assigned to an ordinary subsystem of one fixed single-\(U(1)\)
> parent action.  If it remains inequivalent, at least one sector, action,
> background, phase, interface, or comparison premise has changed.

An observation of an aligned inequivalent value would falsify the claimed parent
or its completeness assumptions.  It would not be dismissed as metaphysically
impossible.

## Hostile cases

| Apparent counterexample | Disposition |
|---|---|
| \(\alpha(0)\) differs from a value quoted at an electroweak scale | Running along one common trajectory; compare at one scale and scheme. |
| Two renormalization schemes quote different coordinates | Apply the fixed scheme map or compare invariant amplitudes. |
| A dielectric or crystal has a different effective coupling | Screening, permittivity, band velocity, and quasiparticle response belong to the medium, not a second vacuum base alpha. |
| Species \(i\) has charge \(n_iq_0\) | Its vertex is \(n_ie\); divide out the fixed representation factor before comparing the base coupling. |
| New species changes running | It changes the common spectrum and thresholds.  It does not give only one record an independent RG boundary. |
| Dark \(U(1)\) or kinetic mixing | The coupling is a matrix with another connection; SAI2 is deliberately violated. |
| \(Z_F=f(\phi,x)\) varies | The background/modulus and its gradients are additional physical structure; SAI1/SAI3 are violated. |
| Opposite sides of a domain wall have different alpha | They are distinct phases joined by an interface, not two ordinary subsystems of one homogeneous phase. |
| Electric-magnetic dual coordinates give reciprocal-looking values | Quotient exact dual descriptions before declaring a physical difference. |
| A noisy finite record estimates a shifted value | A fitted estimator is not the exact action coefficient; compare a predeclared uncertainty region after nuisance control. |
| A field redefinition rescales \(q_0\) | The invariant ratio \(q_0^2/Z_F\) is unchanged. |
| Ordinary charge superselection | It labels total charge/representation, not a different alpha-valued action sector. |

## Relation to RFT

The existing nonselection theorem already did essential work.  It established
that the portable record criteria cannot, without new completeness/dynamical
premises, determine one electromagnetic coupling across all admitted models.
SAI supplies the complementary within-parent theorem.  It does not retract or
evade nonselection.

The resulting architecture is therefore:

\[
 \text{parent physics realizes a sector}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{same-sector records inherit its alpha}
 \longrightarrow
 \text{records can measure and redundantly broadcast that standard}.
\]

Whether record dynamics feeds back strongly enough to stabilize or select the
parent phase is a separate mechanism theorem and remains open.

## Record-world versus complete-universe audit

The cavity result earns the non-singleton set
\({\cal A}_{\rm RF}^{\rm EM,toy}\): its canonically normalized coupling lies on
the sole write path, EM ablation kills the nominated record, the apparatus is
fixed across parents, and each episode independently satisfies `REC` and
`FCLPD_W`.  This is sufficient to refute numerical selection by present
finite-mission recordhood.

It does not earn a non-singleton \({\cal A}_{\rm RF}^{\rm full}\).  The current,
detector, cavity closure, and complete toy census are supplied; stable dynamical
charged matter, atomic and chemical structure, RG/threshold closure, and gravity
are absent.  Calling the construction another complete universe is forbidden.

The full-universe fork has two lawful outcomes: a proved non-singleton makes
alpha contingent, while an exhaustive singleton theorem would select the
observed alpha.  Repeated construction failure is not an exhaustive no-go.

## Two proof standards

The hostile audit must not use possible alternative universes to deny an
actual-world theorem.  AWAI is a valid empirical-premise-plus-dynamics proof:
measurement fixes the actual parent coefficient and SAI propagates it to every
same-sector record.  Counterfactual alphas are irrelevant to that quantifier.

They matter only to the stronger full-universe cardinality question.  Since bare
RFT and the active-EM finite record construction admit more than one alpha-valued
parent model, current RFT does not calculate the observed numeral without the
actual-world empirical premise or an added parent selector.  Such a deeper
selector is possible, not presumed to exist.

## Wall-model audit

The phase-lock construction is retained only as an existence proof.  Its exact
claims are homogeneous vacua, common canonical coupling within each vacuum, the
planar kink, and planar wall/slab tension.  It does not claim a universal
surface-area lower bound for arbitrary curved quantum inclusions.  It also does
not prove that a foreign-sector object disintegrates, that tunneling is absent in
finite volume, or that the toy parent describes nature.
