# GL6AT strict evidence ladder

The levels below are ordered by what is actually established, not by the
terminology used in any paper.

## Level A — exact, admitted at the GL6AO point

1. The `A3` parent/shared-child incidence is graph-isomorphic to the diamond
   net under the tetrahedral embedding in (AT03).
2. Degree two is exactly the two-dimers-per-site / ice constraint.
3. The projected six-`X` toggle is exactly the alternating-hexagon ring
   operator and annihilates nonalternating states.
4. After the common scalar is removed,
   `g=(63/8)h^6/U_d^5>0` and the flippability potential is exactly zero at
   order six.
5. Therefore the displayed GL6AO order-six Hamiltonian is exactly the
   published fully-packed-loop quantum-ice point `v/g=0`.

This level is an operator isomorphism.  It does not identify the full
finite-`h/U_d` F3 Hamiltonian with the comparison model.

## Level B — exact, but at the wrong parameter

At `v/g=1`, not zero, the RK Hamiltonian has exact equal-weight ground states
within connected sectors.  Exact RK degeneracies and the special quadratic
long-wavelength theory are useful checks on the comparison family, but cannot
be imported as exact properties of GL6AO.

## Level C — finite numerical evidence at `v/g=0`

Shannon et al.'s zero-temperature GFMC and small exact diagonalization show
flux-sector finite-size scaling consistent with a three-dimensional `U(1)`
liquid, with zero potential well away from the inferred ordered transition.
This is strong phase evidence subject to finite-size, flux-sector,
cluster-geometry, population/statistical, and ergodicity systematics.  It is
not a rigorous thermodynamic theorem.

## Level D — effective field theory calibrated by equal-time QMC

Benton et al.'s Gaussian lattice theory, quantitatively fitted to equal-time
QMC and finite-size ground-state energies, gives a linear collective branch
and `c=(0.6+-0.1)g a_0/hbar` at zero potential.  It also predicts a one-photon
delta function in the link/spin structure factor whose residue vanishes as
`omega(k)`.

The dispersion and real-frequency atom are effective-theory outputs.  The
QMC comparison does not directly reconstruct the microscopic dynamical
spectral function.

## Level E — approximate composite-operator mechanism

Fu et al.'s XXZ/slave-particle Raman calculation maps a local spin pair to an
electric-field bilinear and predicts a zero-total-momentum two-photon
continuum in the active cubic `T2g` channel.  Its prose and displayed
low-energy integral do not give the same infrared power under direct
three-dimensional counting, so no `Omega` exponent is admitted here.  The
calculation shows a plausible pair/tensor overlap mechanism only.

It is not the sealed two-dimensional local pair `E` channel, it is not an
exact projected-ring calculation, and it supplies no exact nonzero residue or
threshold for `N_E`.

## Level F — expressly unresolved or forbidden

The packet does not claim any of the following:

- a mathematically rigorous `v/g=0` liquid, gap, gaplessness, or pole theorem;
- an exact local pair-`E` spectral overlap or nonvanishing residue;
- spectra of degree-violating charges inside the projected locked Hilbert
  space;
- survival of the comparison phase after uncomputed higher F3 orders;
- a physical momentum, length, or speed from an uncalibrated `A3` character;
- a physical cone, Lorentz invariance, a graviton, Ricci/Einstein dynamics,
  gravity, or Newton's constant `G`.
