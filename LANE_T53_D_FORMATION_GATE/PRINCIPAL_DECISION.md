# Principal decision: the constitutive record-formation predicate

**Decision date:** 2026-08-22

## Decision

The principal selected Option A and wrote:

> “I select Option A but of course also recognize autonomous formation as under this umbrealla.”

The selected constitutive rule is therefore:

> A record forms when a physical process causally creates previously absent information about an earlier event in a definite physical state; that information persists after the formation interaction ceases under common hold or storage conditions for a prospectively declared mission time; and the information can be read without recreating the formation interaction.

Engineered or externally driven formation and autonomous or endogenous formation are instances of this same rule. They are not different standards of formation. For an autonomous case, the experiment must identify an event-bounded endogenous formation interaction and distinguish it from the subsequent writer-off storage or hold dynamics. Calling a process autonomous does not waive causal controls, the interaction boundary, the mission time, or any other gate.

“Earlier event” is causal, not an artificial demand that the event predate the entire formation interval. On a naturally incorporating surface, forcing may occur while the target is forming. The event signal must precede the target update attributed to it, and the event or independently measured forcing must precede physical closure and readout. A controllable coupling uses randomized intervention. An uncontrollable natural event uses a prospective natural contrast with independently measured forcing, matched negative time or surface controls, blind response analysis, and explicit alternative-cause falsifiers.

Prospective means frozen before outcome access, not necessarily generated after registration. A response-blind archival holdout is admissible when the full protocol and analysis are frozen before numerical access or unblinding and custody establishes the blind. For a unique natural event, independent reproduction may newly measure independent physical specimens or archives carrying that event; the event itself need not be recreated.

## Provenance and copying

The rule applies to the target physical state. A causal copy can therefore form a new, derivative record when the target previously lacked information about the earlier event and the copy interaction creates it. Such a result must preserve a machine-readable lineage edge to the parent record and cannot be reported as primary formation. Primary formation is reserved for a target whose formation interaction is causally coupled to the earlier event itself rather than to an already formed parent record.

This produces two positive classifications under one constitutive predicate:

- `PRIMARY_FORMATION_CONFIRMED`: the target acquired the information through a formation interaction directly coupled to the earlier event.
- `DERIVATIVE_FORMATION_CONFIRMED`: the target acquired the information through a causal transfer from an identified parent record.

Engineered versus autonomous describes the origin of the formation interaction. Primary versus derivative describes information provenance. These axes are independent: either kind of interaction may produce either kind of record.

If the target was already informative about the declared event before the alleged formation interval, or the alleged write only read, selected, or revealed that existing information, the outcome is `READ_OR_PREEXISTING`, not new formation. A claim about a new carrier coordinate remains possible only if that coordinate and its prior absence were declared prospectively.

## What is not constitutive

Redundant availability in multiple fragments is a measured strength, reach, or robustness property. It is not required for record formation. Redundancy measurements may qualify a confirmed record, but their presence cannot rescue a failed formation gate and their absence cannot by itself refute formation.

## One theory, not separately scoped theories

Primary formation, derivative copying, autonomous formation, engineered formation, persistence, read-only access, rewriting, and erasure are modes of one causal dynamical object:

`C_t = C(X, W_t)`, `R_(t+) ~ K(R_t, C_t, B_t, G_t)`, and `Y_t ~ M(R_t, Q_t)`,

supplemented by a provenance graph for the earlier event `X`. Here `R` is the physical carrier state, `W` is an exogenous or endogenous formation or modification interaction, `C` is the measurable causal transduction or coupling path by which event/writer forcing reaches `R`, `G` is the measurable surface formation or closure gate that marks when incorporation ceases and hold begins, `B` is the hold environment, `Q` is the read intervention, and `Y` is the observed readout. Writer-off persistence is the `C=OFF` post-closure hold evolution of the same `K`, not a separate persistence theory. `C` and `G` must be physical, measurable, and falsifiable; narrative arrows do not satisfy them.

`R` is representation-independent. It may be discrete, continuous, count-valued, field-valued, distributed, or mixed. “Definite physical state” means an actual per-specimen physical state or trajectory resolvable within prospectively declared uncertainty. It does not mean that every record is a binary bit or a point microstate.

The causal path types the mode. Direct `X -> C_psi -> K -> R_target` under `G` is primary formation when the target previously lacked the information. `X -> R_parent -> C_copy -> K -> R_target` under `G` is derivative formation. An endogenous driver of `C_psi` is autonomous and an exogenous driver is engineered. A `Q` that reveals existing target information without a causal increase is read-only. A transition from old recorded content or lineage to new content is rewriting. A causal loss of accessible recorded information is erasure. These types do not license separate laws.

The intended general theory must derive or predict `C`, `G`, `K`, its writer-off hold dynamics, and `M` from prospectively measurable physical primitives. A taxonomy that merely assigns these labels after observing trajectories is not the theory and cannot close the formation gate.

## Scientific scope

This decision fixes the meaning of “formed record.” It does not establish that any studied surface satisfies the predicate, does not establish a universal record-formation law, and does not establish gravity emergence. Those conclusions require actual physical data, prospective falsification, independent reproduction through the released URM, and the wider any-and-every-surface closure demanded by the program.
