## What these surfaces have earned

They are actual physics and they constrain the candidate theory.  Together they show
that the common law must admit engineered and autonomous forcing, continuous as well as
device states, bounded/state-dependent transitions, an explicit transduction path `C`,
a physical closure gate `G`, writer-off dynamics, destructive or nondestructive readout,
and provenance.  They do not satisfy the prospective causal and independent-reproduction
conjunction.

## Immediate scientific step

The first possible positive formation result requires a new, prospectively randomized
or prospectively natural-contrast acquisition with event-linked BEFORE observations,
physical `C` and `G` assays, common hold, repeated reads, read-backaction calibration,
held-out predictions from measurable primitives, and independent reproduction.  The CA1
and living-DNA commissions are being specified to meet this boundary.
