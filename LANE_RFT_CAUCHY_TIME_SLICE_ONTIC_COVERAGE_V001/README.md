## Result first

This lane proves one bounded physical bridge toward `OPCC_FM`.  If an
independently specified finite record mission has a genuine branch-independent
Cauchy macro-cut, an exact theory-level Cauchy or time-slice law, a complete
crossing datum, standard positive/normalized physical propagation, and no
post-cut event-label input, then the actual mission has an ontic complete causal
cover in the already existing K/W*/C*/I union.

Formally,

\[
 \operatorname{CTS}(r)
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists {\cal C}\,\operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C})
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists\Xi[\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)
             \land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}(\Xi)]
 \Longrightarrow A1\land A2\land A3\land A4.
\]

The first implication is this lane's exact concentration/reduction lemma.  The
second and third use the sealed
finite-mission faithful-transcription and Coverage-U lanes.  The proof constructs
only two or finitely many macro-modules: exact propagation from the event side to
the Cauchy boundary and exact propagation from that boundary to complete
registered histories.  A continuum field configuration or infinite-dimensional
algebra can be the boundary object.  No lattice, time step, state truncation, or
finite-dimensional approximation is introduced.

The routing is physical rather than material-specific:

1. well-posed classical deterministic/stochastic evolution with standard-Borel
   Cauchy data routes to K;
2. an ordinary finite-depth quantum circuit or comb with finite-dimensional
   wires routes to K;
3. a fixed-background AQFT or gauge theory with a C*-algebraic time-slice
   isomorphism and complete CP instruments routes to A, or to W in one fixed
   folium when states and maps are normal; and
4. a finite-dimensional deterministic completely CP-preserving higher-order
   module routes to I only when it lies wholly on one side of a definite external
   macro-cut.

## Exact boundary

This is a conditional theorem, not the missing universal premise.  It does not
prove

\[
 \operatorname{RECORD}_{\rm FM}(r)\Longrightarrow\operatorname{CTS}(r),
\]

and therefore does not prove `OPCC_FM` for every bona-fide record.  Operational
finiteness and positive record contrast do not themselves establish a complete
Cauchy datum, a time-slice isomorphism, exact UCP/Markov sewing, or a definite
macro-cut.

`CTS` is ontic.  Its clauses are physical facts about the episode and governing
dynamics, fixed without fitting the scored record response, but this lane does
not require surviving independent evidence for those facts.  Consequently the
conclusion is `PhysEnc/ARCH`, not `AuthEnc/ADM`; it does not supply C5, A5, a
reset operation, or a finite-sample certificate.

The theorem covers fixed-background field theories only when all relevant
constraint, center, edge/flux, topological, dressing, asymptotic, apparatus,
memory, and correlation data are in a full field-plus-apparatus joint separator
algebra and one compatible normalized joint separator state exists.  A field
time-slice algebra is only one embedded component; embeddings or separate
marginals are not enough.  In branch I the correctly typed object may instead be a normalized
boundary strategy with a common future tester.  It does not cover
formal perturbative series merely by calling them C*-algebras.  Exact dynamical
gravity remains open because a branch-independent bounded separator, a common
physical observable algebra, positive normalized instruments, and even the
event-to-query causal order are not established in that generality.

The reduction is deliberately close to the physical content of C0--C4.  Its
value is concentration: standard initial-value/time-slice physics supplies the
dynamics and finite macro-module portions, while actual completeness of the
joint separator remains the irreducible physical premise.  It is not presented
as a new empirical coverage result.
