# Hostile audit

## 1. Circularity audit

`CTS` is not defined by a successful encoding.  T0--T5 refer to the actual
episode, a physical macro-cut, independently specified initial-value or
time-slice structure, physical propagation maps, the full crossing census, and
source-label custody.  None uses positive contrast, quotient dimension, decoder
success, a fitted factorization, an admission token, or the desired conclusion.

The theorem is nevertheless conditional.  T1--T4 contain substantial physical
facts not implied by the finite-mission record predicate.  The proof must not be
reported as a derivation of those facts.  Its valid role is to show that, once
standard physical Cauchy completeness exists, no further representation class or
material taxonomy is needed.

**Finding:** noncircular conditional bridge; no universal admission theorem.

## 3. Time-slice audit

Equation (4) is an assumed physical time-slice isomorphism for a fixed-background
observable theory.  The lane does not infer it from microcausality or global
hyperbolicity alone.  Source preparation and apparatus measurements are not
silently generated by the isomorphism; T3 separately requires their complete CP
instruments and UCP Heisenberg maps.  Equation (4) yields only the field
time-slice algebra \(\mathcal A_{{\rm fld},G}\).  T4 separately requires the
full joint separator \(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\), its compatible joint state, and
the embedding \(j_G\).  The field marginal is pulled to the cut by composition
with \(\mathcal A_{\rm fld}(\iota_G)\), the joint state restricts to that
marginal, and the future effect map lands in
\(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\); equations (7)--(10) are therefore typed.

The theorem makes no joint-distribution claim for incompatible measurements.

**Finding:** no AQFT time-slice overclaim.

## 4. Continuum and finiteness audit

The K branch permits standard-Borel infinite-dimensional classical state
objects.  The A and W branches permit infinite-dimensional algebras.  A W*
algebra in the fixed physical folium may be type III; the abstract C*-algebra is
not itself assigned a Murray--von Neumann type.  Disjoint algebraic states route
through A only when they are states on one prospectively common physical
C*-observable algebra.  A continuum
PDE/QFT slab counts as one module only if its exact physical initial-value map is
well posed and all ports are included.  A numerical mesh, integrator, fitted
surrogate, or unexplained endpoint channel is not an exact physical module.

This is consistent with C1: causal architecture is finitely macro-slotted, while
the internal state/algebra need not be finite.  Singular/nonunique evolution or
an unresolved infinite ancestor process is expressly outside T3.

**Finding:** no hidden discretization or finite-dimensionality assumption.

## 5. Classical and operational-quantum audit

For classical dynamics, a merely existing regular conditional probability is not
called a physical causal kernel.  T2--T3 require the actual interventional
initial-value law.  Non-Markov visible dynamics qualify only after the real
memory/bath/history variables or future noise ports are included.

For ordinary quantum mechanics, K is limited to a finite-depth physical
circuit/comb with finite-dimensional wires and complete CP instruments.  Its cut
object is a normalized quantum state on the complete wire tuple, not a classical
standard-Borel law.  Infinite-dimensional operational
systems require the A or W conditions.  Branch-conditioned record propagation
does not derive an unconditioned actualization law.

T5 requires a conditional future kernel, UCP map/extension, or tester with no
\(\operatorname{src}(x)\) argument after conditioning on the complete separator
and context.  Equal future-input marginals alone would not exclude an
\(x\)-dependent separator/future-input correlation.

**Finding:** branch routing is scoped to physical causal maps.

## 6. Gauge audit

The theorem does not infer a tensor factor across a gauge boundary.  T4 demands
every center, Gauss-law flux, edge/dressing variable, topological/asymptotic
datum, and relevant correlation.  It also requires one compatible normalized
joint field-plus-apparatus separator state, not merely embeddings or mutually inconsistent
marginals.  A gauge-invariant C*- or W*-algebra with these
objects can satisfy the theorem; a convenient local subsystem that omits them
cannot.

The cited Maxwell and Abelian-gauge results warn that universal/reduced nets and
topology affect locality.  They do not prove that every gauge mission satisfies
T2--T4, nor do they furnish an OPCC counterexample for a fixed episode.

**Finding:** gauge nonfactorization is handled conditionally, not waved away.

## 7. Perturbative and dynamical-gravity audit

A formal perturbative star algebra is not asserted to be a physical C*- or W*
algebra with positive states and UCP instruments.  Fixed-background
perturbative time-slice results are cited only as scoped support for T2.

For exact dynamical gravity, nonlocal dressing, constraints/asymptotic charges,
state-dependent causal structure, and the lack of a generally established common
positive observable/instrument system can defeat T1, T3, or T4.  The lane calls
this open.  It does not claim the literature proves that every possible
gravitational cover fails.

**Finding:** no dynamical-gravity or all-QFT claim.

## 8. Authentication and A5 audit

proves `PhysEnc/ARCH`, not `AuthEnc/ADM`.

No physical incidence-graph authentication or spanning replacement family is
supplied.  A5 and reset remain open.

**Finding:** ontic and evidential claims remain separated.

## 10. Reduction-versus-physics audit

T1, T3, and T4 deliberately overlap much of C1; T1, T2, T4, and T5 overlap much
of C2.  Thus CTS1 is an exact concentration/normal-form lemma, not a derivation of
complete physical coverage from weaker record facts.  Its physics content is the
branch-specific identification of standard well-posed initial-value or time-slice
structures that can supply T2--T3 without finite discretization.  T4 remains the
irreducible physical premise.

**Finding:** valid bounded reduction; no inflated empirical-coverage claim.

## 11. Universal-quantifier audit

The exact covered domain is
\(\mathfrak R^{\rm bf,CTS}_{\rm FM}\), not all of

cover.  Excluded gravity and no-macro-cut cases remain candidate obstructions,
not counterexamples.
