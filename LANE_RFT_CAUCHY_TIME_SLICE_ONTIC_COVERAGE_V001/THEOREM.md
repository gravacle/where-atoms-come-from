# Cauchy/time-slice theorem for ontic finite-mission coverage

**Claim class:** exact conditional physical-cover normal-form/reduction theorem

**Not claimed:** `RECORD_FM -> CTS`, all-QFT coverage, dynamical-gravity
coverage, independent authentication, A5, reset, finite-data confirmation, or
an objective quantum actualization law

## 1. Imported definitions and target

Let \(r\in\mathfrak C_{\rm FM}\) be an independently specified finite-mission
candidate in the sense of
`LANE_RFT_FINITE_MISSION_FAITHFUL_ADMISSION_V001`.  In particular, its physical
referents, event alternatives, contexts, interventions, complete registered
histories, nominated lineage, write region, closure regime, and finite resolution
are specified without using a later representation or score.

That lane defines an ontic complete causal cover

\[
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C})                 \tag{1}
\]

by physical clauses C0--C4 and proves

\[
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C})
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists\Xi[\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)
             \land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}(\Xi)].     \tag{2}
\]

It also proves that the architecture in (2) supplies Coverage-U A1--A4 as
objective physical statements.  What remains open there is

\[
 \operatorname{OPCC}_{\rm FM}:\quad
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM}\ \exists {\cal C}\,
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C}).                 \tag{3}
\]

This lane identifies a standard physical sufficient condition for one record at
a time.  It neither assumes nor proves (3).

## 2. The physical Cauchy/time-slice predicate

Write \(\operatorname{CTS}(r)\) when T0--T5 are true **as facts of the physical
episode**.  They are not inferred from positive record contrast, a fitted latent
state, an admission result, or the desired theorem.

### T0 -- same episode and outcome-independent referents

There is one actual physical episode realizing the source operations, event
supports, apparatus, carrier lineage, clocks, complete registered histories, and
queries named by \(r\).  The candidate referents and governing theory are fixed
independently of the scored response.  A synthetic simulator with a newly added
wire carrying the event label does not satisfy T0.

Write \(\operatorname{src}(x)=\operatorname{do}(x)\) when \(x\) is an
authorized intervention and \(\operatorname{src}(x)=\operatorname{arm}(x)\)
when \(x\) is an endogenous alternative.  For an endogenous alternative, every
conditional state used below is required only for a positive-probability arm and
is normalized within that arm.  This prevents an observational branch from being
silently promoted to an intervention.

T0 is ontic rather than evidential.  It does not assert that an independent C5
certificate of these facts survives.

### T1 -- bounded ancestor slab and definite Cauchy macro-cut

The registered source-to-query ancestors lie in a prospectively bounded causal
mission domain \(D_r\), or in finitely many such domains joined at declared
ports.  There is one physical macro-partition \(G\), common to every event arm
and every complete outcome branch, with all authorized event occurrences on its
past side and all registered queries on its future side.

For a spacetime theory, \(G\) contains an open globally hyperbolic Cauchy
neighborhood \(N_G\subset D_r\) which contains an achronal Cauchy hypersurface
\(\Sigma_G\) for the relevant ancestor domain.  For a circuit or
higher-order operational theory, \(G\) is the analogous external wire boundary:
every event-to-query route crosses it.  A causal order that changes so that no
single such external partition exists does not satisfy T1.

Operationally finite output alphabets do not imply T1.  Boundedness here is a
claim about the actual ancestor domain and declared ports, not merely the time
stamps of the writer and reader.

### T2 -- theory-level Cauchy sufficiency

The physical theory supplies a complete initial-value object on \(G\), selected
from the theory rather than from record-response success.  One of the following
physical statements holds.

1. **Classical Cauchy/Markov statement.**  The complete Cauchy datum \(S_G\) is
   a standard-Borel state object and the deterministic or stochastic
   initial-value problem is well posed on each side of \(G\).  If the displayed
   variables are non-Markovian, the actual bath, memory, or history variables
   required to make the causal propagation complete are included in \(S_G\) or
   in a declared arm-common future input.
2. **Ordinary operational quantum statement.**  The physical implementation is
   a finite-depth causally ordered circuit or comb with finite-dimensional wires,
   and the tuple of actual wires crossing \(G\), including classical outcome
   histories and references, is its complete external interface.
3. **Algebraic time-slice statement.**  On a fixed globally hyperbolic
   background, \(D_r\) and \(N_G\) are admitted globally hyperbolic objects,
   \(\iota_G:N_G\hookrightarrow D_r\) is an admissible Cauchy morphism, and the
   physical field-observable theory assigns unital C*-algebras obeying

   \[
    {\cal A}_{\rm fld}(\iota_G):{\cal A}_{\rm fld}(N_G)
        \overset{\cong}{\longrightarrow}{\cal A}_{\rm fld}(D_r).  \tag{4}
   \]

   Thus \(\mathcal A_{{\rm fld},G}:=\mathcal A_{\rm fld}(N_G)\) is the
   theory-level **field** time-slice algebra, not a tensor factor inferred from
   the response.  T4 separately constructs the full joint field-plus-apparatus
   separator \(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\); equation (4) alone does not contain a
   crossing probe, controller, or memory.  A W* realization is allowed only when
   one physical folium is fixed, the Cauchy isomorphism and its inverse extend as
   normal maps there, and all relevant states, evolution maps, and instruments
   are normal.
4. **Bounded internal indefinite-order statement.**  Each causally nonseparable
   object is a finite-dimensional deterministic completely CP-preserving
   higher-order module wholly on one side of \(G\), with a complete finite
   external interface.  T1's macro-order is not relaxed.

T2 is a substantive premise.  Microcausality, finite resolution, endpoint
probabilities, or a formal state space alone does not prove it.

### T3 -- exact positive/normalized propagation in finitely many macro-modules

The past slab, future slab, apparatus interactions, and any declared intermediate
slabs are finitely many prospectively bounded **exact physical** modules.  Their
maps are, as appropriate,

- normalized standard-Borel causal kernels;
- completely positive maps whose complete outcome sum is unital in the
  Heisenberg picture (or trace preserving in the Schrödinger picture);
- UCP maps between the actual joint C*-boundary algebras, including maps into
  \(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\); normal UCP maps between the corresponding joint
  W*-algebras in the W branch; or
- deterministic completely CP-preserving higher-order maps which contract with
  complete local instruments to CP macro-maps.

A deterministic well-posed classical PDE flow is the delta kernel concentrated
on its exact solution.  A stochastic PDE includes the physical noise law in the
kernel or an arm-common future input.  A continuum slab may therefore be one
exact propagator module.  T3 never replaces it by a lattice, a time-step scheme,
a truncated Hilbert space, or a response-fitted endpoint table.  An unresolved
infinite ancestor process, singular evolution, nonunique continuation, unknown
port, or merely formal nonpositive series fails T3.

Explosive evolution also fails T3.  Overlapping physical couplings are bundled
into their actual exact joint module; they may not be placed in an arbitrary
sequential order merely to fit a comb.

### T4 -- complete crossing datum and external-port census

The complete crossing object at \(G\) contains every physical degree and joint
correlation crossing \(G\) in the ancestors of registered histories.  This
includes, when present, target and environment state, controller, clock,
reference, classical outcome history, active-maintenance state, gauge center,
Gauss-law charge/flux, edge or dressing variable, topological sector, asymptotic
datum, and correlations among them.  Every remaining external port and its joint
input rule are declared.

For classical K there is an actual normalized joint law \(\mu^G_{x,z}\) on the
complete crossing object.  For finite-dimensional quantum K there is an actual
normalized density operator/state \(\rho^G_{x,z}\) on the tensor product of all
complete crossing wires, including classical flags and reference wires.  For A
or W there is an actual normalized positive state
\(\widehat\omega^G_{x,z}\) on a **joint** separator algebra
\(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\).  This algebra contains a declared unital embedding
\(j_G:\mathcal A_{{\rm fld},G}\hookrightarrow\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\), and the
joint state's restrictions agree with the field state and every declared
apparatus component state.  Common embeddings or separately specified marginals
do not by themselves guarantee such a compatible joint state.  If the complete
field-plus-apparatus theory itself obeys (4), one may take
\(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G=\mathcal A_{{\rm fld},G}\).  For I,
the crossing object may instead be the valid finite-dimensional boundary
strategy/process object \(\Theta^G_{x,z}\), with deterministic completely
CP-preserving normalization and one arm-common future tester under the link
product.  Only when that higher-order boundary is a state wire is it called
\(\rho^G_{x,z}\).

T4 does not require a tensor-product split.  A nonfactorizing C*- or W*-algebra
with center is allowed.  Conversely, calling a convenient local tensor factor
"the system" while omitting a physical center, dressing, bath memory, or bypass
does not satisfy T4.  Naming an asymptotic port does not make a noncompact
gravitational dressing satisfy T1's prospectively bounded mission premise.

### T5 -- label provenance, common future, and complete outcomes

The actual source-generating mechanism and allowed recipients of the event label
are physical facts.  After \(G\), no module, controller, instrument, analyst, or
external port receives \(x\) except through the complete crossing object.  All
remaining future inputs have one \(x\)-independent conditional **joint** rule
given the complete crossing object and \(z\); equality of their separate arm
marginals is insufficient.  Failure,
no-click, saturation, censoring, destructive-read outcomes, adaptive histories,
and internal order-control outcomes remain in the complete registered alphabet.

More explicitly, the common future object has no
\(\operatorname{src}(x)\) argument:

1. in classical K it is a normalized conditional kernel
   \(L_{j,z}(dh,du\mid C_G)\), including all fresh future inputs \(u\);
2. in finite-dimensional quantum K it is one common CPTP/comb future extension,
   with complete CP-instrument branches for the registered histories;
3. in A or W it is a UCP (normal-UCP for W) Heisenberg map
   \(L_{j,z}:\ell^\infty(Y_j)\to\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\), together with any
   declared arm-independent joint extension conditional on the separator;
4. in I it is one arm-common future tester \(T_{j,z}\) linked to
   \(\Theta^G_{x,z}\).

T0--T5 are required independently of whether the candidate has zero or positive
read contrast.  `CTS` is therefore not a record definition and cannot be earned
by fitting the desired factorization after seeing the endpoints.

## 3. Theorem CTS1 -- conditional Cauchy/time-slice normal form

For every independently specified finite-mission candidate,

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{CTS}(r)
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists {\cal C}_r\,
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,{\cal C}_r).}               \tag{5}
\]

### Construction

Take the actual past propagation from the source side to \(G\), the complete
Cauchy object at \(G\), and the actual future propagation from \(G\) to each
complete registered history.  Retain every intermediate intervention slot and
external port declared by T3--T5.  Call this physical object \(\mathcal C_r\).
It has two macro-slabs when no intermediate slot is needed and finitely many
otherwise.

### Proof of C0

T0 identifies the source, modules, boundary, lineage, and registered query with
the physical episode.  The maps are the actual maps named by the independently
fixed physical theory, not a score-table model.  Hence C0 holds.

### Proof of C1

T1 bounds the actual ancestor mission.  T3 supplies finitely many bounded exact
propagator modules, and T4 supplies the actual compatible joint crossing object
and complete port census.  Treating an exact continuum
propagator as one module does not leave its continuum evolution unresolved: its
state object and law are the well-posed physical initial-value map.  T3 excludes
singular/nonunique propagation, unknown label-bearing ports, unbounded feedback,
and an unresolved infinite ancestor process.  Hence C1 holds without a finite
state-space or spacetime discretization.

### Proof of C2

T1 places every authorized event occurrence before \(G\) and every registered
query after it.  T2, together with T4, makes \(S_G\), the circuit-wire tuple,
\(\widehat{\mathcal A}_G\) containing \(\mathcal A_{{\rm fld},G}\), or the
higher-order boundary strategy into a theory-level Cauchy object for the complete
ancestor domain.  T4 retains every crossing degree, port, and joint correlation,
including nonfactorizing constraint data.  T5 supplies one
\(\operatorname{src}(x)\)-independent conditional joint future object for the
appropriate branch.  Thus the crossing object is a complete definite
event-to-query boundary.  C2 holds.

### Proof of C3

T0 fixes the actual source and T5 states the physical label custody.  After the
cut, the event label can enter only through the complete crossing object; no
hidden label-conditioned postselection is removed because histories are
complete.  C3 holds.

### Proof of C4 and branch routing

T2--T3 provide at least one existing composability branch:

1. A classical deterministic/stochastic initial-value system is a finite
   standard-Borel stochastic DAG after any finite feedback unrolling.  The exact
   Cauchy flow is its normalized transition kernel.  This is K.
2. A finite-depth ordinary operational quantum circuit or causally ordered comb
   with finite-dimensional wires and complete CP instruments is K.
3. Equation (4), arbitrary algebraic states, UCP evolution/instruments, and T4's
   full joint separator algebra, compatible joint states, and common observable
   lineage are A.  If one physical folium is fixed and all
   states/maps and the Cauchy isomorphism in both directions are normal, the same
   construction may instead use W; that W* algebra may be type III.  Separately,
   A can carry mutually disjoint algebraic states only when they are states on one
   prospectively common physical C*-observable algebra.  Centers do not force a
   tensor factor or finite dimension.  The abstract C*-algebra itself is not
   assigned a Murray--von Neumann type.
4. A T2(4) module contracts to CP macro-maps on its side of the definite external
   cut and gives I.  No order between internal laboratories is asserted.

Therefore C4 holds.  C0--C4 prove (5).  QED.

This is an exact normal-form/reduction theorem.  T1, T3, and T4 deliberately
instantiate most of C1--C2 as independently physical facts; CTS1 does not derive
them from `RECORD_FM`.  Its branch-specific content is that a well-posed exact
initial-value law or fixed-background time-slice theory can supply T2--T3 without
discretizing the continuum.  T4's actual joint separator remains irreducible.

## 4. Exact factorization at the Cauchy cut

The construction gives more than a branch label.  In the classical route, let
\(\mu^G_{x,z}\) be the normalized law of the complete Cauchy datum.  Contracting
the common future modules gives an \(x\)-independent kernel \(L_{j,z}\) on the
complete history alphabet \(Y_j\):

\[
 P_r(B\mid \operatorname{src}(x),z,j)
 =\int_{S_G}L_{j,z}(B\mid s)\,\mu^G_{x,z}(ds).            \tag{6}
\]

For the C*-route, compose the complete future instruments in the Heisenberg
direction to obtain a map into the full joint separator,

\[
 L_{j,z}:\ell^\infty(Y_j)\longrightarrow
          \widehat{\mathcal A}_G.                        \tag{7}
\]

If \(\omega^D_{x,z}\) is the physical post-source field state on
\(\mathcal A_{\rm fld}(D_r)\), the field marginal at the cut is

\[
 \omega^{{\rm fld},G}_{x,z}
 =\omega^D_{x,z}\circ\mathcal A_{\rm fld}(\iota_G).     \tag{8}
\]

T4 supplies the actual compatible joint state satisfying

\[
 \widehat\omega^G_{x,z}\circ j_G
 =\omega^{{\rm fld},G}_{x,z}.                            \tag{9}
\]

Then for \(B\subseteq Y_j\),

\[
 P_r(B\mid \operatorname{src}(x),z,j)
 =\widehat\omega^G_{x,z}\!\left(L_{j,z}(1_B)\right).    \tag{10}
\]

The future kernel or effect map has no \(x\)-argument by T5.  Equations (6) and
(10) concern one registered instrument at a time and do not assign a joint law to
incompatible quantum instruments.

## 5. Theorem CTS2 -- faithful encoding and Coverage-U A1--A4

Combining CTS1 with the already proved FM2 transcription gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{CTS}(r)
 \Longrightarrow
 \exists\Xi[\operatorname{PhysEnc}_{\rm FM}(r,\Xi)
             \land\operatorname{ARCH}_{\cup}(\Xi)]
 \Longrightarrow A1\land A2\land A3\land A4.}           \tag{11}
\]

The first arrow in (11) preserves the physical referents, exact registered law,
complete event-bearing paths, complete outcomes, and intervention provenance.
The existing union branches then supply bounded mission, positive/normalized
causal sewing, complete physical boundary, and no post-cut label injection.

No C5 authentication premise occurs in `CTS`.  Thus (11) does **not** imply
`AuthEnc`, any of the four `*_ADMIT` predicates, or A5.  An independent physical
certificate may upgrade a particular episode, but is not part of this theorem.

## 6. Corollary and the remaining universal obstruction

Define the covered bona-fide subclass

\[
 \mathfrak R^{\rm bf,CTS}_{\rm FM}
 :=\{r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM}:\operatorname{CTS}(r)\}. \tag{12}
\]

Then

\[
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf,CTS}_{\rm FM}\ \exists\mathcal C_r\,
 \operatorname{OCC}_{\cup}(r,\mathcal C_r).              \tag{13}
\]

This is genuine ontic coverage of a physical subclass.  It is not (3).  Closing
`OPCC_FM` by this route would require the additional physical theorem

\[
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm bf}_{\rm FM},\quad
 \operatorname{CTS}(r),                                  \tag{14}
\]

or a separate cover theorem for every record outside (11).  The definition of a
bona-fide record establishes creation, lineage, closure, noncreation, and
positive registered contrast; it does not establish a branch-independent Cauchy
surface, a complete constraint algebra, exact positive propagation, or a finite
bounded physical ancestor cover.  Hence `RECORD_FM -> CTS` is neither assumed nor
proved.

The smallest unresolved physical obstruction is the existence, for every
bona-fide record, of one exact branch-independent event-to-query Cauchy separator
with all constraint/asymptotic correlations and positive normalized sewing.
Fixed-background standard physics often supplies it.  Exact dynamical gravity is
the sharp open case: diffeomorphism-invariant dressing can be nonlocal, the
metric and causal order can be state dependent, and a common positive physical
C*- or W*-instrument description has not been established generally.

No concrete standard-physics bona-fide record is proved here to violate
`OPCC_FM`.  Failure to establish CTS is an unproved/excluded case, not an ontic
counterexample.

## 7. Primary theoretical sources and their limited use

1. R. Brunetti, K. Fredenhagen, and R. Verch, [*The generally covariant locality
   principle -- A new paradigm for local quantum field theory*
   (math-ph/0112041)](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0112041).  This supplies the
   locally covariant framework and time-slice axiom used as an independent T2
   premise; it does not prove that every record mission has the required cut.
2. B. Chilian and K. Fredenhagen, [*The time slice axiom in perturbative quantum
   field theory on globally hyperbolic spacetimes*
   (arXiv:0802.1642)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1642).  This proves a scoped
   perturbative scalar-field time-slice result, not all-QFT or quantum-gravity
   coverage.
3. C. J. Fewster and B. Lang, [*Dynamical locality of the free Maxwell field*
   (arXiv:1403.7083)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7083), and M. Benini,
   [*Locality in Abelian gauge theories over globally hyperbolic spacetimes*
   (arXiv:1503.00131)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.00131).  These motivate T4's
   explicit topological/center/flux qualification; gauge locality is not obtained
   by silently choosing a tensor factor.
4. W. Donnelly and S. B. Giddings, [*Observables, gravitational dressing, and
   obstructions to locality and subsystems*
   (arXiv:1607.01025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01025), and [*How is quantum
   information localized in gravity?*
   (arXiv:1706.03104)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03104).  These support the
   gravity boundary warning; they are not used as a no-go proof against every
   gravitational separator.
