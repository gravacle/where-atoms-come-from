T-14   ALPHA'S TWO EXPONENTS, MEASURED
====================================================================================================
  carrier                 d  n* (Z-only)  GENERIC slope  Z-ONLY slope          
  toric 2x2 [[8,2,2]]     2            2         2.0211        2.0389          
  [[5,1,3]] non-CSS       3            5         3.0036        4.9836          
  [[7,1,3]] Steane        3            3         3.0046        3.0118          

  READ: the GENERIC exponent is the code distance d. The Z-ONLY exponent is n*, the fewest
  AVAILABLE terms whose product reaches the code space -- which equals d only when the
  available terms happen to include a minimum-weight logical. On the toric they coincide;
  on [[5,1,3]] and Steane they do not, and Theorem D's unqualified form is wrong there.
