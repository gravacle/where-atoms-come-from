## What was not accepted

U-DCL is not derived from recordhood, the pointer circuit, data, Lorentzian
geometry, or gravity. It packages C/S/J-grade physical ontology and T5-grade
provenance in a concrete, falsifiable form. Its gain is exact physical typing and
a frozen counterexample protocol, not weaker premises.

The directed incidence is primitive proto-causal structure. The proposal is
metric-free and pre-Lorentzian, not causal-assumption-free. The scale-natural
pointer circuit proves one consistent architecture-relative realization only.

## Falsifier boundary

The accepted falsifier freezes the bona-fide record admission, mission,
incidence, ports, complete instruments, locality rules, and every applicable
positive K/W category. Failure in one optional representation is insufficient;
the witness must exclude every applicable category or the one category
independently warranted as exclusive. One actual admitted exception falsifies
U-DCL and cannot be removed from the record domain afterward.

postulate/conjecture** whose adoption yields conditional universal coverage. It
does not adopt the postulate. That remains the user's theory decision.
