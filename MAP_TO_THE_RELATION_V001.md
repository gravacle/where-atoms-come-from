# MAP — FINDING THE RELATION BETWEEN GRAVITY, EM AND ALPHA AT THE RECORD SURFACE — V001 — 2026-08-18

**PROPOSED, NOT ADOPTED.** Written on the principal's instruction after W-50.

## 0. WHY THE THREE GRAVITY ROUTES FAILED, AND WHAT THEY HAVE IN COMMON

| route | what was tried | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| W-31 | make the carrier's **topology** dynamical | **REFUTED 3/3.** A topology-free twin reproduced every number to `1e-16` |
| W-44 | install a **classical metric** (link length, site potential, clock) | **bought nothing.** Redundancy null in all three; the sieve result was a report on `eps/g²` |
| W-45 → W-50 | identify gravity **functionally** via capacity | **forced by counting.** Any finite resource passes all four markers |

> **THEY SHARE ONE THING: ALL THREE ACCEPTED THE GRAPH AS GIVEN.** Topology varied *which* graph;
> the metric decorated a fixed graph; capacity counted on a fixed graph. **In every lane of this
> program, which vertex is adjacent to which link was installed by the registrar and never
> questioned.** W-38 made a *region* an output. The adjacency was still input.

**If gravity is what makes geometry meaningful, the untested question is whether the geometry can be
an OUTPUT of record dynamics rather than an input to it.**

## 1. THE TARGET, STATED SO IT CAN FAIL

> **Can the carrier's geometry be RECONSTRUCTED from the records it holds — and does alpha set how
> much of it is recoverable?**

If yes, the three-way relation is not an assembly of separate roles but a single statement:
**EM supplies the records, the geometry is what they jointly determine, and alpha bounds how much of
it survives to be determined.** That is a relation in which all three appear and none is decoration.

**This is also the paper's own stated open problem** (Gravacle v337, Appendix C): step 1 is *a local
record-to-geometry map*, step 2 *compatibility across overlapping patches*, step 3 *the large-record
limit*. **W-39/41/42 are step 2 with numbers in them. Step 1 has never been attempted here.**

## 2. THE STEPS, IN ORDER, EACH WITH ITS FALSIFIER

### STEP 1 — THE RECORD-TO-GEOMETRY MAP
**Build.** Take the carrier. Run the dynamics. Compute the record–record correlation matrix, using
**nothing but the records** — no link list, no coordinates. Attempt to reconstruct the adjacency.
**Measure.** Reconstruction fidelity against the true graph.
**Falsifier.** Shuffle the graph while holding the record set fixed; if reconstruction succeeds
equally on the shuffled graph, the correlations carry no geometry and the step fails.
**Why first.** Everything downstream is void without it, and it is cheap.

### STEP 2 — ALPHA AS THE RESOLUTION LIMIT
**Build.** Repeat Step 1 across `g²`.
**Predict, in advance.** Reconstruction degrades as `g²` rises, and fails where W-37b's legibility
bound says the region stops being readable: `9.6·g⁴·P/τ ≳ 1`.
**Falsifier.** Fidelity independent of `g²`, or a breakdown unrelated to the legibility bound. Then
alpha does not set the resolution and the relation is two-way, not three-way.
**Why this is the whole point.** If it holds, **alpha is the resolution limit of geometry** — the
first statement binding all three that is not an assembly.

### STEP 3 — THE SIGN PROBLEM, WHICH IS AN HONEST OBJECTION AND MUST NOT BE SKIPPED
> **OUR CAPACITY STORY IS REPULSIVE. GRAVITY IS ATTRACTIVE.** Records **crowd each other out**
> (W-39: margin `43.8x → 2.0x → 1.1x`), and past capacity one is **evicted** (W-41). Nothing in this
> program has ever shown records DRAWING each other.
**Build.** Ask whether any regime makes record formation *more* likely near existing records.
**Falsifier.** If none exists, the resource identification has the wrong sign, and that must be
recorded as a standing objection to calling capacity gravity-like — not buried.

### STEP 4 — ACCUMULATION
**Build.** Reconstruct from progressively larger record sets and ask whether the geometry converges.
**Falsifier.** No convergence, or convergence to something that is not the carrier.
**Why last.** It is the paper's step 3 and is meaningless before steps 1 and 2.

## 3. WHAT WOULD COUNT AS THE RELATION

**Steps 1 + 2 succeeding gives:** *the geometry of a region is what its records jointly determine,
and the coupling sets how finely it can be determined.* **Step 3 failing does not kill that** — it
would mean the resource is not gravity, while leaving the geometry claim intact. **Step 4 is what
would connect it to anything macroscopic, and is furthest out.**

## 4. WHAT THIS MAP DOES NOT PROMISE

It does not produce attraction, an equivalence principle, or a field equation. It does not touch
data (obligation E, still zero). It does not make the toy representative (obligation D). **It
attacks obligation A's gravity line and obligation B's three-way composition, and nothing else.**
