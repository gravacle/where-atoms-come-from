================================================================================================================
S5   REQUIREMENT (b): IS THE QUANTITY ADDITIVE OVER DISJOINT REGIONS?
================================================================================================================

EXACT ARGUMENT 4 -- ANY 'LARGEST EIGENVALUE OF A RELATION MATRIX' FAILS (b) AT EVERY N.
  Disjoint regions share no record and no relation, so the relation matrix of the union is
  BLOCK DIAGONAL: M = M_1 (+) M_2.  The spectrum of a block-diagonal matrix is the UNION of
  the blocks' spectra, so lam_max(M) = MAX(lam_max(M_1), lam_max(M_2)) -- never the sum.
  A maximum is not a sum.  Doubling the enclosed matter leaves lam_max EXACTLY UNCHANGED
  whenever the two halves are alike.  This holds at every N, for every relation, and no
  amount of growth in lam_max inside one region can repair it.

================================================================================================================
  S5 SUMMARY
================================================================================================================
  P_o IS additive over disjoint regions but is NOT a function of the enclosed record count:
  two disjoint 6-record regions give P_o = 2 x 7 = 14, while ONE region holding 12 records
  gives 21.  Its super-linear growth inside a region is a PACKING effect -- k logicals crowded
  onto n = k+2 qubits -- and it disappears the moment the records are actually far apart.
  A source term must not care how the matter is packed; this one cares about nothing else.

  P_int (either relation) and W_tot ARE additive over disjoint regions -- they are sums over
  records or over pairs, so additivity is automatic and carries no information.
  lam_max of ANY relation matrix is NOT additive, by exact argument 4, at every N.
  Total chi on a SHARED bath is NOT additive: the regions split the bath's capacity (C-36).
  Total chi on SEPARATE baths IS additive, but only because the bath was grown with N.
