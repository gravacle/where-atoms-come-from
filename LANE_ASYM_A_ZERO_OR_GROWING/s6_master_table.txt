EVERY strictly-linear, strictly-additive quantity on this family is the record count N up to a
constant factor.  C-35 already rules that class out: a count is topological and does not know how
much is enclosed.  NO quantity satisfying (a) AND (b) AND (c) was found.

The two strongest results are the (Z) rows, and they are exact rather than trends:
  * the ENERGY spread over all 2^N record configurations is IDENTICALLY 0 at every finite N,
    because H restricted to the code space is exactly -2*I.  Gravity's source is stress-energy;
    the record sector of this family carries none, at any N.
  * the symplectic pairing over record pairs and the number of records disturbed by a single
    local operation are IDENTICALLY 0 at every finite N, by commutativity and by d = 2.
Each of these rules out emergence at ANY N, with no appeal to reaching large N.

The chi rows are ruled out by FORM, not by range: total chi is bounded by log2 dim(bath) = nq for
every N by an exact chain-rule argument, and it is strictly SUBadditive over disjoint clusters.

WHAT IS NOT RULED OUT BY THIS LANE: any quantity requiring a NON-ABELIAN carrier.  Transport is
trivial on this family by construction, so the C-43 result (40 of 40 records moved on D(D_4)) is
untouched here.  This lane says nothing about it.
