## 5. THE THEORETICAL DISCUSSION AREAS

These are the places where computation has done what it can and the next move is a decision or a
piece of theory. Each states: the question, why it is live now, and what it blocks.

### TD-1 — THE WORLD-LEVEL Γ CLAIM (→ T-37; blocks T-15, T-16)

**The question.** The charter's claim is *"Γ is where gravity comes from — via emergence, not
identity at the record level."* What does that assert, **falsifiably, about the world's records**
under the amended definition?

**Why it is live now.** Three things changed at once: (a) the corner exclusions that appeared to
close the question at any N do **not** inherit to the world — on a metastable record the forbidden
interval is a continuum (T-30); (b) written records carry an **extensive, additive, sign-definite
configuration energy created by the act of writing** (T-32/T-34, pending solidity) — the first
source-shaped quantity in the program on a real object; (c) the standing framework block forbids
testing the record level against classical forms, so the claim's test must be stated in emergence
terms. **The claim currently has no falsifiable statement at all.** Nothing can be proved or refuted
until it does, and writing it is a theory decision, not a computation.

### TD-2 — THE THREE ROLES, RESTATED FOR THE WORLD (→ T-15)

**The question.** A-EM / A-GR / A-AL were established in DEF-A's corner. What are the three roles'
sentences **for the world's records**?

**Why it is live now.** The census gave each role a world-side fact to reckon with. **EM:** every
terrestrial record surveyed — anisotropy, trapped charge, chemical bond, spin state,
photoisomerisation — is electromagnetic at bottom; EM supplies the carrier of essentially every
record in the world, which is a *stronger* grounding than the corner ever had. **Γ:** the world
protects records by **barriers**, not homology (13/13 in the census; the toric code's finite-T
lifetime is L-independent while one grain reaches 10⁹ years); homology is the `E_b → ∞` idealisation
(C-74 holds on the torus, exactly). A-GR's "supplies the protection" needs restating as the limit
statement or it is false of the world. **α:** see TD-3.

### TD-3 — ALPHA THROUGH THE BARRIER (→ T-38)

**The question.** Does alpha's **value** enter the record laws through `E_b`?

**Why it is live now.** T-22 settled that nothing in the corpus was dimensionful and "alpha" named a
generic coupling. The amendment changed that: the PROVED lifetime law `τ = f₀⁻¹ exp(E_b/k_BT)` is
dimensionful, and **every terrestrial barrier in the census is an EM energy scale** — anisotropy,
bond, trap depth — whose magnitude is set by α (atomic energy scales ~ ½α²m_ec²). If so, *records
exist at terrestrial temperatures because α makes `E_b/kT ≫ 1`* is a statement in which α's value
matters — the content H-2 said alpha never had, and a possible route to the distinguishing
prediction PF-6 could not find. Needs a discussion of what exactly the claim is and what would
falsify it before any lane runs.

### TD-4 — ADMISSIBLE AND PROTECTION, UNIFIED (→ T-25; ratification)

**The question.** Three protection notions now coexist — kinematic (DEF-A), topological (homology,
C-74), energetic ((v′), the census) — and two writability notions (free vs work-paid dilation).
Ratify the hierarchy: **(iv′)/(v′) are THE definitions; DEF-A is the named `W=0, E_b→∞` limit;
topological protection is the homology realisation of `E_b→∞`.** O-4 then closes. Clause (v)'s
wording inherits. Mostly a decision to ratify what O-51 already adopted, plus the wording.

### TD-5 — A PREDICTION OF OUR OWN (→ T-39; blocks the proof's thesis)

**The question.** Both PROVED laws are standard physics validated through our machinery. PF-6 found
every measured result already belongs to a rival account. **Is there one statement this program makes
that no rival makes?** Candidates on the table: the clustered count law `k(t_m)` — the number of
records a carrier holds as a **step function of the required retention time** (T-31's width
mechanism); the written/unwritten ratio law (C-72) as a quantitative signature of formation. If none
exists, the proof must say so plainly (T-17 territory). Deciding which candidate is worth theory time
is prior to any computation.

### TD-6 — WHAT PLAYS THE FIELD (→ O-39, O-53, T-21)

**The question.** The program now has a source-shaped scalar on real records. **Nothing anywhere in
the corpus is a field** — every lane measured sources (O-39). And the one exact question left from
the corner work is O-53: is every extensive durable operator's extensive part a multiple of the
identity? What instrument would count as measuring the *field side* of the emergence claim — for the
world's records, not the corner? This is where the collective-level question (T-21, H-8) actually
lives now.

### TD-7 — THE BRACKETS: THE CMB PHOTON AND B−L

**The observation worth a discussion.** The census found the writable/protected tension realised at
nature's extremes: the CMB photon passes (i)–(iv) exactly and fails (v) at zero cost — the least
protected object surveyed; B−L passes (ii) exactly and has **no writer in nature**. Terrestrial
records live strictly between these brackets, and C-60's free-write/durability exclusion is the
statement both extremes instantiate. Whether the roles statement (T-15) should carry these as its
boundary cases is a framing decision worth ten minutes of discussion before the proof is drafted.
