  The decisive question is whether a transport-FIXED record EXISTS AT ALL.

  ERRATUM: a first attempt decomposed each eigenspace by the eigenvalues of sum_h (A_h + A_h^t)
  and did a subset-sum on those block dimensions. It reported NO transport-fixed record on
  D(Z_2) -- CONTRADICTING the direct measurement four lines above it, which found 0 of 40 moved
  with ||[A_h,R]|| = 0.000e+00 exactly. The bug: for an abelian group A_h IS the identity, so
  the commutant is EVERYTHING, but a symmetrised sum of identities has one eigenvalue and the
  method reported a single coarse block. Replaced with the model's own commutant.

  A record fixed by transport is exactly a record of the model built with the A_h as the
  theory's operators: clause (ii) then demands [A_h,R] = 0 as well as [H,R] = 0.
