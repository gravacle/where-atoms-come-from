## Result first

This lane tests the exact implication that remained open after the
Cauchy/time-slice coverage lane:

\[
 \operatorname{RECORD}_{\rm FM}(r)
 \stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}
 \operatorname{CTS}(r).
\]

The implication is false on the presently defined all-physical domain.

A bona-fide finite-mission record does guarantee the same physical episode and
outcome-independent referents in CTS T0. It also guarantees a weaker fact: a
real interaction creates an event-bearing distinction in the nominated lineage,
that distinction survives the declared closure regime to a label-blind query,
and the finite registered response has positive contrast. This is a genuine
lineage result, not a fitted representation.

The first CTS clause that does not follow is T1. Finite operational support and
a later record read do not imply that the **entire registered ancestor process**
is chronological, globally hyperbolic, prospectively bounded, or crossed by one
branch-independent event-before-query macro-cut.

The exact countermodel is a compact classical record mission in an explicitly
declared Deutsch-style distributional closed-timelike-curve theory. The
controller has a positive normalized transition kernel with one unique
fixed-point marginal law. This is not an ordinary acyclic joint sample-path
claim. The event writes a fresh stable bit, and a label-blind query reads it with
positive contrast. But a controller channel runs from the query region back to
the writer region. Any partition placing the write before the query therefore
has a future-to-past physical edge and cannot be a CTS macro-cut.

This countermodel is not a claim that closed timelike curves occur in nature.
It proves the logical non-implication because RECORD_FM nowhere assumes
chronology or global hyperbolicity. Excluding it requires a new physical
chronology/cut premise.

## Secondary transpose boundary

A basis-state qubit record held by matrix transposition is a useful independent
warning. Transposition is positive and trace preserving on one qubit but is not
completely positive under ancillary extension. The basis record itself is valid
in an isolated positive-map theory, yet the hold is outside CTS standard quantum
sewing.

It is not an OPCC counterexample. On a basis-only mission, transposition is the
identity on the diagonal algebra, so the same physical record coordinate has a
classical K cover. Tomographically complete preparations, including a Y-axis
state, are needed even to distinguish transposition from identity. Blocking all
classical recodings additionally requires frozen operational equivalences and a
contextuality witness, or authenticated spacelike locality and a Bell witness.
If an entangled ancilla is allowed directly, partial transposition becomes
nonpositive and the proposed operation is not physically coherent standard
quantum mechanics.
