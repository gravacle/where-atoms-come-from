# Hostile audit

## 1. Record-definition circularity

The countermodel is tested against D0--D4 and the physical RECORD_FM invariant
before CTS is evaluated. The record is not declared true because a cut,
factorization, rank, decoder, or admission class succeeds. Its carrier is the
actual forward bit \(B\), fixed from the physical construction.

**Finding:** no representation-defined record.

## 2. T0 implication

D0, D3, D4, and the ontic physical record predicate fix the episode, referents,
complete histories, and do/arm status independently of response success. This
matches the sealed ontic reading of T0. It does not supply C5 evidence.

The governing-law step comes from REC being an ontic physical truth evaluated
under the actual pre-existing physical law; it does not come from D4 alone.
If “governing theory fixed” were instead read as a demanded named, complete, or
authenticated theory packet, that extra packet would not follow and T0—not
T1—would be the first missing clause. The CTS lane explicitly chose facts of the
physical episode and separated C5 authentication, so RCTS1 uses the same sealed
ontic reading and states the alternative reading openly.

**Finding:** T0 follows at ontic scope only.

## 3. CTC normalization and novelty

The loop kernel is row-stochastic. Its unique distributional fixed-point law is
\((3/4,1/4)\), with contraction eigenvalue \(1/2\). The event label is not sent
around the loop. The writer maps one common blank to \(B=X\); the CTC bit is an
independent controller. The carrier is held unchanged. The query receives no
direct \(X\) argument and retains both possible loop outcomes.

The joint query histories for the two arms have disjoint support. Thus positive
contrast is not produced by postselection. The record remains valid even if the
loop controller is treated as nuisance.

The declared semantics is Deutsch-style consistency of the loop marginal, not
an ordinary acyclic joint trajectory for one sampled loop bit.

**Finding:** normalized coherent distributional generalized-causal record, not
a bootstrap label or an ordinary stochastic-trajectory claim.

## 4. T1 no-go

The physical support has both \(W\to Q\) and \(Q\to W\). T1 requires \(W\) past
and \(Q\) future. The reverse controller channel then crosses future to past.
It cannot be hidden inside one permitted indefinite-order module because that
module would contain both endpoints that T1 requires on opposite sides.

The argument uses chronology violation, not an infinite material taxonomy or an
incomplete numerical model. It proves non-implication on the current domain.
It does not assert empirical CTC existence.

**Finding:** T1 is the first failed implication.

## 5. Later-clause calibration

The no-go at T1 is already decisive. The lane separately shows that the record
predicate contains no standard sewing, full joint separator, or global
future-provenance axiom. Failure of derivation is not the same as an ontic
counterexample in every standard theory.

A hidden bypass defeats a proposed small separator but can be included in a
larger one. No claim is made that ordinary classical, finite quantum, or
fixed-background AQFT records lack T4.

**Finding:** no inflated T4 or gravity no-go.

## 6. Re-entry theorem

Premises C, S, and J are physical and outcome-independent. C supplies T1, S
supplies T2--T3, and J supplies T4. T5 follows only because C places every
source occurrence before the cut, S gives local causal/Markov sewing, J contains
every crossing event-bearing port, and D3 keeps complete outcomes.

This is a conditional implication, not a proof that RECORD_FM supplies C, S, or
J.

**Finding:** exact minimal grouped re-entry premises.

## 7. Transpose audit

The transpose calculation is exact: it is positive/trace preserving on one
system and non-CP on an entangled extension. The lane does not call it a
standard quantum operation.

The diagonal basis mission is classically recodable because transposition
restricts to identity on \(\mathbb C^2\). Single-system tomography blocks that
specific recoding but does not, without operational-equivalence or locality
constraints, prove absence of all contextual classical hidden-state covers.
Allowing the ancillary witness invalidates the proposed standard-quantum
operation rather than producing a physical record counterexample.

**Finding:** correct secondary boundary; no OPCC overclaim.
