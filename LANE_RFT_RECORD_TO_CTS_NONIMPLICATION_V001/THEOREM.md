# Theorem and no-go for RECORD_FM to CTS

**Claim class:** exact implication audit and ontic countermodel

**Not claimed:** empirical existence of chronology violation, failure of T4 in
standard globally hyperbolic physics, an OPCC counterexample in standard quantum
mechanics, universal record admission, gravity coverage, or actualization

## 1. Imported record domain

For an independently specified finite-mission candidate, define

\[
 \operatorname{REC}(r)
 :=
 \bigl[r\in\mathfrak C_{\rm FM}\bigr]
 \land \operatorname{RECORD}_{\rm FM}(r).                \tag{1}
\]

Membership in \(\mathfrak C_{\rm FM}\) imports D0--D4:

1. physical source, target, writer, query, clocks, outputs, and roles are fixed
   before scoring;
2. the event-to-query operational region, interval, alternatives, contexts,
   protocols, and complete registered outcome alphabets are bounded or finite at
   the declared resolution;
3. the alleged new or derivative distinction, physical lineage, acquisition
   region, reference, and closure regime are fixed;
4. every registered failure and adaptive history remains in the response law;
   and
5. no later representation, quotient, decoder, or admission result defines the
   candidate.

The physical record predicate adds that an interaction in \(\mathcal W\) creates
the declared counterfactual predictive distinction in \(\mathcal R\), the
distinction survives the declared closure regime to a registered label-blind
query, and neither writer nor reader recreates the label. Its necessary
registered consequence is

\[
 \Delta_r>0.                                             \tag{2}
\]

## 2. Weak lineage consequence

Define \(\operatorname{WLF}(r)\), the weak lineage fact, by:

1. the source/event, write interaction, nominated carrier lineage, and query are
   actual referents of the same episode;
2. the write creates a new event-bearing distinction in that lineage;
3. at least one physical event-bearing continuation of the nominated lineage
   reaches the registered label-blind query under the declared closure regime;
4. the complete registered response has \(\Delta_r>0\); and
5. the writer and reader do not locally reconstruct the event label from a
   fitted response or postselected outcome.

### Theorem RCTS1

On the sealed definitions,

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{REC}(r)
 \Longrightarrow
 T0(r)\land\operatorname{WLF}(r).}                       \tag{3}
\]

Here T0 is read ontically, as it is in the sealed CTS lane: the actual physical
episode and governing physical facts are not selected by the score. Equation
(3) does not supply a surviving C5 authentication certificate.

This use of “governing physical facts” is exact but limited. It means the actual
law under which the physical episode occurs, not a theory name, complete model,
or empirical packet supplied by the claimant. REC is already an ontic physical
truth evaluated in that pre-existing law; the scored response cannot select or
create it. If T0 were instead redefined to demand that the candidate explicitly
submit and authenticate a complete governing-theory packet, D0--D4 would imply
only the same-episode/referent core and that strengthened T0 would be the first
missing clause. The sealed CTS lane expressly says T0--T5 are facts of the
physical episode and separates C5 authentication, so RCTS1 uses that same ontic
semantics.

### Proof

D0 fixes the physical episode and referents independently of the scored
response. D3 supplies the complete registered histories. D4 forbids
representation-success from defining the systems or roles. REC's ontic physical
truth is evaluated under the actual response-independent physical law, rather
than a law inferred from its score. The base record domain already distinguishes
an authorized \(\operatorname{do}(x)\) from an endogenous positive-probability
\(\operatorname{arm}(x)\). Under the preceding sealed ontic semantics, these are
T0.

The remaining five statements are the physical invariant defining RECORD_FM
and its necessary positive registered consequence (2). No factorization or
constructor is invoked. QED.

### Exact limitation

The phrase “survives to the query” supplies an oriented event-bearing lineage
fact. It does **not** quantify over every controller, nuisance, reference,
feedback path, boundary condition, or correlation in the full registered
ancestor process. D1 expressly says operational finiteness does not imply a
finite exact ancestor cover or a complete external-port census. Therefore WLF
is strictly weaker than CTS T1 and T4.

## 3. Theorem RCTS2 — T1 is the first non-implication

There exists a physically coherent finite stochastic consistency model
\(r_{\rm CTC}\) such that

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{REC}(r_{\rm CTC})
 \land T0(r_{\rm CTC})
 \land\neg T1(r_{\rm CTC}).}                             \tag{4}
\]

Consequently,

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{RECORD}_{\rm FM}
 \not\Longrightarrow
 \operatorname{CTS}.}                                   \tag{5}
\]

### CTC controller

Use an explicitly declared classical **distributional fixed-point CTC theory**.
A binary CTC register \(C\) is present at the query and writer interfaces of a
compact closed causal loop. The backward segment applies the row-stochastic
kernel

\[
 K=
 \begin{pmatrix}
  7/8 & 1/8\\
  3/8 & 5/8
 \end{pmatrix},                                         \tag{6}
\]

and the forward segment transports the register unchanged at the level of its
interface law. Deutsch-style closed-loop consistency requires a row law
\(\tau\) satisfying \(\tau K=\tau\). The unique solution is

\[
 \tau=(3/4,1/4),                                        \tag{7}
\]

because the nontrivial eigenvalue of \(K\) is \(1/2\).

Let the record carrier initially have a blank state \(\bot\). An authorized
source supplies \(X\in\{0,1\}\). At the writer,

\[
 \bot\longmapsto B=X,                                   \tag{8}
\]

while the independent loop bit passes through. The carrier then holds \(B\)
exactly. At the label-blind query,

\[
 Y=B\oplus C,\qquad H=(Y,C),                             \tag{9}
\]

and the same \(C\) returns through (6). The query has no \(X\)-argument.

For \(x=0\),

\[
 P(H=(0,0)\mid x)=3/4,\qquad
 P(H=(1,1)\mid x)=1/4,                                  \tag{10}
\]

whereas for \(x=1\),

\[
 P(H=(1,0)\mid x)=3/4,\qquad
 P(H=(0,1)\mid x)=1/4.                                  \tag{11}
\]

The supports are disjoint, so

\[
 D_{\rm TV}\!\left(P(H\mid0),P(H\mid1)\right)=1.         \tag{12}
\]

Even if \(C\) is not reported and only \(Y\) is retained, the total-variation
contrast is \(1/2\).

### Why it is a bona-fide record

The source, blank carrier, writer, loop controller, query, clocks, and complete
registered query outcomes are fixed independently. The compact mission and
alphabets are finite.
The write in (8) creates a new distinction from a common blank. The stable
carrier holds that distinction to the query. Equation (9) is label-blind, and
\(X=Y\oplus C\) is recovered without recreation or conditioning away an
outcome. Equations (6)--(7) give a positive normalized law with one unique
closed-loop distributional consistency state. Thus the episode is mathematically
and operationally coherent in the explicitly declared distributional CTC theory
and satisfies REC. This does not assert an ordinary joint Kolmogorov trajectory
in which one sampled bit simultaneously realizes a stochastic backward update
and a deterministic forward identity around an acyclic time parameter.

### Why T1 fails

The physical causal support contains both

\[
 W\longrightarrow Q
 \quad\text{through }B,
 \qquad
 Q\longrightarrow W
 \quad\text{through the closed }C\text{ channel}.        \tag{13}
\]

Any T1 partition must put every authorized write occurrence \(W\) on its past
side and the registered query \(Q\) on its future side. The second arrow in
(13) then runs from the future side back to the past side. No achronal Cauchy
hypersurface separates the two regions, and the CTC module cannot be placed
wholly on one macro-side because it contains both the named writer and the named
query. A later external log would define a different derivative-read mission;
it would not supply a cut for this \(r_{\rm CTC}\).

The countermodel is compact and normalized. Its failure is chronology, not
operational infinity or missing probabilities. QED.

### Physical ceiling

Equation (4) does not assert that a CTC exists in our universe. It proves a
model-theoretic non-implication on a record domain that did not assume chronology
protection, stable causality, or global hyperbolicity. If the intended universal
domain excludes such models, that exclusion must be added as an independent
physical premise and defended.

## 4. Clause-by-clause status after T1

Once (4) is established, later CTS clauses cannot rescue the universal
implication. They also do not follow separately from REC:

- **T2:** a physical record truth does not prove a well-posed standard-Borel
  initial-value law, finite quantum comb, algebraic time-slice isomorphism, or
  deterministic higher-order module.
- **T3:** RECORD_FM imposes no CP-under-ancilla or UCP/CPP composability axiom and
  no finite exact macro-module theorem.
- **T4:** the nominated carrier distinction is not a census of every apparatus,
  center, edge, memory, port, and joint correlation. Separate physical
  marginals do not prove a compatible full joint separator.
- **T5:** writer/reader noncreation and complete registered outcomes do not by
  themselves prove that every other downstream component has no source argument
  outside a cut or that one common conditional future object exists.

This is a failure of derivation, not a claim that each clause fails in ordinary
laboratory physics.

## 5. Conditional re-entry into CTS

The weakest missing physical content can be grouped without defining a record by
successful representation.

**Causal-cut premise C.** The complete registered ancestor process is stably
causal on a prospectively bounded mission domain and admits one
branch-independent event-before-query macro-cut. For spacetime theories the
relevant domain is globally hyperbolic; for finite operational theories the
actual incidence has an acyclic external frontier. This supplies T1.

**Standard-sewing premise S.** The actual theory on both sides of the cut has a
well-posed physical initial-value law and at least one existing K, W, A, or I
composability branch, with finitely many exact bounded macro-modules and
normalized/CP/UCP/CPP maps as appropriate. This supplies T2--T3.

**Joint-completeness premise J.** The cut carries one actual compatible
apparatus-inclusive law, state, algebraic state, or higher-order strategy
containing every crossing degree, port, constraint datum, and correlation. This
supplies T4.

Then

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{REC}(r)\land C(r)\land S(r)\land J(r)
 \Longrightarrow \operatorname{CTS}(r).}                \tag{14}
\]

### Proof of T5 under C, S, and J

C places every source occurrence before the cut. S supplies local causal
sewing. J includes every crossing path and external port. If a future module
still had an independent \(\operatorname{src}(x)\) argument, its physical input
would be another event-bearing path or port crossing the cut, contradicting J.
Thus the future conditional kernel, UCP map, or tester is common to the event
arms once conditioned on the complete separator and context. D3 retains all
outcomes. T5 follows. Together with RCTS1 and the stated premises, T0--T5 hold.
QED.

The new content in (14) is C, S, and J. It is not secretly derived from positive
record contrast.

## 6. Secondary theorem — transpose is a composability boundary, not OPCC no-go

Let the writer prepare

\[
 \rho_x=|x\rangle\langle x|,\qquad x\in\{0,1\},
\]

from a common blank, let the hold map be

\[
 T(\rho)=\rho^{\mathsf T},                               \tag{15}
\]

and read in the computational basis. Transposition is positive, trace
preserving, and fixes both \(\rho_0\) and \(\rho_1\). The declared basis value
is therefore held and read exactly in an isolated single-system positive-map
theory.

But \(T\) is not completely positive. For
\(\lvert\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2\),

\[
 (T\otimes{\rm id})(|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|)
 =\frac12 F,                                             \tag{16}
\]

where the swap \(F\) has eigenvalue \(-1\) on the antisymmetric vector. Equation
(16) has eigenvalue \(-1/2\). Thus the process is outside standard quantum T3,
and under the strict word “comb” it is already outside T2(2).

This does not refute OPCC. On the declared basis-only mission the physical
diagonal algebra is \(\mathbb C^2\), \(T\) restricts to the identity, and the
record has the classical K cover

\[
 X\longrightarrow C=X\longrightarrow Q=C.               \tag{17}
\]

A tomographically complete single-qubit declaration must include a state with a
nonzero Pauli-\(Y\) component, because transpose acts on Bloch coordinates as

\[
 (s_x,s_y,s_z)\longmapsto(s_x,-s_y,s_z).                 \tag{18}
\]

This distinguishes \(T\) from identity and blocks the two-state diagonal
recoding. It does not alone prove the absence of every contextual classical
hidden-state cover. Such a claim additionally needs frozen convex and
operational equivalences plus a preparation/measurement contextuality witness,
or an authenticated spacelike causal graph plus a Bell witness. Directly
allowing an entangled ancilla instead makes (16) nonpositive, so the proposed
hold ceases to be a coherent operation in standard quantum mechanics.

Therefore transpose is an exact boundary on the assumptions needed for T3. It
is not a clean standard-physics ontic counterexample to OPCC_FM.
