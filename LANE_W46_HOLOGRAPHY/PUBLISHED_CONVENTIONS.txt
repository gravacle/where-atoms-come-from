LANE W46 -- STORAGE vs LEGIBILITY. Region A is a rectangular block of plaquettes inside a larger
lattice. STORAGE = independent records A can hold = |A| - 1 (the W-42 law). LEGIBILITY = how many of
A's internal records an observer confined OUTSIDE A can separate, computed as the GF(2) rank of the
pairing <T,S> = |bd(T) INTERSECT bd(S)| mod 2 between A's records and outside-supported loops.
THE PAIRING IS BILINEAR, so the rank over ALL outside loops equals the rank over a BASIS of them --
the single outside plaquettes. The first version enumerated all subsets instead and was killed by
the OS (exit 137, out of memory); enumerating subsets was never necessary.
