================ THE ANSWER LEG 1 SETTLES ================
  There is ONE transport semigroup in play, generated by COR-F's T.  M_gamma is T^L.
  The CIRCUIT convention is a SCHEDULE on that generator, not a rival operator.
  The invisibility set is a SUBLATTICE of the clock lattice: {L_F | mF} x {L_C | mC}.
  The circuit ray lies inside it; the edge ray meets it only at multiples of lcm(L_F,L_C).
  OPERATIVE VARIABLE, NAMED: the CLOCK RAY -- equivalently, whether the record is read
  while a branch is MID-LOOP.  NOT 'the transport convention'.
