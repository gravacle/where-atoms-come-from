LANE W38 -- IS THE REGION GIVEN OR SELECTED? 3x3 patch, Z_2, physical dim 16, g2=0.01, gamma=0.5.
A REGION is a subset S of the 4 plaquettes; its BOUNDARY is the product of them (discrete Stokes),
and the boundary's link support is printed for every one of the 15 non-empty regions. Decay rates
come from the LINDBLADIAN SPECTRUM (row-major convention, per the erratum), as an overlap-weighted
mean of the left-eigenmode rates against each boundary operator. The sieve is then run with the
environment in SEVEN different places. Margin = runner-up rate / winner rate; a margin near 1.0
means NO selection, and is reported as such rather than as a winner.
