VERDICT (as data for the registrar; no register write from this lane)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
FALLOFF NOT FOUND.  The O-54 hypothesis SURVIVES the adversarial attempt on the corner
tier: every pure-Gamma two-region quantity measured is topological (constant or set by
a discrete homological datum), contact-only, or GROWS with earned separation.  The one
genuine separation law found is w_min = d exactly -- confinement character (cost grows
linearly, string-tension-like), the OPPOSITE of a falloff -- with degeneracy N_min
growing (entropy-like).  The adversarial find: the local-record cost SWITCHES
topologically (min(4, wrap weight)) with the connector's crossing type -- still not a
falloff (two-valued, d-independent within type, both values present at d=1).

other venues not excluded; the world tier's exponent-3 falloff (T42_B) remains the
coupling's contribution under the openness clause, never re-attributed to Gamma.
