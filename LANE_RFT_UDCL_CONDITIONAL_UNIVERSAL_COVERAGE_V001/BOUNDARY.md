# Boundary and falsifier for the typed U-DCL theorem

## 1. Independent ontic domain

The physical postulate ranges over

\[
 \mathfrak R^{\rm actual,bf}_{\rm FM},
\]

the actual bona-fide finite-mission record domain established by the existing
representation-independent `REC` criteria.  Formally, membership entails
`REC(r)`.  `DCL_phys`, a successful certificate, or Coverage-U success does not
define membership.  An admitted failure may not be removed from the domain.

`DCL_phys(r)` is an ontic physical existence claim.  `Cert_DCL(r;P)` is a
prospective evidential claim.  Noncertification, an old public outcome, or lack
of an untouched holdout does not by itself make `DCL_phys(r)` false; it makes an
empirical verdict unavailable.

## 2. Exact witness and its negation

A positive physical realization uses one common tuple

\[
 (b,G,{\cal I},{\cal P},H_G,Z_G,\Omega^b,
 \Phi^b_{<G},\Phi^b_{>G},{\cal J}^b,U^b),
 \qquad b\in\{K,W\},
\]

through every D1--D4 clause.  Branch, frontier, incidence, census, state,
history, maps, instruments, or future-input rules may not be mixed across
different candidate tuples.

Therefore a decisive negative composition witness must negate the full
existential.  Before scored-data access, a packet must freeze:

1. independent record admission, the physical mission, source/write and query
   supports, outcomes, settings, and actual incidence;
2. the complete apparatus/environment/boundary/charge/dressing/reference,
   history, correlation, and external-port census;
3. every allowed external frontier and one outcome-independent realization
   ledger covering each applicable typed K/W separator, state, map, instrument,
   locality, and provenance family; and
4. a checkable completeness theorem proving that no physically applicable
   typed family consistent with the frozen episode is absent from that ledger.

Without item 4, a negative fit is `NOT_TESTED`, not refutation.

## 3. Decisive falsifiers

One independently admitted actual bona-fide record refutes U-DCL if either:

- a proof from its complete authenticated incidence shows that no physically
  bounded branch-independent, no-return/no-bypass acyclic external frontier
  exists; or
- an exact separating theorem, or a finite-data witness with its frozen error
  grade, excludes the full closed union over every allowed frontier and every
  applicable typed K/W realization in the complete ledger.

If one category is independently and exclusively physically warranted, the
closed union may contain only that category.  Otherwise both non-excluded
applicable branches remain.

The following are not decisive:

- failure of one cut, branch, state ansatz, optimizer, or endpoint model;
- separate-marginal agreement or disagreement without the complete joint
  instrument law;
- negativity in one optional quasiprobability representation;
- an omitted outcome, fitted marginal, or endpoint-fitted channel;
- a hypothesized but unmeasured late port; or
- a mathematical countermodel not physically realized as an admitted record.

A census can be amended only by independently measured, ancestry-relevant
physical carrier/port evidence under a versioned refreeze.  The old packet is
preserved but superseded if the new evidence invalidates its completeness
premise; current and global status return to `NOT_TESTED` pending a fresh test.
An invented latent port cannot block an otherwise complete negative witness.

## 4. Confirmation asymmetry

One sound prospective certificate establishes `DCL_phys(r)` for that record and
declared resolution only.  No finite/open collection of successes proves
U-DCL.  A global positive status requires an independently closed, exhaustive,
nonempty actual-world domain and a certificate for every member.

## 5. Separate programs

Coverage-U is a physical-cover and factorization result for an independently
admitted record.  It is not an outcome-selection theorem.  Nothing here derives
actualization, Born weights, A5, or reset.

U-DCL's primitive directed incidence is not a Lorentzian cone, metric,
curvature law, Einstein equation, or source law.  Gravity remains a separate
emergence program.
