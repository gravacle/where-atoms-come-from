== A1  THE LANE'S KERNEL, CHECKED AGAINST THE OPERATORS (I nearly called it a bug) ==
   The third pair term uses sin^2(pi k (alpha+BETA)), the SUM.  That is correct ONLY under
   the lane's own sign convention x = e^{-2pi i alpha}, y = e^{+2pi i beta} (x = conj(W_F)).
   Under the opposite convention it would be the DIFFERENCE and every rank-1 row would be
   wrong.  Tested here from the operators, both ready states, 200 random connections:
   max |  |Z_k|_operators  -  |Z_k|_lane-kernel  | over 200x2x60 = 24000 cells : 1.137e-12
   max (S - 1) over the same cells (the lane clamps S at 1; a WRONG third term would
   drive S above 1 and the clamp would hide it): 0.000e+00  -> the clamp never binds.
   VERDICT: the kernel is RIGHT.  Recorded because I tested it expecting it to be wrong.
