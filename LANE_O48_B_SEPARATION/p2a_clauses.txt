[A3] EXACT STRUCTURAL STATEMENT (an argument, not a trend).  Every record operator Zr_i enters H
     only as Zr_i, so [H, Zr_i] = 0 for ANY parameters: clause (ii) is automatic and H is block
     diagonal over the record configuration z.  Clause (iv) is the one at risk.  The global flip
     U = X^(rec) tensor X^(med) sends Zr_i -> -Zr_i and Za_i -> -Za_i, so it preserves the XaXa
     and YaYa terms and preserves Zr_i Za_i, but sends the field term -mu Za_i -> +mu Za_i.
     Hence mu = 0 is exactly the condition under which that flip is admissible.  In fermion
     language mu is the mediator's CHEMICAL POTENTIAL and the flip is PARTICLE-HOLE conjugation:
     clause (iv) is the demand that the mediator sit at HALF FILLING.  The search in [A2] tests
     this over the WHOLE Pauli group, not just that one candidate.
