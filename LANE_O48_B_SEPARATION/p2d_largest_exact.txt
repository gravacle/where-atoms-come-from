READ (filled from the numbers above): EVERY ODD ORDER IS EXACTLY 0.000000 at every g.  That is
      not luck: the admissible writer found by search in p2a is the global flip, so E0(-z) = E0(z)
      identically and all odd Walsh coefficients vanish.  Clause (iv) is what makes the record-record
      energy purely even.  The two-body term carries 99.999431 % of the non-constant weight at
      g=0.02, 99.981081 % at g=0.05, 99.650918 % at g=0.10, 95.985319 % at g=0.20 and 94.560371 %
      at g=0.40, the remainder sitting almost entirely in the FOUR-body coefficients.  So the
      pairwise description of E0(z) is essentially complete in the weak-coupling regime where the
      power law lives, and starts to leak at strong coupling -- which is the same place [B8] found
      the tail collapsing.  The control puts 100.000000 % in order 2, so the decomposition is not
      manufacturing the four-body weight.
