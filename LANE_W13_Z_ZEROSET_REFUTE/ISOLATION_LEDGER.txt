LANE W-13 / Z_REFUTE -- ISOLATION LEDGER
One line per comparison.  HELD FIXED | THE ONE THING THAT MOVES | WHERE | ARMS DIFFED.
W-08's isolation audit says the commonest FATAL defect in this program is not "two variables
moved" but "ZERO variables moved", and that a ledger records only what an author INTENDED, so it
is evidence of nothing.  This ledger therefore prints, for every comparison, the measured
difference between the arms' INPUTS and their OUTPUTS, and marks explicitly which entries are
NOT controlled comparisons.

MY OWN CONFOUND, RECORDED RATHER THAN PATCHED OUT (r1(e)):
   My first ROUTE-4 root count used clustering radius 1e-6 with Newton tolerance 1e-13, and the
   ONE stratum over-counted in 28 of 1994 states (up to 12 'zeros' where there is one).  IT IS A
   RESOLUTION ARTEFACT OF EXACTLY THE COR-E CLASS: at a TANGENTIAL zero |P| ~ q r^2, so
   {|P| < 1e-13} is a blob of diameter ~ sqrt(1e-13/q) ~ 1e-6 -- the clustering radius itself.
   Both radii are printed side by side (1e-6: 28 mismatches; 1e-4: 0).  Note which way it fails:
   the artefact appears ONLY on the stratum lane Z's theorem calls degenerate and NEVER on the
   TWO stratum, so it is an unplanned second confirmation of the degeneracy.  The load-bearing
   reading -- no state anywhere with three or more isolated zeros -- is identical at both radii.
