  READ — from the numbers above

  THE CENSUS WAS RIGHT, AND THE PERTURBATION MUST BE GENERIC. A single-site Z only PARTIALLY
  lifts the degeneracy -- [4,8,4] becomes [4,4,4,4] and v_2(4) is still 2, so the count
  survives. A GENERIC splitting collapses every multiplicity to 1 and the count falls from
  2 to 0. The degeneracy-dependent chain -- C-14's count law and W-42/43/44/51 --
  rests on a symmetry the world does not have.

  AND THE AMENDED DEFINITION ALREADY CARRIES THE FIX. Clause (ii') gives a durability width;
  eigenvalues closer than it are indistinguishable to a record that need only last t_m.
  Clustering within a stated width recovers the symmetric count exactly whenever the
  splitting is inside that width — no new machinery, and the tolerance is declared, not fitted.
