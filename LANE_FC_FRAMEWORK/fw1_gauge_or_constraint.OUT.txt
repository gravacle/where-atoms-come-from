== ARM A — LOCAL GAUGE CONSTRAINT (Z_2 gauge theory, what we have been doing) ==
  gauge: system = link 0, fragment = its Gauss cut dim   16   I(S:F)/H(S) = 1.000000  1.000000  1.000000   

== ARM B — GLOBAL CONSERVATION CONSTRAINT (no gauge law, no links, no vertices) ==
  spins: fixed total magnetization M = 1       dim  126   I(S:F)/H(S) = 2.000000  2.000000  2.000000   (fragment = ALL the rest)
  spins: fixed total magnetization M = 3       dim   84   I(S:F)/H(S) = 2.000000  2.000000  2.000000   (fragment = ALL the rest)

== AND THE STRUCTURAL COMPARISON THAT DECIDES IT ==
  gauge:  knowing the flux on a CUT determines the link it encloses  -> LOCAL, a surface
  spins:  knowing the magnetization of the COMPLEMENT determines the region -> GLOBAL, no surface
  Both give a non-factorizing algebra. Only one of them makes the determining set a SURFACE.
  THAT is what gauge buys, and it is the only thing it buys here.
