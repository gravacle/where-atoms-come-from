[1] CASINI, HUERTA, ROSABAL -- "Remarks on entanglement entropy for gauge fields"
    arXiv:1312.1183, submitted 4 Dec 2013, Phys. Rev. D 89, 085012 (2014).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED, and the brief's paraphrase is broadly right but incomplete in a way that matters.

What the paper actually says, in order:
  (a) In gauge theories the constraints obstruct writing the global Hilbert space as a tensor
      product across complementary regions.  Algebraically this is the presence of a CENTRE in
      the gauge-invariant algebra of a finite region.
  (b) A UNIQUE ENTROPY CAN BE ASSIGNED TO AN ALGEBRA WITH CENTRE.  This is the half of the paper
      the brief omits, and it is decisive for the round: given the algebra, the entropy is
      DETERMINATE.  What is not determinate is the correspondence ALGEBRA <-> REGION.
  (c) It is always possible, in many different ways, to choose local algebras with TRIVIAL centre
      for any region, giving a genuine entanglement entropy.  Those choices are in correspondence
      with MAXIMAL TREES OF LINKS ON THE BOUNDARY, which the authors interpret as PARTIAL GAUGE
      FIXINGS.
  (d) In the CONTINUUM LIMIT the ambiguities are boundary-local terms, so that relative entropy
      and mutual information are finite, universal and gauge-independent.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF.  The brief describes this as "a live, unsettled problem".  Points (b)
and (d) are the authors' own resolution of it: the ambiguity is in the QUESTION, not the ANSWER,
and it cancels in the quantities they regard as physical.  What remains genuinely open is which
algebra deserves the name "the region", and that is a question about physical input, not about
mathematics.  This lane's k2b measured (b) exhaustively -- 67 admissible algebras, 41 distinct
determinate entropies -- and confirms it.

CAVEAT THIS LANE ADDS.  (d) is a CONTINUUM statement.  On a finite lattice there is no continuum
limit to take, and k2b block 2.3 finds mutual information between complementary regions as
ambiguous as the entropy (spread 3.998776 bits at g^2 = 0.10).  Nothing in CHR predicts otherwise
for touching regions on a lattice.  Do not import (d) to a finite carrier.

[2] DONNELLY -- "Decomposition of entanglement entropy in lattice gauge theory"
    arXiv:1109.0036.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED.  The entropy of an arbitrary state decomposes into three non-negative terms: a Shannon
entropy of the distribution over boundary representations; a term depending on the DIMENSIONS of
those representations, present only for NON-ABELIAN groups; and a term of nonlocal correlations.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS PROGRAM, AND IT IS A HARD ONE.  Z_2 IS ABELIAN.  Every irrep is
one-dimensional, so the middle term is IDENTICALLY ZERO on every carrier this program has ever
used.  The "edge mode / log dim R" content that motivates the extended-Hilbert-space literature
DOES NOT EXIST IN Z_N.  Any Z_N result presented as exhibiting edge-mode entropy is exhibiting
the zero of that term.

[3] DONNELLY, WALL -- "Entanglement entropy of electromagnetic edge modes"
    arXiv:1412.1895, Phys. Rev. Lett. 114, 111603 (2015).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED.  The vacuum entanglement entropy of Maxwell theory contains a term with no known
statistical interpretation; the authors identify it as the entanglement entropy of EDGE MODES --
classical solutions determined by the electric field normal to the entangling surface -- obtained
by extending the Hilbert space with degrees of freedom on the entangling surface.  This resolves
a puzzle about logarithmic divergences of gauge fields in 3+1 dimensions.

CORRECTION TO A PARAPHRASE THIS LANE ITSELF MADE AND THEN RETRACTED (see k2b block 2.4).  The
extended-Hilbert-space construction SPLITS each boundary link into two half-links carrying matched
edge modes.  It is NOT the same as assigning a whole boundary link to one side, and the two do not
agree numerically.  Filed as this lane's own instance of identification-by-headline-paraphrase.

[4] TYPE III_1 FOR LOCAL ALGEBRAS IN QFT -- Buchholz, D'Antoni, Fredenhagen;
    uniqueness of the hyperfinite III_1 factor by Connes and Haagerup.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED.  Under mild assumptions (Reeh-Schlieder for the vacuum, approximate locality,
Poincare covariance), the local algebra of a bounded region in QFT is isomorphic to THE UNIQUE
HYPERFINITE TYPE III_1 FACTOR.  The structure is universal: free electrodynamics, interacting
Yang-Mills and any other reasonable Poincare-covariant theory all give the same abstract factor.

A type III factor admits no non-zero normal semifinite trace, hence no density matrices, hence
von Neumann entropy is UNDEFINED rather than divergent.  The brief states this correctly.

[5] WITTEN -- "Gravity and the crossed product", arXiv:2112.12828.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED.  Abstract, in substance: Leutheusser and Liu identified an emergent Type III_1 algebra
in large-N N=4 super Yang-Mills; including 1/N corrections turns it into a Type II_infinity
algebra, which is the crossed product of the Type III_1 algebra by its modular automorphism
group.  In that setting the entropy of a black hole state is "well-defined up to an additive
constant, independent of the state", which Witten calls somewhat analogous to classical entropy.

CORRECTION TO THE BRIEF, AND IT IS A REAL ONE.  The brief says "including gravity ... yields a
TYPE II algebra".  What actually enters is (i) 1/N corrections in a specific holographic setting,
and (ii) the crossed product by the MODULAR AUTOMORPHISM GROUP, which is forced because the boost
/ ADM generator must be an operator of the algebra.  "Gravity" is not a general input to the
theorem; a constraint tying the algebra to a global generator is.  Also: "well-defined up to an
additive constant" means RENORMALISED, not finite -- the divergence is absorbed into the constant,
not removed.

[6] CHANDRASEKARAN, LONGO, PENINGTON, WITTEN -- "An algebra of observables for de Sitter space"
    arXiv:2206.10780, JHEP 02 (2023) 082.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED.  The algebra of the static patch, with operators gravitationally dressed to an
OBSERVER'S WORLDLINE, is of Type II_1 and has a natural entropy.  Restricting the observer to
positive (or bounded-below) energy is implemented by a projection in the crossed product, and
Type II_1 has a MAXIMAL entropy state.  The entropy of a semiclassical state agrees with the
expected generalized entropy up to a state-independent additive constant.

NOTE.  The observer is not decoration.  Without an added degree of freedom to dress to, the
construction does not go through.  That is the piece the brief's question (3) is about, and k3
answers it: in finite dimensions the added degree of freedom factorises off (Skolem-Noether), so
the finite analogue of this step is empty.

[7] THE LITERATURE THE BRIEF DID NOT CITE, AND WHERE W-19'S BIT ACTUALLY LIVES.
    Casini, Huerta, Magan, Pontello -- "Entropic order parameters for the phases of QFT",
    arXiv:2008.11748.  Also Casini, Magan, Martinez, arXiv:2110.02980.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERIFIED and DIRECTLY ON POINT.  These papers analyse exactly two properties of the net of
algebras attached to regions: ADDITIVITY (is the algebra of a union the join of the algebras of
the parts?) and HAAG DUALITY.  Different types of generalized symmetry are associated with the
BREAKING of these properties on regions of different topology.  The existence of an area-law order
parameter -- the Wilson loop in the confining phase, the 't Hooft loop in the dual Higgs phase --
is shown to IMPLY more than one possible choice of algebra for the same underlying theory, and an
entropic order parameter arises from that non-uniqueness.

THIS IS THE CORRECT HOME FOR W-19'S ONE FULL BIT.  k4 shows the bit is exactly the failure of
ADDITIVITY: A(S u F) strictly contains A(S) v A(F), the excess being the Wilson loop of the cycle
inside S u F, and the coupling dependence W-19 read as a phase index is the coupling dependence of
<W_loop>, i.e. confinement versus deconfinement.  It is an order parameter for the Z_2 one-form
symmetry.  It is a determinate structural fact about gauge theory.  It is NOT the centre
ambiguity, it is NOT gauge-variance, and it has NOTHING to do with von Neumann type.

[8] THE ONE CLAIM THAT NEEDS NO SOURCE, AND SETTLES THE ROUND.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Artin-Wedderburn: every finite-dimensional von Neumann algebra is a finite direct sum of matrix
algebras.  It is therefore ALWAYS type I, ALWAYS has a faithful normal trace, ALWAYS has minimal
projections, and NEVER has the "entropy undefined" problem.  k1 exhibits the decomposition, a
minimal projection and a trace for every region algebra it builds, rather than citing this.

Skolem-Noether: every automorphism of a finite-dimensional central simple algebra is inner.  The
entire content of the crossed-product construction is that modular flow on a type III_1 factor is
OUTER.  Finitely it cannot be.  k3 exhibits the collapse: A x|_sigma Z_N = A (x) C^N, dimension
64 = 16 x 4 measured by explicit closure, with an explicit untwisting unitary.
