  READ
====================================================================================================
  Transport between records requires TWO records; no single record has it. It is the IDENTITY
  for every abelian group, so on a Z_2 carrier a record taken around another comes back
  UNCHANGED (0 of 4), the order never matters (0 of 8), and no additional enclosed record
  changes anything (0 at every k).

  THAT SINGLE FACT EXPLAINS THE PROGRAM'S ENTIRE NEGATIVE RECORD ON THIS QUESTION. Every
  carrier built here has gauge group Z_2. The nulls were not evidence against emergence; they
  were measured where the effect is identically zero by construction.

  On S_3: the record COMES BACK CHANGED (18 of 36); the ORDER of encirclement MATTERS (54 of 216);
  and EACH ADDITIONAL enclosed record changes the outcome in half of all sequences
  (108/216, 648/1296, 3888/7776). Path-dependent transport between records is PRESENT.

  WHICH SIGNATURES ACTUALLY DISCRIMINATE
  signature                                  Z_2           S_3    discriminates?
  record comes back changed                  0/4         18/36               YES
  order of encirclement matters              0/8        54/216               YES
  each added record contributes              0/8       108/216               YES
  closure fraction                           2/4          6/36                no
  last record determined                     [1]           [1]                no

  Closure holds at fraction 1/|G| on BOTH carriers and the last record is determined on BOTH,
  so neither is an emergence signature -- both are properties of being a group at all.

  WHAT IS NOT HERE: a MONOTONE density law. The reachable outcomes are bounded by the
  conjugacy class, |class| = 3 on S_3, so 'more records -> more transport' cannot be seen on a
  group this small. That is H-4's small-carrier warning, and it is the next thing to measure.
