  READ -- from the numbers above
========================================================================================================
  THE GAP IS LARGE. Of the operators that are a bit, commute with H, and are NOT a function of H
  -- clauses (i), (ii) and (iii) -- clause (iv) as derived keeps only 16 of 240 at n=3 and 772 of
  199204 at n=4. It DISCARDS 93% and 99.6% of them.

  WHAT IT DISCARDS ARE OPERATORS CORRELATED WITH H WITHOUT BEING A FUNCTION OF H. Their block
  imbalance |Tr(P_E R)| is 2 throughout: not a function of H, and not uncorrelated with it either.
  The framework's stated reason for clause (iii) -- 'if R = f(H) its value is fixed by the energy'
  -- rules out only the FUNCTION case. The derived form of clause (iv) rules out ALL correlation.

  BUT THE GAP TURNS OUT NOT TO BE THE PLACE TO LOOK. O-47 shows configuration energy and free
  energy-conserving writers coexist WITHOUT entering this gap at all: on H = Z1 Z2 both records
  satisfy clause (iv) exactly, with Tr(P_E R) = [0,0], and the energy sits in their CORRELATION,
  which clause (iv) never constrains. No clause has to be weakened and nothing imported.

  So this lane's measurement stands as a fact about the two conditions -- they are genuinely
  different and the difference is large -- and it is NOT the route to a configuration energy.
