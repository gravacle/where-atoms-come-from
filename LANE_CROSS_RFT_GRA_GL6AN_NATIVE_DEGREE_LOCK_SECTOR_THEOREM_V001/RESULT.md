# Result

The F3 degree-lock direction is algebraically exact and has a nonempty strict
inherited subfamily, but it is not an automatic Ward identity.

On `r_epsilon=-6*r_U`, the F3 Hamiltonian completes to

\[
 H=-h\sum_eX_e+U_d\sum_v(k_v-2)^2+C.
\]

Because `epsilon_star=Delta+2*U_d*(1-2*d_star)` is inherited, a positive
`U_d` point is in the strict `Delta>0` domain exactly when
`Delta=4*U_d*(d_star-2)` with `d_star>2`; `d_star=3, Delta=4*U_d` is an exact
witness.  The `d_star=2` realization would instead be the `Delta=0` boundary.

This proves that the GL6AL E-sector clue was pointing toward a genuine native
constraint.  In the strict degree-two sector, the six local pair observables
have only two independent variations, exactly the `E` representation.  `A1`
is fixed and `T2` is absent.  A uniform local locked ensemble has exact
connected covariance `(A1,E,T2)=(0,8/3,0)`.

The same calculation also gives a sharp no-go.  At finite `h`,

\[
 [H,k_v-2]=-ih\sum_{e\ni v}Y_e,
\]

and no nontrivial linear combination of the local degrees is conserved.
Thus neither an exact continuity equation nor a Ward identity follows from
degree locking alone.

There are nevertheless two controlled collective indications.  The native
incidence quadratic form has two exact flat directions at every generic
translation character and one additional constraint-Gram eigenvalue (squared
singular value) that vanishes quadratically at the trivial character.  More
strongly, on the positive
strong-lock ray, distinct exact locked configurations are mixed by the
inherited transverse dynamics.  On the verifier's explicit period-four

\[
 H_{\rm eff}^{(2)}=-{M h^2\over2U_d}P,
 \qquad

The first
off-diagonal process is a native alternating hexagon and its exact leading

\[
 -{63\over8}{h^6\over U_d^5}.

This is a rigorous multi-link collective process enabled on the selected
