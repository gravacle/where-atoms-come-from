## 1. THE FOUR AXES

Any candidate answer fixes four things. The register's failures each sit on one axis, and naming
them separates failures of the CANDIDATE from failures of the AXIS.

| axis | the question it answers | visited | unvisited |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CARRIER** — what the record lives on | where | K1 (inert; no dynamics). `Z_N` gauge graphs to `L=21` | matter coupled to gauge; continuum; **a surface as the carrier rather than as a cut** |
| **DYNAMICS** — what evolves | how it changes | none in attempt one (transport by hand, clock by stipulation). `Z_N` Hamiltonian with coupling `g^2` in attempt two | charge sectors; matter; dissipative/driven |

| **CRITERION** — what makes it a record | why it counts | durability/non-recurrence (**both inferences invalid**, W-08, W-17); redundancy `R_delta` (**Gauss-forced**, W-19); SBS | **area vs volume scaling**; kinetic-proofreading cost; **existence-by-geometry** |

**THE PATTERN OF FAILURE, STATED ONCE.** Attempt one failed on the DYNAMICS axis — it ran spectral
arguments on an object with no spectrum. Attempt two's first computation failed on the CRITERION
axis — it applied a redundancy criterion to a theory whose algebra does not factorize, so the
criterion had nothing to stand on and returned a theorem. **Both failures are inherited machinery
not fitting the surface.** That is the evidence behind "established rules may not apply here": it is
not a licence, it is a measured pattern with two instances.

## 2. THE REGIONS, AND WHAT IS KNOWN OF EACH

**R-I — THE RECORD IS WRITTEN BY A PROCESS ONTO A CARRIER.** Attempt one. **Visited and closed.**

which boundary degrees of freedom constitute the record is **not derived anywhere in the
literature.** W-19's finding is the first datum in this region: the algebra choice is a **one full
bit** disagreement in the magnetic phase and **exactly zero** in the electric one, **and the
coupling decides which.**

**R-IV — THE RECORD IS PAID FOR.** Durability bought by dissipation — kinetic proofreading, Landauer,
Bennett. **UNVISITED. Corpus contact zero on every term.** This is the only region that supplies an

## 3. THE PRINCIPAL'S HYPOTHESIS, STATED SO IT CAN FAIL

> **H-SURFACE.** The record is not written onto a carrier by a process. **The record IS the boundary
> data of a surface, and its content is fixed by the geography of the field's relationship to that
> surface.** Formation is then not an event but a consequence: given a surface and a flux, the record
> exists.

**What would make it TRUE, testably:** the record content depends on the surface (its topology, its
geometry, the flux through it) **and not on the dynamics that produced the state.** Concretely, on
W-19's own machinery: hold the surface fixed and vary the state, the coupling, and the history —
if the record content does not move, it is geographic. **Note carefully: that is the same shape as
the Gauss identity W-19 just convicted.** So H-SURFACE must be tested where the Gauss law does *not*
already force the answer — **the magnetic phase at small `g^2`, on a system region carrying a cycle**,
which is exactly where W-19 measured the one-bit disagreement and exactly what its next step builds.

**What would make it FALSE:** the record content moves when the state or the history moves, with the
surface held fixed. **That falsifier does not exist for the different-shape hypothesis generally**
(standing correction) — but it **does** exist for H-SURFACE, and that is what makes H-SURFACE worth
running rather than believing.

**WHAT IS COINED HERE AND MUST CARRY A FALSIFIER.** "Surface" is not yet defined in this program's
own terms. On a graph it can mean: a vertex cut, an edge cut, the boundary of a region of links, or
the set of plaquettes bounding a region. **These are different objects and they will give different
answers.** The first work in R-III is to fix which one, DERIVED from the carrier rather than coined.

## 4. STEPPING OUTSIDE ESTABLISHED RULES — WHAT IT MEANS OPERATIONALLY

The instinct is earned (§1). The discipline that keeps it from becoming licence is already adopted:

1. **Name the variable.** An import matters only if it acts on something the observable can see
   (W-14). Two of this program's dead ends were imports acting on variables nothing could see.
2. **Every departure from an established rule is a COINED assumption and carries a falsifier**, or it
   is not entered. The standing correction records that same-function/different-shape has **no
   falsifier yet** and is therefore never scored — H-SURFACE, by contrast, has one.
3. **The frame challenge runs at route decisions** (R-2): does either branch obtain, and is the
   binary a sample of a space.
4. **No route closes without a next step** (R-3).

**What this explicitly does NOT license:** discarding a rule because it is inconvenient, or because
the answer would be nicer without it. Both failures in §1 were found by holding the rules and
watching them not fit — **not** by assuming they did not apply.
