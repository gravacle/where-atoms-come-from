# GL6AW self-audit

## Exactness attacks

1. **Sector nonemptiness:** an explicit union of two perfect matchings has
   `N_0=V/2` whenever the twist period is even.
2. **Component scope:** the twist is diagonal and therefore preserves every
   component span.  Orthogonality uses component translation stability; if
   stability fails, the theorem states ground-component degeneracy instead.
3. **PF scope:** no equal-amplitude or graph-regularity premise is used;
   `0<=<T_c><=1` follows from positive PF entries and operator norm.
4. **Term count:** only triples `{0,1,2}` and `{0,1,3}` change the twisted
   port-zero `x1` coordinate, giving exactly `2V` affected terms.
5. **Wrap scope:** a displacement `L1-1` has the opposite unit phase to a
   displacement one; the energy uses the same cosine.
6. **Character arithmetic:** `N_0/L1=L0L2/2` is half-integral exactly when
   the transverse area is odd.
7. **Følner scope:** every period tends to infinity and boundary/volume tends
   to zero, but the aspect ratio diverges and is not called isotropic.

## Promotion attacks

1. Finite component-gap closure is not called selected-GNS gaplessness.
2. The `-1` total twist character is not called a small-character mode.
3. No structure-factor lower bound, isolated pole, or dispersion is inferred.
4. The centered port sector is not asserted to be the actual all-sector
   physical ground.
5. Higher-order stability of the pure order-six Hamiltonian is not claimed.
6. No physical momentum, cone, stress/Ricci law, gravity, or `G` is claimed.
