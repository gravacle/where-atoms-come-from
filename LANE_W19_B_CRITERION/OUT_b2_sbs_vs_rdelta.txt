     held fixed.  A sweep that reports 'a record' must say WHICH criterion and WHICH cut.

========================================================================================================
  CLAIM.  On theta_L the single-link Gauss operator G = prod_l X_l anticommutes with Z_0 and
  with Y_0.  For any PHYSICAL rho (G rho G = rho),  <Z_0> = Tr(rho Z_0) = Tr(rho G Z_0 G)
  = -<Z_0>, so <Z_0> = 0, and likewise <Y_0> = 0.  Hence rho_{link 0} = (I + <X_0> X)/2 for
  EVERY physical state: its eigenbasis is the ELECTRIC (X) basis, or is degenerate.
  CONSEQUENCE.  Korbicz's SBS reads the pointer basis off rho_S.  On a gauged link that
