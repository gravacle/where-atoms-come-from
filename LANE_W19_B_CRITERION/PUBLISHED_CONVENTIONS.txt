INSTRUMENT, AND WHY IT SHOULD BE BELIEVED
Algebra entropies for any *-subalgebra generated by Pauli strings, via symplectic Gram-Schmidt over
GF(2): A = 2^c blocks of M_2^k, S_A = H({p_s}) + sum_s p_s S(sigma_s).  Every algebra choice below
is ONE GENERATOR LIST fed to the SAME function, so arms differ by exactly one printed object.
b0 checks the machinery against two independent routes (reduced density matrices; Shannon entropy
of the measured X-distribution) on 3 carriers -- PASSED after it caught two real bugs of mine
(a partial-trace bit-order inversion, and a wrong GF(2) back-substitution).
b4 PART 0 reproduces the sealed LANE_T1_NEW_PROGRAM null to 6 decimals:
   I(1:2) = 0.690763   I(1:3) = 0.384496   I(1:{2,3}) = 1.075259.

(1) THE CRITERION DISCRIMINATES.  b1, carrier theta_8, S = link 0, E = links 1..7, ONLY the state
moves (carrier spec sha256 82a5288191...).  Extended-Hilbert-space I(S:F), delta = 0.1:
   magnetic GHZ         H(S)=1.000000  R_delta=7/7  plateau defect 0.000e+00  spread 0.000000
   electric GHZ         H(S)=1.000000  R_delta=7/7  defect 0.000e+00
   Bell(link0,link1)    H(S)=1.000000  R_delta=1/7  defect 0.714286  spread 2.000000
   Haar physical x3     H(S)~0.997     R_delta=0/7  defect 0.967..0.980
The scrambled arm COULD have plateaued -- the identical estimator returns a flat curve on the
broadcast arm -- and did not.  SEPARATION 7 vs 0.

(2) TWO WAYS R_delta IS TRIVIALLY SATISFIED, BOTH EXHIBITED.
  (a) H(S) = 0.  |+>^L has H(S) = 0, so "I >= (1-delta)H(S)" reads 0 >= 0 and R_delta = 7 of 7 for
      a state with no information anywhere.  R_delta AS USUALLY WRITTEN NEEDS AN H(S) > 0 PRECONDITION.
  (b) R_delta is blind to HOW MUCH is recorded: the amplitude-0.99 electric GHZ has H(S) = 0.140879
      and also returns R_delta = 7/7, defect 0.000000.
  (c) The same trap is reachable by CHANGING THE ALGEBRA CHOICE at fixed state: on the magnetic GHZ
      the dressed choice C6 gives H_A(S) = 0.00000 and hence R_delta = 6/6 (b3 PART B).

(3) SBS AND R_delta ARE DIFFERENT CONDITIONS.  Three disagreements, b2, carrier theta_9,
S = link 0, fragments {1,2} {3,4} {5,6}, unobserved {7,8}:
  (i) STRONG INDEPENDENCE.  Constructed state QDNS (gauge invariant, 4 amplitudes) has
      R_delta = 3/3, I(S:F_k) = 1.000000 = H(S) for every k and every union, D_coh = 0.00e+00,
      D_orth = 0.00e+00, and D_prod = 0.875000.  QD holds, SBS fails, and the only failing clause
      is the one QD has no counterpart for.
  (ii) SBS NEEDS A TRACE-OUT.  Same GHZ state: with U = {7,8} traced out, SBS = YES; with all 8
      environment links observed, D_coh = 5.00e-01 and SBS = no, while R_delta goes 3/3 -> 4/4.
  (iii) GAUGE-SPECIFIC.  G = prod_l X_l anticommutes with Z_0 and Y_0, so every physical state has
      <Z_0> = <Y_0> = 0 (measured max 2.917e-17 and 2.841e-17 over 44 physical states; 0.085401 on
      unprojected controls).  rho_S = (I + <X_0>X)/2 ALWAYS, so the eigenbasis of rho_S is electric
      or degenerate.  SBS READS ITS POINTER BASIS OFF rho_S AND THEREFORE CANNOT CERTIFY A MAGNETIC
      RECORD ON A GAUGED LINK.  The pointer basis must be imported from outside the criterion.

(4) THE GAUGE PROBLEM.  YES, THE ANSWER DEPENDS ON THE CHOICE.  b3, theta_8, S = link 0, magnetic
GHZ (= the g^2 -> 0 ground state):
   C1 EXT   I(S:F) = 1.000000 at every fragment size, R_delta = 6/6, PLATEAU
   C5 MAG   identical to C1 (see the collapse theorem below)
   C3 FULL  I(S:F) = 0.000000 at every fragment size, R_delta = 0/6
   C2 ELEC  I(S:F) = 0.000000 at every fragment size, R_delta = 0/6
   C6 DRESS H_A(S) = 0.00000, vacuous
The same table on the ELECTRIC GHZ has every choice agreeing at 1.000000.  Choice-dependence is
state-dependent and bites exactly on the states whose record is magnetic -- which is the phase the
dynamics produces at small g^2.

(5) FOUR STRUCTURAL RESULTS THAT CONSTRAIN ANY FUTURE SWEEP.
  T1  MAGNETIC CONTENT = CYCLE SPACE.  A Pauli commutes with G_v iff z.G_v = 0, so the gauge-
      invariant Paulis supported in a link set R have z-part exactly the cycle space of R.  A_R is
      abelian (purely electric) iff R is a forest.  Verified against union-find cyclomatic numbers
      on 1762 link subsets over 6 carriers: 0 mismatches.
      COROLLARY: a single link -- any forest -- has NO localised gauge-invariant holonomy, on any
      carrier.  A magnetic record needs |S| >= girth.
  T2  C5 MAG COLLAPSES ONTO C1 EXT ON PHYSICAL STATES.  C5 keeps exactly the Gauss operators
      supported inside R; a physical rho_R lives in their +1 eigenspace; compressing, both algebras
      assign it the same state.  Measured max |S_MAG - S_EXT| = 6.661e-16 over 270 triples, versus
      0.287346 on unprojected controls.  The published choice list has FEWER independent members
      than it looks.
  T3  R_delta OVER-DEMANDS IN A GAUGE THEORY.  A_S^FULL for a system plaquette carries a magnetic
      bit AND an unrecordable electric centre bit.  On theta_10 the Wilson-loop-GHZ has
      I(S:F_k) = 1.000000 with H_A(S) = 2.000000, so R_delta = 0 on a state that plainly has a
      record.  Restricting to a DECLARED POINTER SUBALGEBRA alg{W_01} restores R_delta = 4/4.
  T4  JOIN =/= UNION.  A composite fragment's algebra is ambiguous: the join of its members'
      algebras or every gauge-invariant operator on its links.  On theta_12 at m = 3 plaquettes the
      entropies are 5.822326 vs 5.274058 and I(W_01:F) is 0.177383 vs 0.614049 on a Haar state --
      but 1.000000 vs 1.000000 on the Wilson-loop GHZ.  THE AMBIGUITY IS INVISIBLE ON EXACTLY THE
      CONSTRUCTED BROADCAST STATES ONE WOULD USE TO DEMONSTRATE A PLATEAU.

(6) THE THRESHOLD.  Two answers, because (4) means the question has two readings.
  READING 1, extended Hilbert space:  SIX LINKS.  theta_6, S = 1 link, E = 5 links, cut S|E,
     I(S:F) = 1.000000 at |F| = 1,2,3,4 (every fragment, min = max) then 2.000000 at |F| = 5.
     R_delta = 5/5.  TIGHT: plateau points = L - |S| - 1 <= L - 2 for a pure global state on ANY
     graph, so four points force L >= 6.  L = 3,4,5 give 1,2,3 points (theta_3 = the sealed T1 null).
     CAVEAT: the bound uses purity of the global state; a mixed global state has no final jump and
     could reach four points at L = 5.
  READING 2, gauge-invariant algebra with a declared pointer:  TWELVE LINKS on a girth-2 graph.
     theta_12, S = plaquette {0,1}, pointer W_01, five plaquette fragments; H(W_01) = 1.000000,
     I = 1.000000 at 1,2,3,4 fragments, R_delta = 5/5, plateau defect 0.000e+00.  Haar control on
     the same carrier and cut: R_delta = 0/5, defect 0.996129.  On a girth-3 carrier: 18 links.
  AND THE COUPLING IS PART OF THE THRESHOLD.  theta_8 held fixed, g^2 swept: plateau defect
     0.000072 (g^2=0.10), 0.005806 (0.30), 0.042978 (0.50), 0.085033 (0.60), 0.147283 (0.70).
     R_delta = 7 through g^2 = 0.60 and 0 from g^2 = 0.70.  The plateau is a property of
     (carrier, coupling, state), never of the carrier alone.

CONFOUNDS AND LIMITS OF THIS LANE, RECORDED RATHER THAN FIXED
  * C5 MAG is not an independent arm on physical states (T2), and on theta it is not even a
    different generator list.  Reported anyway, marked, and proved.
  * C3 FULL and C2 ELEC coincide for any forest region -- so they are the SAME ARM whenever the
    region is a single link.  They separate only from 2-link regions upward (theta_8 HAAR,
    |F| = 2: 0.01813 vs 0.01069).
  * theta_L is degenerate as a graph: 2 vertices, every pair of links a plaquette, girth 2, and
    every vertex is a boundary vertex of every proper region.  fan_n and wheel_n were run to break
    that; the qualitative conclusions of (4) survive on both.
  * The plateau states in (6) READING 2 are CONSTRUCTED, not ground states: L = 12 is past this
    lane's dense-diagonalisation ceiling (ground states computed only for L <= 10).
  * The UNION reading of T4 is out of reach at m = 5 (k = 9 hyperbolic pairs).  Only m <= 3 computed.
  * Z_2 only.  Nothing here tests Z_N for N > 2, and nothing tests a non-abelian group, where the
    electric-centre / extended-Hilbert-space difference is known to be a log-dim term that vanishes
    for abelian groups.  That is the single largest untested generalisation.
  * The identification of C1/C2/C3/C5/C6 with the named published constructions is MY reading of
    those constructions, made without literature access in this lane.  The mathematics of each
    generator list is exact; the attribution is not certified.
