MODEL (INSERTED, GR1/T42_C): n x n x n block of barrier records, one per grain; outside
access ONLY through the 6n^2 block-complement face adjacencies; operations traverse grains
in nearest-neighbour steps.  Exact integers; degrees read by constant finite differences
(T42_C instrument); no fit, no floats.

DEFINITIONS (declared before the numbers; D-24 table in D24_AUDIT.txt):
  chain = path of distinct grains from a channel-bearing grain; certification = a chain
  relays its ENDPOINT's value out (relays read through); write = the same chain inward;
  independent = grain-disjoint chains; epoch = one synchronous round of grain-disjoint
  chains (per-step tick reading declared beside it; the split below is invariant).
