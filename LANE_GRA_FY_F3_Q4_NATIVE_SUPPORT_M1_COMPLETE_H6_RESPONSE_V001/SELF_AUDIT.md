# FY builder self-audit

## Exact source custody

- Pair and gap derivatives remain on their physical `A/B` vertices.
- Hopping-numerator derivatives remain on physical link midpoints.
- Differentiated BW/Feshbach folds inherit the differentiated factor's
  support.
- Ring entries use the forward/reverse endpoint-referenced Hermitian average;
  no forward-only Bloch entry is promoted to an observable.
- All six native support phases are gated against exact `zeta_240` exponents,
  and `m=29` is checked as the conjugate of `m=1`.

## Completeness and normalization

- All diagonal closed-word families and folds through H6 are recomputed.
- Exact native sums recover every FX derivative row before Fourier lifting.
- Fourteen free translation orbits exhaust all 420 undirected ring entries;
  every representative contains 720 forward plus 720 reverse histories.
- `sqrt(60)` normalization is removed before comparison with homogeneous
  FX/FW operators.
- Pair, H2, H4, H6, and ring sources all pass translation character and
  `Q(-m)=Q(m)^dagger` gates.

## Neutral lift decision

The builder does not assume that homogeneous `f_E` survives at `m=1`.  For
each order it either proves equality to the homogeneous coefficient modulo
`Phi_240` or requires a nonzero exact cross-minor.  All three orders returned
exact equality.  Floating best-fit residuals are checksums only.

## Response discipline

- The Hamiltonian is fixed; source variation cannot be said to create or move
  its finite eigenvalues.
- Pole residues are summed over degenerate eigenspaces before ranks are taken.
- The gap-weighted spectral moment is checked against the direct operator
  expression.
- Division by a spectral gap occurs only after a positive-gap gate.
- The `6->6->6->6->6` hierarchy is stated only at `x=2/5` and `x=1/2`.
- TT rank two is a finite source-response fact, not a graviton proof.

## Ward and gravity ceilings

The nonzero `k_i Q^{ij}` contraction is only a naive spatial-transversality
diagnostic.  A full Ward identity would require temporal densities, currents,
contact terms, and a conservation theorem not present here.  Graph momentum
is not continuum locality; finite nonzero gaps are not a massless pole.  FY
does not prove RGRL-B, gravity emergence, `G`, or Newton's law.
