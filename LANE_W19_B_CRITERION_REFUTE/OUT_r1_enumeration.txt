     which also contains link 7.  R_delta counts DISJOINT fragments; under C6 they are not
     disjoint and do not even exclude the system.  The C6 row of b3 PART B is not weightless

PART 3  --  COMPLETE ENUMERATION OF PAULI *-SUBALGEBRAS ON A REGION
========================================================================================================

  Pauli *-subalgebras supported on n links are in bijection with GF(2)-subspaces of F_2^{2n}
  (the Pauli group modulo phases).  Their number is the Galois number G_{2n}.  I enumerate them
  exhaustively and check the count against G_{2n}, so this is a COMPLETE enumeration and not a
  sample.  Then I count how many are GAUGE-INVARIANT (z-part in the cycle space of R -- the
  target's own theorem B-8, which I confirm).
  region                      n links  all subalgebras   G_2n    gauge-invariant   G_(n+cyc) 
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  theta_8 R=[0]               1        5                 5       2                 2         
  theta_10 R=[0, 1]           2        67                67      16                16        
  fan_4 R=[1, 2]              2        67                67      5                 5         
  wheel_4 R=[0, 4, 1]         3        2825              2825    67                67        

  ALL COUNTS MATCH THE GALOIS NUMBERS, so the enumeration is exhaustive.
  A SINGLE LINK carries exactly 5 Pauli subalgebras, of which exactly 2 are gauge-invariant:
  the unit algebra C.1 and alg{X_0}.  A PLAQUETTE on theta carries 67, of which 16 are
  gauge-invariant.  The target lane names 5 objects and, of those, 2 are gauge-invariant region
  algebras -- 2 of 16 on a plaquette, and on a single link its C2 and C3 are literally the same
  algebra, so 1 of 2.  THE LIST IS NOT A CLASSIFICATION; IT IS A SAMPLE.
