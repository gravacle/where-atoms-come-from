CONFOUNDS AND LIMITS OF THIS LANE, RECORDED RATHER THAN FIXED
  * THE SBS BASIS SEARCH IS A GRID (25 x 24) PLUS LOCAL REFINEMENT, NOT A PROOF OF THE MINIMUM.
    A first version used a 24-point theta grid, MISSED theta = pi/2 entirely, and reported a
    minimum of 1.000 for a state whose X basis gives 2e-17.  That bug is recorded in rlib.py and an
    assertion now forces an odd grid.  Where a verdict matters (QDNS not SBS) I give a structural
    argument as well as the search.
  * THE SBS BASIS SEARCH ONLY VARIES THE SYSTEM BASIS FOR A 2-DIMENSIONAL SYSTEM.  The 4-dimensional
    case in r3 PART 5(iv) tests three named bases, not all of U(4).  The YES verdict there is a
    WITNESS and needs no search; the two "no" rows are not exhaustive verdicts.
  * THE SUBALGEBRA ENUMERATION IS COMPLETE ONLY FOR PAULI-GENERATED SUBALGEBRAS.  Subalgebras not
    spanned by Pauli strings exist and are not enumerated.  The counts match the Galois numbers,
    which is the correct check for the Pauli-generated class and no more.
  * THE 16-ALGEBRA SWEEP IS ON ONE CARRIER (theta_10), ONE STATE (Wilson-loop GHZ), ONE FRAGMENT
    RULE (C3_FULL plaquettes).  "Eight distinct verdicts" is that cell, not a general count.
  * Z_2 ONLY, exactly as the target.  Every structural conclusion here inherits the target's own
    self-flag that abelian coincidences may not survive Z_N for N > 2 or a non-abelian group.
  * theta_L IS A DEGENERATE GRAPH and most of my numbers are theta numbers, for comparability with
    the target.  fan and wheel appear only in the type/centre audits and the Perron-Frobenius test.
  * THE PARITY-BROADCAST AND BELL-BASIS STATES ARE CONSTRUCTED, NOT DYNAMICAL.  Whether any
    Hamiltonian of this class produces them is UNTESTED, and finding (9) makes that question
    sharper rather than softer.
  * THE SIX-LINK READING-2 STATE IS A CONSTRUCTED STABILISER STATE, NOT A GROUND STATE.  Whether
    any Hamiltonian of this class produces it at any g^2 is UNTESTED -- the same limitation the
    target records for its own theta_12 state, and it is the weakest point of the corrected
    threshold exactly as it was of the original.
  * THE "L >= girth + 4" TIGHTNESS ARGUMENT ASSUMES THE FOUR PLATEAU POINTS MUST COME FROM FOUR
    DISJOINT FRAGMENTS OF THE ENVIRONMENT.  If a plateau were allowed to be read off nested
    fragments of a partition with fewer parts, the count would change.  I did not explore that.
  * CEILING: dense eigh to L = 10 (2^10); constructed and mixed states to L = 12; algebra entropies
