## Adopted statement

The program adopts the **Universal Directed-Composition Law** (`U-DCL`) as the
working physical-domain postulate of URFT:

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname{U\!DCL}:\Longleftrightarrow
 \forall r\in\mathfrak R^{\rm actual,bf}_{\rm FM},
 \quad DCL(r).}
\]

For each independently admitted actual bona-fide finite-mission record,
`DCL(r)` is the four-clause physical predicate frozen in
`URFT_UNIVERSAL_DOMAIN_LAW_DECISION_V001.md`:

1. a prospectively bounded, branch-independent, no-return/no-bypass directed
   frontier with complete write ancestry before it and registered query after

2. one complete apparatus-inclusive joint standard-Borel probability law or
   C*-state, containing pre-frontier history as a register and never

4. incidence-respecting locality, classical conditional factorization or
   quantum commuting/tensor-separated local algebras, causal factorization, and
   no direct post-frontier source-label input.

## Falsifiability and domain integrity

The actual bona-fide finite-mission record domain remains independently defined.
An admitted exception may not be removed from that domain after a failed U-DCL
test.

One actual record falsifies U-DCL if a prospectively frozen complete audit proves
that it has no branch-independent no-return frontier, or that no normalized
positive joint state and exact local composable dynamics in every applicable
DCL category reproduce its complete registered statistics and free-setting
incidence. A category may be treated as exclusive only with independent physical
warrant.

The port census may be amended only by an independently measured missing
carrier, after which the test must be refrozen. Indefinite retreat through
unmeasured ports, postselected separator states, or enlarged categories is not
allowed.
