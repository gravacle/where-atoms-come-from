C-86 NAND/MLC ARM -- WHAT THE PUBLIC NUMBERS SAY, COMPUTED, WITH FLOORS
==================================================================================================

1. THE BAKE-EQUIVALENCE TABLE THE INDUSTRY ACTUALLY USES, INVERTED TO ITS Ea
   Source: Li/Ye/Kuo/Xue, HotStorage 2021 slides p.5 (public PDF): 1 year at 25 C is
   declared equivalent to the following bake hours:
     T_bake (C)     hours   implied Ea (eV)
             60     97.65             1.100
             80     11.16             1.100
            100      1.61             1.100
            120      0.28             1.101
   spread 1.100-1.101 eV; JESD218B 6.1.3 states its Annex A stress-time
   calculation assumes 1.1 eV IN TEXT.  The published table and the standard agree to the
   0.28-hour quantization of the table's last row: ONE number, 1.1 eV, is what the
   JEDEC-class world runs on -- and JESD218B 3.19 itself says the OTHER retention
   mechanism (leak through the transfer dielectric) 'can be weakly accelerated or even
   decelerated by high temperature', i.e., the standard's own text denies that a single
   Ea covers retention.  The 1.1 eV is the DETRAPPING channel only.

2. LEVEL-RESOLVED RETENTION DRIFT (Cai et al., Proc. IEEE 105(9) 2017, Table 5; TLC,
   room temperature, 2,000 P/E; normalized-Vt units, nominal max = 512, GND = 0;
   absolute volts are vendor-proprietary BY THE PAPER'S OWN STATEMENT)

   NOISE FLOOR: quantization 0.1 units; drift statements below 0.1 units are not made.
   Per-state sigma at 1 day: ER=48.2, P1=9.7, P2=9.7, P3=9.4, P4=9.3, P5=9.1, P6=9.3, P7=8.5

   (a) per-state mean drift, 1 day -> 1 year (units):
       ER:+23.3  P1:+10.0  P2:+6.1  P3:+3.3  P4:+0.4  P5:-2.3  P6:-4.7  P7:-7.3
       sign structure: ER,P1,P2,P3 UP; P4 ~flat (+0.4); P5,P6,P7 DOWN, magnitude
       increasing with level (P7 largest down-drift).  The paper's own reading: TAT
       (field-driven, only downward) dominates high states; hole loss lifts low states.

   (b) adjacent-gap trajectories, margin-free (gap = mean[i+1]-mean[i], units):
           pair      1 day     1 week    1 month   3 months     1 year   d(gap)/yr
          ER-P1      159.3      154.2      153.0      148.4      146.0       -13.3
          P1-P2       61.5       60.6       60.1       58.8       57.6        -3.9
          P2-P3       63.8       63.3       62.9       61.7       61.0        -2.8
          P3-P4       63.0       62.4       61.9       61.0       60.1        -2.9
          P4-P5       63.4       62.7       62.3       61.4       60.7        -2.7
          P5-P6       66.0       65.3       65.1       64.5       63.6        -2.4
          P6-P7       63.8       63.1       62.7       62.0       61.2        -2.6
       every programmed-pair gap SHRINKS; the fastest-closing programmed pair over the
       full year is P1-P2; ER-P1 closes fastest of all (ER rises 23.3).

   (c) resolvability in sigma units (gap / (sigma_i+sigma_j)), where sigma is public:
       P1-P2: 1-day gap 61.5 = 3.2 combined-sigma; 1-year gap 57.6 = 2.3 combined-sigma
       P2-P3: 1-day gap 63.8 = 3.3 combined-sigma; 1-year gap 61.0 = 2.5 combined-sigma
       P3-P4: 1-day gap 63.0 = 3.4 combined-sigma; 1-year gap 60.1 = 2.5 combined-sigma
       NO adjacent pair approaches merger inside the public window: the first
       retirement event -- the first step of C-86's staircase -- lies BEYOND one year
       at room temperature and 2,000 P/E for this chip.  Public data shows the
       APPROACH to the first drop, never a drop.

3. CONTROL: the same paper's Table 4 (cycling at fixed 1-day retention, 0 -> 3,000 P/E)
       0->3k P/E drift: ER:+25.9  P1:+2.4  P2:+0.8  P3:+1.5  P4:+0.8  P5:+0.8  P6:+0.6  P7:+0.8
       cycling moves EVERY programmed state UP (more injection through degraded oxide);
       retention moves the high states DOWN.  Opposite signatures on P5-P7: the
       instrument separates the two drives, so 2(a)'s sign structure is retention's own.

VERDICT LINES (data side only; the model-side comparison is a later lane's job):
  * Level-resolved, margin-free PUBLIC data exists (per-state mean/sigma vs time) but in
    NORMALIZED units at ROOM temperature, and the staircase's first drop is outside the
    published window.  JEDEC-class BAKE data with level-resolved retirement positions is
    not public at any granularity found; drive-level bake results are UBER/margin-defined.
  * The absolute calibration the parameter-free drop-time formula needs (B_i, dE_i in eV,
    f0) is vendor-proprietary in every located source.  Order/shape tests are executable;
    absolute t*_i positions are not, on public data as found.
