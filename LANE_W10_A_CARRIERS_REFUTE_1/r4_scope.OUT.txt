E — 'CARRIER-INDEPENDENT' FOR W-02 AND W-08 IS ANALYTIC, NOT MEASURED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The lane's own premise (PUBLISHED_CONVENTIONS.txt, W10A-05): Z_k = P(u^k, v^k) with
P determined by pi alone.  Every object in W-02's criterion and in W-08's four claims is
a function of (pi, u, v) ONLY.  A CARRIER SYMBOL DOES NOT APPEAR IN EITHER STATEMENT.
So 'does the ruling depend on the carrier?' is answered by inspection of the statement,
not by 150 or 120 measured rows.  The rows test whether the CRITERION is true; they
cannot test whether it is carrier-dependent, because carrier-dependence is not a
proposition about them.

WHERE A CARRIER CAN ENTER, AND IT IS EXACTLY ONE PLACE: a claim QUANTIFIED over pi or
over (f,c).  W-01's registered claim is of that shape -- 'the firing region' is a MEASURE
over (f,c) at fixed occupancy -- and it is the one that fell at W-09.  Re-derived here:
   three classes {10,01,11} (K1)            firing region (max-gap test, 1201x1201 grid) = 0.250000
   three classes {00,10,01} (B1q)           firing region (max-gap test, 1201x1201 grid) = 0.250000
   FOUR classes {00,10,01,11} (B0b,B4)      firing region (max-gap test, 1201x1201 grid) = 0.500000
   W-09's exact values: 1/4 for three classes, 1/2 for four.  Reproduced.

>>> THE SHAPE THAT MAKES A CLAIM CARRIER-SCOPED IS A QUANTIFIER OVER pi OR OVER (f,c).
    W-01's row has one and fell.  W-02's and W-08's rows are pointwise biconditionals in
    (pi, connection) and cannot fall for that reason.  The lane's verdict is therefore
    CORRECT and NOT EARNED BY ITS MEASUREMENTS.  Its own self-flag READ TWO says this;
    its headline ('BOTH CRITERIA SURVIVE FOUR CLASSES INTACT ... SO THEY ARE CARRIER-
    INDEPENDENT') leads with the other reading, with '0 mismatches / 150' behind it.
    'Could not have failed' does not void a THEOREM -- and the class-count-free proofs
    ARE exhibited, so the verdict stands.  What it voids is the COUNT as evidence.

>>> THE CRITERION DOES NOT SPLIT.  Formation still occurs iff G != {1}, i.e. iff u != 1
