LANE W42 -- THE CAPACITY LAW. No Hilbert space is used at all: W-41's counting formula reduces the
question to linear algebra over GF(2). The boundary map S |-> bd(S) (links lying in an ODD number of
the plaquettes of S) is LINEAR, a set of records is simultaneously protectable iff some link lies on
NONE of their boundaries, and "bd(S) misses link L" is ONE linear condition. So the largest
protectable independent set via L is exactly dim ker(f_L), and CAPACITY = max_L dim ker(f_L).
Cross-checked against W-39's explicit brute force at the 3x3 patch: both give 3.
