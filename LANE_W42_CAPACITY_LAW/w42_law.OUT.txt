  CROSS-CHECK against W-39's brute force at the 3x3 patch (m=4). Brute force said 3.
    brute force over all independent sets: capacity = 3   (linear-algebra answer 3)  -> AGREE

  THE LAW
    capacity = m - 1 in every case above, where m = number of plaquettes.
    For a planar patch the number of plaquettes IS the AREA in lattice cells,
    and the cycle rank equals it. So:  CAPACITY = AREA - 1.

  AND THE HONEST READING OF THAT
    2x2: area   1  perimeter   4  capacity   0   cap/area 0.000   cap/perimeter 0.000
    3x2: area   2  perimeter   6  capacity   1   cap/area 0.500   cap/perimeter 0.167
    3x3: area   4  perimeter   8  capacity   3   cap/area 0.750   cap/perimeter 0.375
    4x3: area   6  perimeter  10  capacity   5   cap/area 0.833   cap/perimeter 0.500
    4x4: area   9  perimeter  12  capacity   8   cap/area 0.889   cap/perimeter 0.667
    5x4: area  12  perimeter  14  capacity  11   cap/area 0.917   cap/perimeter 0.786
    5x5: area  16  perimeter  16  capacity  15   cap/area 0.938   cap/perimeter 0.938

    capacity tracks AREA, not perimeter. It is NOT a holographic/boundary-law count,
    and must not be reported as one.
