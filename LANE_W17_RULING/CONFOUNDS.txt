C1  FLOATING-POINT, NOT PHYSICS.  In the R1/F5 strengthening block, the row
    "dim = 1000000  E_n = n^2  |A(t=2*pi)| = 0.999999987890023" is a numerical
    artefact, not a partial revival.  exp(-i n^2 2pi) = 1 EXACTLY for every
    integer n; the deficit comes from reducing arguments of size n^2*2pi ~ 6.3e12
    in double precision (relative eps 2.2e-16 -> absolute phase error ~1e-3 rad
    at the top of the range).  The E_n = n rows, whose arguments are 1e6 times
    smaller, return 1.000000000000000 at every dimension.  The claim rests on
    the exact arithmetic, not on the quadrature.  Left in place unedited.

C2  THE CONTROLS ARE NOT ROUTE DECISIONS.  FRAME_CHALLENGE_V001.md section 0
    restricts the instrument to decisions that determine what gets built next.
    Neither PARITY nor DIAGONAL/NON-DIAGONAL is one.  A defender may say the
    instrument was never meant to be pointed at them.  The answer is that F1..F5
    as written contain no clause excluding them, that W-15 nominated
    diagonal/non-diagonal itself as the paradigm of a sound binary, and that a
    test whose specificity is only claimed on the class of frames it was
    reverse-engineered from has no measured specificity at all.  But the
    objection is real and is recorded, not answered away.

C3  I DID NOT BUILD A SOUND ROUTE DECISION.  The ideal control -- a route
    decision in this program whose binary is forced, with a proof under it --
    does not exist on disk to my knowledge, and I did not construct one.  The
    controls therefore bound the instrument's FALSE-POSITIVE rate on sound
    partitions; they do not measure its false-positive rate on sound ROUTES.
