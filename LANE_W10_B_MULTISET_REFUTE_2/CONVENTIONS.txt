  R1 mahler_jensen        mpmath, dps configurable, Jensen in y or x.  Split points are
                          found by SCAN + BISECTION on SA-SB, not by the target lane's
                          closed-form acos -- a different code path on purpose.
  R2 mahler_lawton        Lawton (1983): m(P) = lim_N m(P(x,x^N)), one-variable measure
                          from the ROOTS by Jensen's formula.  Root finding, not
                          quadrature.  float64; a 1e-6-class check at N=400, never a
                          headline digit.
  R3 sorted_domination /  EXACT in fractions.Fraction.  Derives and checks the criterion
     dominated_exact_perm w_max + w_min >= w_mid1 + w_mid2 for 'all 24 arrangements have
                          a dominated Jensen pairing, so lambda = log(w_max) exactly'.
  R4 mahler_ergodic       direct k-average of log|Z_k| at f = 0.9, c = pi/e -- a
                          DIFFERENT irrational connection from the target lane's
                          f = 1.0, c = sqrt(2).  float64.
  R5 mahler_subtorus_res  EXACT-from-roots average on S3/S4's resonant (f,c)=0.1*(20,11)
                          subtorus: mpmath polyroots at dps 40 on Q(w) = p00 w^20 + p10
                          + p01 w^31 + p11 w^11.  Validated against the ERRATUM AGAINST
                          W-02's registered -0.767014993.
  R6 rate_order4          CLOSED FORM at S1's published connection (W_F=-1, W_C=-i),
                          derived on the page: rate = (1/4)[log((a-b)^2+(c-d)^2)
                          + log|a+b-c-d| + log(a+b+c+d)].  Exact in rationals.
  plus a float64 twin of each where a sweep needs speed; every such use is labelled in
