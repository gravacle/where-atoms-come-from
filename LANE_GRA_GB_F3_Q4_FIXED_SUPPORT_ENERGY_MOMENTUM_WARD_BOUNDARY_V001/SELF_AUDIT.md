# GB fixed-support energy/momentum self-audit

## Positive-result discipline

- Local energy continuity is an operator identity for the inherited ring-term
  allocation, not a fitted or continuum statement.
- Edge-disjoint ring terms are checked to commute exactly; no long-range
  current edge is inserted by the term decomposition.
- The twelve-term test is called a finite term subset, not a proved connected
  geometric volume.
- The energy allocation is explicitly nonunique.  The lane does not promote
  its term current to a unique physical stress tensor.
- GA is used only modulo one common identity/reference across the full encoded
  `P+Q` space.  No literal equality or sector-dependent scalar is assumed.

## Translation and momentum discipline

- `[H,U_tau]=0` is called conservation of a finite-group representation label,
  not proof of physical linear momentum.
- Logarithm branch ambiguity proves nonselection by `U_tau`; it is not
  overclaimed as a theorem that every possible enlarged generator is nonlocal.
- Fixed diamond coordinates are c-number support data.  No position,
  conjugate momentum, recoil, or clamp degree of freedom is inferred from
  those labels.
- U(1) charge current from GA is not identified with `T^{0j}` or momentum
  flux.

## Port and projection discipline

- Closed H6 energy continuity is separated from energy exchange under active
  reservoir, support, controller, and boundary couplings.
- The schematic momentum ledger is labeled a required completion, not a
  proved identity of the frozen parent.
- The minimal completion permits either dynamical support recoil or another
  explicit translation-owning dynamical sector; it does not presuppose a
  classical spatial lattice in the `E-EMERGENTSPACE` route.
- Temporal source, local divergence, second derivatives, contacts, and port
  ownership must be present before Feshbach projection.

## Claim ceiling

No unique local energy density, physical momentum density, `T^{0j}`, stress
Ward identity, continuum locality, gravity, or `G` is claimed.
