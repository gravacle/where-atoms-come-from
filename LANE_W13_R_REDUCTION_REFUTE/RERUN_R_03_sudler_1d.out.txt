WHAT THIS ARM DOES NOT SETTLE — STATED BEFORE THE VERDICT
   The three convergent rows read TWO WAYS: (i) convergence holds for these rotations,
   or (ii) seven decades is not enough to see a failure that begins later.  Nothing in
   this script distinguishes them.  R_06 does, and only for algebraic-type rotations.
   'BA' here is a property of a ALONE; the literature's condition is a property of the
   PAIR (a, theta*).  Badly-approximable a does NOT by itself bound ||k a - theta*||
   from below -- see R_06.  No row here is scored as evidence that it does.
