# Self-audit: GRA-FS complete source-rank audit

## 1. Scope discipline

- The theorem says “complete” only relative to the explicitly selected
  reduced CW/FM Hamiltonian `H=H0+VX` at `E_R=0`.
- It does not call that reduction the complete physical BS04 parent.
- It does not infer that periodicity removes physical ports.  Periodicity
  removes a geometric boundary; the selected Hamiltonian, separately,
  excludes boundary exchange, controllers, and ports.
- Every omitted BS sector is named.  None receives an invented zero tensor.
- If one is reattached, its linear weight is classified as missing data.

## 2. Family custody

- `G_(L_r)`, `L_r=5*2^r`, is the FE periodic quotient with an existing proof
  of degree four, girth six, and safe quotient scale.
- The stronger `L>=5` bound makes every quotient-induced cycle longer than
  eight, matching both CW H6 and FM H8 local support.
- The maps `G_(2L)->G_L` are graph covers.  “Covering-matched” does not mean
  that the finite Hilbert spaces are literal subspaces of one another.
- The family remains a mathematical support choice, not a derivation of a
  physical periodic identification from record lineage.

## 3. Term decomposition

- The decomposition is frozen prospectively from the displayed CW/FM parent:
  one flip per edge, one formal zero-detuning onsite per edge, and one unsplit
  degree square per vertex.
- The degree square is the only non-edge-located term.  It contains no
  independent node register and depends only on its four incident link
  occupations.  This lane prospectively freezes FQ17a occurrence one on each
  actual incident support, giving the additive scalar
  `sum_a D_a=(4/3)I`; it does not claim that the source-free Hamiltonian
  uniquely forces that query tensor.
- The constant inside the square is not split off and independently
  reweighted.  Such a split would define a different source query unless the
  pieces retain the source factor of the original square.
- The formal onsite row is listed even though `E_R=0`; its operator
  contribution is exactly zero.
- The exact microscopic lower rank uses the prospectively frozen `h != 0`;
  at `h=0` only the at-most-four ceiling and `E` null survive.

## 4. Rank and representation

- Exact rational row reduction gives rank four for the four tetrahedral
  dyads.
- The two displayed diagonal-traceless tensors annihilate every dyad and,

- The degree-node tensor is the dyad sum and cannot enlarge the span.
- The nonzero flip operators already attain the weight-span rank because an

  corresponding `X_e`.  The

- The blind `E` directions are not called gauge transformations or Ward

## 5. Projection and identity custody

- The source is attached before Feshbach reduction, as FQ requires.
- H6, dressed H6, H8 octagons, scalar histories, energy derivatives,

  degree weight, so they retain the `E` null.
- The verifier exhausts every four-label occurrence composition at orders
  two, four, six, and eight, a superset of the admitted path topologies.
- This establishes an internal tensor-rank obstruction.  It does not compute

## 6. Contact caveat

- A general `O(j^2)` contact is retained.
- It may have nonzero `EE` and mixed `E-A1` Hessians and may change higher CTP

  `Q_E`, its spectral residue, or a commutator moment built from that absent
  operator.
- The theorem does not require every contact to factor through the four edge
  invariants.

## 7. Falsifiers

The branch-relative rank theorem fails if any of the following is found:

1. the selected CW/FM equation contains a nonzero microscopic term outside
   the inventoried flip, onsite-zero, or degree-square classes, or its
   through-order-eight reduction contains an omitted projected class;
2. the degree square owns an independent nonadditive tensor under the frozen
   FQ17a rule;
3. one of the four edge dyads has a nonzero contraction with a displayed `E`
   generator;
4. fixed source-before-Feshbach differentiation creates a first derivative
   outside the span of the microscopic first derivatives; or
5. an `O(j^2)` contact has a nonzero first derivative at `j=0`.

Reattaching a broader BS sector, deriving a rotated coframe, or supplying a
root/cross-dyad tensor does not falsify this theorem; it defines a different,
larger source whose rank must be audited separately.
