## 1. THE OPEN QUESTION

**There is no description in physics of how records form at the quantum level, nor of the roles EM,
gravity and alpha play in it.**

This is not a gap the registrar invented. The decoherence programme explains the suppression of
interference and the selection of a pointer basis; it does **not** explain definite outcomes, and the
preferred-basis and outcome problems remain open (a 2013 foundations survey found **16%** holding
that decoherence had settled the measurement problem). Topological order supplies protected quantum
**memories** — engineering proposals. **Nobody has said: this is what a record IS, this is why it
requires what it requires, and these are the three terms' roles in producing one.**

## 2. THE CLAIM

> **A quantum record is a topologically protected degeneracy of a gauge field.**
>
> **EM** supplies the field and the holonomy that *is* the record.
> **GRAVITY — as the topology of the space** — supplies three things nothing else supplies: the record
> space, the unique writer, and the protection.
> **ALPHA** destroys the record, at order `d`.

**In full:**

## 3. WHAT IS ASSEMBLED, AND WHAT THE ASSEMBLY DOES

**The components are known and are attributed:** gauge theory; Kitaev's toric code (Theorems A–D);
einselection; the Harlow–Ooguri constraint. **None of these is claimed as new.**

**What the assembly does that none of its components does:**

- It **answers what a record is** — a topologically protected degeneracy — rather than describing a
  device that stores one.
- It shows **why records need what they need**: the degeneracy requirement (1) plus the fragility
  dichotomy (2,3) forces topology. Kitaev's theorems say what topology *gives*; they do not say
  records *require* it.
- It **assigns all three roles in one operation** (W-62): EM carries, genus writes and protects, alpha
  destroys — with every control firing.
- It **resolves a real obstruction**: writable-and-durable are conjugate for local operations, and a
  non-local writer exists that noise cannot mimic.
- It **meets an independent constraint from gravity**: Harlow–Ooguri forbids exact global symmetries
  in quantum gravity, so a gravitating world cannot protect records by symmetry. **Topology needs
  none.** Two independent results, same conclusion.
