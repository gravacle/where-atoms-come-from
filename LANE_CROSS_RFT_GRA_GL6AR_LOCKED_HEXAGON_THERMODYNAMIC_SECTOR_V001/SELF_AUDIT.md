# GL6AR Self-Audit

## Earned results

1. The finite boundary Hilbert spaces and flip operators are explicitly
   defined.
2. The quasi-local interaction uses only local degree projectors and has
   exact 18-link support.
3. The divergence and three coordinate integer cut fluxes are derived from
   the two degree-two constraint equations.  The fourth conserved normalized
   port count is only their dependent sum, not a fourth geometric cut.
4. Perron--Frobenius claims are component-wise, not global across fragmented
   sectors.
5. Infinite ground-state limit points are obtained for declared locked
   boundary conditions and exhaustions and are restricted to the locked,
   degree-preserving dynamics; nonlocal sector retention is not asserted.
6. The active finite-periodic energy trial is explicit and yields a bound
   for `4|L`; it does not assert density convergence or a thermodynamic
   energy-density limit.
7. The `L^-2` conclusion concerns only a selected sequence of finite
   nontrivial component gaps with positive variance and is conditional on an
   extensive variance lower bound, which is not supplied by the sealed
   inputs.
8. Finite-size component-gap closure is not promoted to thermodynamic/GNS
   spectral gaplessness.  That promotion additionally requires a compatible
   selected weak-* ground-state limit and normalized local or form-domain
   quasi-local excitations outside the full zero-energy GNS subspace with
   vanishing limiting ground-state energy form.

## Promotion attacks

- Integer cut flux is not named an electromagnetic or gravitational field.
- The quotient tent function is not physical position or momentum.
- Perron--Frobenius positivity is not state selection by nature.
- Frozen components are not called global periodic ground states.
- Ground-state existence is not uniqueness, a phase theorem, or a pole.
- The conditional soft mode is not called a photon or graviton.
- A closing finite-size tower is not called an infinite-volume gapless mode.
- No cone, stress, Ricci/Einstein response, gravity, or `G` is claimed.

## Remaining sharp gate

Prove extensive slowly varying port-density variance or another
vanishing-gap bound in the actual maximizing finite-volume components while
controlling sector and boundary selection.  Separately construct the
compatible limiting ground state and local/quasi-local excitation sequence
needed for a GNS spectral statement.  Until then, the thermodynamic model is
rigorous, but neither unconditional finite-size closure nor infinite-volume
GNS gaplessness is established.
