SECTION 1 -- THE BALANCE LEMMA  (DEF-A: admissible == [U,H] = 0)
==============================================================================

LEMMA (necessity).  Let [H,R]=0, R=R^dag, R^2=I.  Let U be unitary with [U,H]=0 and
U^dag R U = -R.  Since [U,H]=0, U commutes with every spectral projector P_E of H, so
   Tr(P_E R) = Tr(P_E U^dag R U) = Tr(U P_E U^dag R) = Tr(P_E R),
while U^dag R U = -R gives Tr(P_E U^dag R U) = -Tr(P_E R).  Hence Tr(P_E R) = 0.

LEMMA (sufficiency).  If Tr(P_E R) = 0 for every E, then inside each eigenspace of H
the +1 and -1 eigenspaces of R|_E have equal dimension, so a unitary swapping them
exists inside that block; the direct sum U over blocks satisfies [U,H]=0 and
U^dag R U = -R.

=> Under DEF-A, clause (iv) is EXACTLY the statement: at every energy, the record's
   two values are equally available.  ("The bit costs no energy.")

SECTION 2 -- DEF-B IS VACUOUS  ('generated by the theory's own operators')
==============================================================================

CLAIM.  If "admissible" means "lies in the *-algebra generated by {H, L_k}", then NO
record can satisfy clause (iv), for any (H,{L_k}) whatever.

PROOF (one line, and it is the framework's own P-2).  Clause (ii) says [H,R]=0 and
[L_k,R]=0.  The set of operators commuting with R is an algebra closed under sums,
products and adjoints, and it contains every generator; hence it contains the whole
generated algebra.  So every DEF-B-admissible U has [U,R]=0, and U^dag R U = +R != -R
(R != 0).  Clause (iv) is unsatisfiable.  QED

=> DEF-B is REJECTED: it fails criterion (1) outright.  It does not merely restrict
   clause (iv); it empties it, and with it the whole definition of "record".

SECTION 4 -- DOES DEF-A MAKE CLAUSE (iv) TRIVIAL?  (criterion 2, abstract half)
==============================================================================

Under DEF-A, clause (iv) is equivalent to the per-eigenspace balance condition.  If
that condition were implied by clauses (i)-(iii) it would be trivial.  It is not:
the Section-3 witness satisfies (i),(ii),(iii) and fails (iv).  So (iv) is an
INDEPENDENT clause under DEF-A.
