## Adversarial checks

1. **Projected versus microscopic gauge law.**  The full finite-`U_d` F3
   action contains single-link `X_e` tunnelling and does not commute with the
   ice Gauss operators.  The theorem explicitly confines exact Gauss
   preservation to the projected effective branch.  Virtual charge defects
   are not erased from the microscopic parent.

2. **Hard-core Hilbert space versus rotor phase space.**  Dirac's
   `2N-2F` counting assumes a canonical compact-rotor completion.  It is used
   only to type the supplied Maxwell infrared theory, not as the literal
   dimension of the finite hard-core ice Hilbert space.

3. **Global dependency and zero modes.**  A connected incidence matrix has
   rank `|V|-1`, not `|V|`.  Periodic winding/harmonic sectors account for the
   special zero-mode count and do not create new local first-class generators.

4. **Supplied support.**  The periodic diamond completion remains supplied;
   F3 has not derived that global lattice.  The finite graph checks establish
   algebra on admitted supports, not a support-selection theorem.

5. **Same two-polarization count.**  Maxwell and a massless rank-two field
   both end with two configurations.  Their starting canonical variables,
   generator species, symbols, Ward identities, and continuum helicities are
   different.  The theorem does not infer equivalence from the final count.

6. **`S4` naming collision.**  The standard vector and one tensor fragment
   both carry the irrep label `T2` under tetrahedral restriction.  Complement
   parity and `2cos(theta)` versus `2cos(2theta)` show why that finite-group
   label does not determine continuum spin.

7. **Four scalar samples false positive.**  Four values or a first jet of one
   scalar can be arranged as `A1+T2`, but at fixed momentum they depend on one
   amplitude.  They are not four independent lapse/shift gauge functions.

8. **Pair identities false positive.**  The three opposite-pair identities
   plus the fixed sum have formal coefficient rank four and the suggestive
   `A1+T2` type.  On the projected fiber they are zero operators, have no
   independent conjugates, and generate no gauge orbit.  Calling them
   first-class would insert a symplectic structure absent from the parent.

9. **Commuting with Gauss is not generating Gauss.**  Pair observables and
   closed rings commute with the inherited constraints because they are gauge
   invariant.  Their commutation does not enlarge the generator algebra.

10. **Finite order.**  Closed-loop preservation is exact for the owned order
    six and eight and trivially for an exactly projected `P2 H_eff P2`.  No
    volume-uniform convergence or all-orders phase stability is claimed.

11. **No thermodynamic exclusion.**  A distinct collective redundancy could
    emerge only in a thermodynamic/critical limit.  This lane rules out
    extracting it from the present local links/pairs by representation count;
    it does not prove that no collective rank-two phase exists.

12. **RGRL status.**  The screen does not refute or retract adopted RGRL-B.
    It proves that one named microscopic route has not yet derived RGRL-B from
    below.  The real-world theorem remains conditional on its adopted physical
    law until a lower derivation or decisive empirical test is supplied.

13. **Successor discipline.**  The recommended next test uses the already
    fixed `H_eff^(6)+H_eff^(8)` dynamics.  A tensor pole without the required
    null/constraint algebra is insufficient; a local `S4` tensor label without
    a pole is also insufficient.  No rescue coupling is authorized.
