  READ -- filled in from the tables above
==============================================================================================================================
  1. FRACTION MOVED, for a record drawn from the record set, is EXACTLY 0 on every abelian
     carrier and EXACTLY 1 on every non-abelian carrier, at |G| = 2,4,8,16,32,64 and record
     counts from 4 to 4096.  It is a 0/1 indicator of non-abelianness and carries NO scale.
     S2 measured it directly on matrices up to dim 1024: GENERIC records moved 0/464 on the
     abelian carriers and 456/456 on the non-abelian ones.
  2. THE FRACTION IS 1 BECAUSE THE FIXED SET IS A PROPER SUBVARIETY, NOT BECAUSE NO FIXED
     RECORD EXISTS.  A transport-fixed record exists on EVERY carrier in the ladder -- proved
     by exact subset-sum on the isotypic block structure (S1) and exhibited as an explicit
     matrix with ||[A_h,R]|| < 3e-11 on 464+456 of 464+456 draws (S2).  On D(D_4) this
     settles the question LANE_O34 left OPEN, and it settles it the other way from the
     natural reading of '40 of 40 moved'.
  3. ORBITS.  The record set is a CONTINUUM, so the number of transport orbits is INFINITE on
     every carrier, abelian and non-abelian.  What is finite and exact is the orbit SIZE: every
     orbit has size dividing |G/Z(G)|, the generic stabiliser is trivial, so the LARGEST orbit
     is exactly |G/Z(G)| and the smallest is 1.
  4. WHAT THE VARIATION TRACKS.  Within the non-abelian carriers alone -- abelianness held
     fixed, so it cannot be doing the work -- phi_fix ranks against |G/Z| at -0.9958 and
     against the record count at only -0.4468, and the residual record-count correlation is
     itself explained by record count correlating with |G/Z| at +0.4495.
  5. THE DOUBLE DISSOCIATION IS EXACT, NOT STATISTICAL.
     ARM A  record count 22 -> 88 -> 352 (x16) at fixed G/Z = Z_2^2:  phi_fix 0.5318 ->
            0.5318 -> 0.5318 and largest orbit 4 -> 4 -> 4.  UNCHANGED.
     ARM B  record count 184 = 184 (D_8xZ_2 vs the extraspecial 2^(1+4)):  phi_fix 0.2325 vs
            0.1664 and largest orbit 8 vs 16.  CHANGED.
     Growing the record count with transport fixed changes nothing; changing transport with the
     record count fixed changes everything.
  6. AND IT IS NOT PURELY GROUP-THEORETIC EITHER (D-17).  ARM C keeps the group AND the record
     count fixed -- the anyon count is a topological invariant of the torus -- and refines the
     lattice from 2 edges to 4:  phi_fix falls 0.5318 -> 0.2583 on D(D_4).  Transport's grip is
     a property of the CARRIER'S SIZE and the TRANSPORT GROUP, and of neither the record count
     nor non-abelianness alone.
