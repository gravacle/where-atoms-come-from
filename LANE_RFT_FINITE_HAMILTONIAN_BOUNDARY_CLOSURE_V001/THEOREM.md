## 1. Question and prior theorem

The sealed FCLPD theorem proves

\[
 \operatorname{FCLPD}(r)\Longrightarrow DCL_{\rm phys}(r),
 \qquad
 \operatorname{REC}(r)\land\operatorname{FCLPD}(r)
 \Longrightarrow\operatorname{COV}_{\cup}(r).             \tag{1}
\]

Its F0--F3 antecedent still asks for a complete finite query-ancestor
micro-incidence, one joint root state, exact UCP/complete-CP composition, and
arm-common source provenance.  The present theorem asks whether those four
clauses have to be authenticated separately for an ordinary finite Hamiltonian
device.

The answer is no inside one sharply defined physical class.  One exact outer
Hamiltonian closure statement makes the full finite device algebra a complete
query boundary.  Standard finite-dimensional quantum mechanics then supplies
the joint reduced root, exact UCP propagation, and complete CP read maps.  The
write/query schedule supplies the acyclic incidence.  This is a reduction of
F0--F3 to a smaller microphysical packet, not a derivation of device closure
from record contrast.

## 2. The finite Hamiltonian boundary-closure class

Fix an independently specified finite-mission candidate `r`, contexts `z`,

 \operatorname{FHBC}(r)                                  \tag{2}

### H0 -- prospective finite physical device

Before any scored response, one prospectively bounded physical device `D` is
specified with

\[
 {\cal H}_D=\bigotimes_{k=1}^{n}{\cal H}_k,
 \qquad n<\infty,\qquad \dim{\cal H}_k<\infty.           \tag{3}
\]

`D` contains the source register/transducer, writer and carrier degrees,
controllers, clock, detector-ready and pointer/history systems, references,
and memories actually used by the mission.  Finite classical registers are
included as diagonal factors.  The external complement is denoted `E`; its
dimension need not be finite.

The device, source support, mission times, contexts, registered instruments,
and physical referents are fixed independently of positive contrast or success
of (1).  An endpoint-fitted effective Hamiltonian is not H0.

### H1 -- exact outer Hamiltonian closure

Throughout the declared mission, after including every actually coupled
apparatus/environment degree in `D`, the total dynamics has no interaction
term across the `D|E` boundary:

\[
 H_{DE,z}(t)=H_{D,z}(t)\otimes I_E
             +I_D\otimes H_{E,z}(t).                    \tag{4}
\]

Here `H_E,z(t)` is self-adjoint on a declared well-posed time-dependent domain
and generates a strongly continuous unitary propagator `U_E,z(t,s)`.  The total
Hamiltonian likewise generates the exact propagator

\[
 U_{DE,z}(t,s)=U_{D,z}(t,s)\otimes U_{E,z}(t,s).          \tag{4a}
\]

Equation (4a) is part of the outer-closure packet; equation (4) supplies its
no-interaction physical realization.  Merely writing the formal sum (4) without
the self-adjoint domain and propagator facts does not satisfy H1.

Every declared source operation and registered query operation acts on `D`
and as the identity on `E`.  There is no later external cable, field, analyst,
controller, setting generator, or memory that couples to a registered query;
any such object must instead be included in `D` or the packet fails H1.

The device Hamiltonian is a finite sum of bounded, piecewise-continuous terms
on a prospectively declared finite interaction hypergraph,

\[
 H_{D,z}(t)=\sum_{\alpha=1}^{m}H_{\alpha,z}(t),
 \qquad m<\infty.                                       \tag{5}
\]

Its exact time-ordered propagator `U_D,z(t,s)` is the physical propagator.  The
theorem uses that propagator as one bounded exact W-slot between registered
operations; it does not replace noncommuting terms by a finite Trotter product.

Equations (4)--(4a), together with D-local source and read operations, are the
load-bearing physical completeness statement.  It is stronger than weak
shielding, small leakage, or agreement of a reduced model with passive data.
Boundedness of `H_D,z` makes the device propagator well posed; the complement
domain and total factorization remain explicit H1 facts.

### H2 -- one standard quantum root and one source location

Every Hamiltonian slab, controller law, context generator, and future query

### H3 -- exact complete read operations

The instrument may be obtained from an actual finite apparatus dilation already

### H4 -- finite mission separation and nonvacuity

The physical schedule has finitely many registered source, propagation,
control, and query modules.  Every declared source/write module is before every
terminal registered query in the external module order; no registered query is
an ancestor of a later relevant write in the same packet.  At least one declared
state-wire route runs from a source/write module to a registered query.

Choose any interface time `t_G` after the last declared write-side operation and
before the terminal query-side operation.  The exact propagator may then be

\[
 U_D(t_Q,t_S)=U_D(t_Q,t_G)U_D(t_G,t_S)
              =U_{>G}U_{<G}.                            \tag{8a}
\]

A source can remain dynamically active until immediately before a terminal
query: the complete coherent interval is then a write-side W-slot.  H4 is a
finite-mission ordering condition, not source-off sealing.

## 3. Lemma FHBC-L1 -- exact autonomy from an arbitrary complement

Under H1, the total propagator factorizes,

\[
 U_{DE,z}(t,s)=U_{D,z}(t,s)\otimes U_{E,z}(t,s).          \tag{9}
\]

For every D-local normal CP source branch, finite dimensionality of `D` gives
the exact partial-trace intertwining identity

\[
 \operatorname{Tr}_E\!\left[
  (S_{x,z}\otimes\operatorname{id}_E)(\rho_{DE,z})\right]
 =S_{x,z}\!\left(\operatorname{Tr}_E\rho_{DE,z}\right)
 =S_{x,z}(\rho_{D,z}).                                  \tag{9a}
\]

Let `S_x,z` be an authorized source map and let
`E_y^{j,z}=(I_y^{j,z})^*(I_D)` be one terminal effect.  Then, for an arbitrary
initially correlated or entangled `rho_DE,z`,

\[
\boxed{
 p(y\mid x,z,j)
 =\operatorname{Tr}_D\!\left[
 E_y^{j,z}U_{D,z}
 S_{x,z}(\rho_{D,z})
 U_{D,z}^{\dagger}\right].}                             \tag{10}
\]

For an endogenous source arm, replace `S_x,z(rho_D,z)` by its normalization
`S_x,z(rho_D,z)/p_x,z`.

### Proof

The well-posed outer-closure packet gives (9) as equation (4a); no formal
unbounded-operator sum is used without its domain theorem.  Write the D-local
normal CP branch in finite Kraus form.  Moving each D-only Kraus operator
through the defining partial-trace pairing proves (9a).  Source and query
operations are D-local by H1--H3.  Apply (9a) to the full Born probability and
use invariance of the partial trace under E-only unitary conjugation.  The
result is (10).  Complete positivity preserves positivity; trace preservation
gives normalization in the authorized case, while division by the positive
number (7) gives normalization in the endogenous case.  QED.

Thus an initially entangled outside system does not have to be placed on the
record frontier merely because it is entangled.  If it never recouples and no
registered query acts on it, every future query depends on it only through the
single reduced root (6).  A future recoupling or joint read would violate H1
and return the degree to the physical device census.

## 6. Theorem FHBC-T2 -- passive response cannot authenticate H1

Even an exact visible Hamiltonian trajectory and perfect passive record contrast
do not establish the outer closure equation (4).

Let the visible device contain bits `W,R`, begin in `00`, use a source operation
`W:=X`, and apply the same visible CNOT `R:=R xor W`.  In both worlds the visible
trajectory is

\[
 W=X,\qquad R=X,\qquad Q=X.                              \tag{17}
\]

In the honest world `M_0`, the query is `Q:=R` and the visible device is a
complete source-to-query boundary.  In the bypass world `M_1`, a hidden external
bit is also written `H:=X` and the physical query is `Q:=H`.  The complete
passive law of `(X,W,R,Q)` and the visible CNOT dynamics are identical in the
two worlds.  Yet replacing `R:=0` before the query gives

\[
 Q=0\quad\text{in }M_0,
 \qquad Q=X\quad\text{in }M_1.                           \tag{18}
\]

Therefore no rule whose inputs are only the visible reduced dynamics and the
passive registered law can be both sound and complete for H1.  The two worlds
give it identical inputs but have different physical boundary closure.  A
physical port/coupling inventory, source custody, or a valid causal-break family
adds the information that distinguishes them.  QED.

This is not a counterexample to FHBC-T1: `M_1` fails H1.  It proves that H1 is
an irreducibly physical premise and cannot be manufactured from the same record
contrast whose causal route is being tested.

1. the declared finite device boundary must really satisfy exact equation (4),

No theorem in the current corpus establishes

\[

noncompact gauge/gravitational dressing, branch-dependent-background, or
genuinely nonstandard-composition missions.  H2's normal trace-class tensor
root and H1's factorized Hilbert-space propagator deliberately exclude those
more general roots.  Approximate laboratory isolation also does not imply exact
FHBC without a separately proved error-carrying FCLPD/DCL theorem.

The actual-world universal target therefore remains: derive `DCL_phys` for
