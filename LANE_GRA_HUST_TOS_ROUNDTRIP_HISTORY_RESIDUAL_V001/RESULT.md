# Result

The official HUST Figure-2 period summaries support an exact descriptive
equal-configuration round-trip diagnostic. For all nine `N-F-N` and nine
`F-N-F` triples in each of the source-present and source-absent panels, the
observable is the change in \((2\pi/T)^2\) between the two equal-configuration
endpoints. The middle opposite configuration certifies the excursion and has
zero endpoint weight.

The source-present-minus-background mean returns are

\[
 \bar d_N=-1.8506327675395133\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{s}^{-2},
 \qquad
 \bar d_F=-1.8282181693912770\times10^{-10}\ \mathrm{s}^{-2}.
\]

Their orientation-even mean is
\(-1.8394254684653953\times10^{-10}\,\mathrm{s}^{-2}\). Their orientation-odd
half-difference mean is
\(-1.1207299074118054\times10^{-12}\,\mathrm{s}^{-2}\), but no uncertainty or
zero claim is authorized.

Those displayed digits are deterministic replay digits from workbook periods
on a nominal \(10^{-5}\,\mathrm{s}\) grid, not measurement precision. The source-present and
source-absent loop index is a sequence ordinal across separate panels, not a
simultaneous, randomized, or authenticated matched trial. The orientation
means do not depend on how loopwise values are paired, but the individual
even/odd components and the reported odd-component RMS do.

The exact linear ledger uses a `36 x 40` return matrix of rank 36 and an
`18 x 40` panel-differential matrix of rank 18. Adjacent rows share one endpoint
with opposite sign. Their exact unit-endpoint overlap Gram blocks have diagonal
`2`/off-diagonal `-1` for individual panel returns and diagonal
`4`/off-diagonal `-2` for panel differentials. These are overlap matrices, not
empirical covariance matrices. Each nine-return mean telescopes to the last
minus first endpoint divided by nine.

Every return includes an opposite-configuration excursion, but the release has
no matched no-excursion counterfactual. The observable therefore cannot
distinguish hysteresis or memory from ordinary time drift. The dominant common
panel difference cannot be assigned to record history,
ordinary drift, fibre ageing, mechanics, thermal state, controller history, or
source-motion memory from these released summaries. The panels are separate,
row covariance is absent, printed periods are rounded, and paired loop durations
are not identical. Here “paired” means same-ordinal bookkeeping only; it is not
an experimental match. This is therefore a reproducible figure-level
history-confound diagnostic only—not `beta_TM`, lineage gravity, statistical
evidence, gravity emergence, full `GC16`, or a new value of \(G\).
