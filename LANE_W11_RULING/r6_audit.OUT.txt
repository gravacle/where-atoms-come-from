   -> the covariant root variety is POSITIVE-DIMENSIONAL, not 27 points.  The steelman-
      cross's leg E3 is not exhaustive and its 'EXACTLY ONE passes' is not established.
      Its VERDICT is unharmed -- every member of Lam.T is pi-visible (my R4.3) -- but the
      one leg it offers as EXACT and EXHAUSTIVE is neither.
