LANE W11 RULING — PUBLISHED CONVENTIONS
Commissioned to answer W-10 section 0.4.1: is "the formation functional is carrier-independent"
a FINDING (Reading A) or a restatement of the TRANSPORT CONVENTION (Reading B)?

CODE. Four legs plus rlib.py. Nothing is imported from any W-11 lane; the carriers, the operators,
the states and the observable are rebuilt from the sealed corpus bytes.

SEED. numpy default_rng(20260818) throughout — deliberately NOT the registrar's 20260817, NOT
LANE_W11_R_UNIQUENESS_CROSS's 11072026, and NOT any refuter's. No arm anywhere inherits a draw.

CONNECTION. f = 1.0, c = sqrt(2) — S4_THE_MEASUREMENT_V001.md:603, THE ONLY GENERIC CONNECTION
THE CORPUS PUBLISHES (W-10 N-4: every rational (f,c) is exactly resonant). Built by
rlib.a_generic, which solves for the last step of each walk WITH THE SIGN OF ITS TRAVERSAL
DIRECTION — B0b's gamma_F traverses its last two edges backwards, and a forward-only solve
misses the target by O(1) radians. Verified: |angle(hol) - target| < 1e-15 on both carriers.
Legs R5.2 additionally run the resonant point f=2.0,c=1.1 and the order-4 point f=pi,c=3pi/2.

CARRIERS. K1 from S1_CARRIER_K1_V001.md:16-25. B0b: 3x3 ring torus, gamma_F = one face boundary
(length 4, last two edges traversed backwards), gamma_C = the row j=0 (length 3). Class multisets
re-derived from my own incidence and checked against S4:575 — K1 {11:1,10:2,01:2}, B0b
{00:4,01:1,10:2,11:2}. Both match.

ISOLATION. Every same-pi state family is generated by rlib.same_pi_states, which holds the four
class sums EXACTLY and randomises the within-class split and the phases. EVERY REPORTED ARM IS
DIFFED: min_{i<j} ||s_i - s_j|| is printed beside every spread. max|pi_i - pi_0| is printed too
and is <= 3.4e-16 everywhere. Where an arm pair could be byte-identical it is checked, not assumed.

PRECISION. Double precision is the default. T^L = M_gamma is settled in exact Gaussian-rational
Fraction arithmetic (R1.2) with zero rounding. The class-indicator identity diag(M_gamma)_v =
W^{1[v in gamma]} is exact (0.00e+00). Every other precision-sensitive figure has at least six
orders of magnitude between the two sides of its threshold; there is no borderline case.

COULD-NOT-HAVE-FAILED, DECLARED IN ADVANCE. Leg R2.1 and every circuit-convention row in this
lane are IDENTITIES. They are reported as identities and scored as ZERO EVIDENCE in both
directions. Leg R4's steps 3 and 4 are arithmetic and need no numerics; only steps 1 and 2 are
tested, by a statistic that prints O(1) when the claim is false.

SELF-DEFECTS, RECORDED IN THE SEALED CODE RATHER THAN PATCHED OUT. (i) My first uniqueness search
was a numerical-gradient descent that returned "0 of 24 converged" — a null about the search, not
the variety — and an Adam retry that diverged to loss ~9e+16. Caught only by a positive control
run first. Abandoned for an algebraic settlement. (ii) My first Fourier test asserted a support
claim about the whole matrix that is true only of its diagonal, and printed 6.13 against its own
sentence. Both are documented in the header of r4_uniqueness.py.
