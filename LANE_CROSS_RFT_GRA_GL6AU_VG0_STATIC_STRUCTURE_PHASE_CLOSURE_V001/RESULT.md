# GL6AU result

The phase problem has been reduced to one measured static exponent rather
than an unspecified thermodynamic assumption.  In a translation-stable
finite ground component of the pure `v/g=0` model,

```text
Delta_C(L)
 <= 6J sin^2(pi/L)/S_T(2pi/L).
```

Thus `S_T(2pi/L)>=sL^-alpha` with any `alpha<2` proves finite-component gap
closure.  The Coulomb/linear-mode law `alpha=1` yields `O(J/L)`.  Equivalently,
one of the real first-character quadratures then has variance at least
`(s/2)L^2`; full `L^3` variance is unnecessary.

The exact PF ground-state transform rewrites this as a Dirichlet quotient for
the reversible stationary law `pi_C=psi_C^2`.  This locates the remaining
mathematical phase problem precisely: control the low-character variance of
that nonuniform law.

The sealed algebra does not itself prove this static lower bound.  The exact
RK state belongs to `v/g=1`; at zero potential the configuration-dependent
degree term prevents a direct RK/Markov comparison.  A one-port large twist
has trivial translation character on the even cubic half-filled tori.
Inter-sector flux towers do not prove a local GNS excitation, and rigorous
continuous-link Wilson/Villain Coulomb theorems have no exact microscopic map
to this spin-`1/2` projected ring model.

At the exactly crosswalked `v/g=0` point, published QMC and its fitted lattice
Gaussian theory support `S_T(q)~|q|` with nonzero coefficient.  This is strong
scientific evidence for `ICE0-STATIC` and hence for `O(J/L)` finite-size
closure, but it is not a mathematical lower bound.  Infinite-volume GNS
gaplessness additionally requires `ICE0-GNS-BRIDGE`.

No physical momentum, pole, cone, stress/Ricci law, gravity, or `G` is
derived.
